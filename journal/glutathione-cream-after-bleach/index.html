Can you use glutathione cream after face bleach?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, but not the same day. After face bleach, rinse well, use a plain fragrance-free moisturiser, avoid sun and wait about 24 to 48 hours, until the skin no longer stings or looks pink, before using the glutathione cream again. Bleach lightens facial hair, not skin.

Yes, you can use glutathione cream after face bleach, but not the same day: wait about 24 to 48 hours, until your skin no longer stings, feels hot or looks pink. On the bleach night, rinse well, use only a plain, fragrance-free moisturiser, and go back to the cream once the skin is calm.

Face bleach is very common in India before weddings, festivals and parlour visits. It is worth knowing what it really does: a face bleach lightens the colour of fine facial hair so it shows less. It does not lighten your skin, fade dark spots or remove tan, and it often leaves the skin more sensitive for a day or two. That is why the order and the wait matter.

2. Why you should wait 24 to 48 hours

Bleach can leave the top layer of skin a little irritated and dry, even when it looks fine in the mirror. Putting any active cream on that skin, including one with glutathione, niacinamide and alpha arbutin, can make it sting more and may leave red or darker patches on Indian skin. Giving the skin a day or two to settle is the gentle choice. If you still feel burning, tightness or see redness after 48 hours, wait longer.

3. The right order on bleach day

  1. Patch test the bleach on the jawline or behind the ear 24 hours before, as most packs say, even if you have used it before.
  2. Bleach on clean, dry, unbroken skin. Follow the time on the pack; do not leave it on longer for "more effect".
  3. Remove it and rinse well with cool or lukewarm water. No soap scrubbing.
  4. Pat dry gently with a soft, clean towel.
  5. Apply a plain, fragrance-free moisturiser or a little pure aloe gel if skin feels warm.
  6. Stay out of strong sun for the rest of the day, and use sunscreen the next morning.
  7. Skip the glutathione cream, scrubs, peels and face packs that night and the next.

4. Restarting the cream

  1. Check your skin after 24 to 48 hours. No stinging when you wash, no redness, no tight patches? You are ready.
  2. Start with a thin layer at night, a pea-sized amount for face and neck.
  3. If it tingles more than usual, wash it off and wait another night or two.
  4. Back to your normal routine after that, with sunscreen every morning.

6. Bleach myths to drop

  • "Bleach makes skin fair." No. It lightens hair colour. Any "glow" is mostly hair showing less and a fresh, just-washed look.
  • "Bleach removes tan." No. Tan fades slowly over weeks with sunscreen and shade; see glutathione cream for sun tan.
  • "Mixing cream into bleach makes it gentler." Never mix anything into bleach, and never mix bleach into your cream jar.
  • "Stinging means it is working." Strong burning means remove it at once and rinse.

8. When to see a dermatologist

Rinse off immediately and see a dermatologist if bleach causes strong burning, blisters, swelling, oozing or a rash, or if you notice darker patches where you bleached. A doctor can also help if facial hair growth is new, thick or comes with irregular periods.

FAQ

Can I apply glutathione cream right after bleach?

It is better not to. Use a plain moisturiser that night and wait about 24 to 48 hours, until the skin feels calm, before using the cream.

Can I apply glutathione cream before bleach?

Not on the same day. Skip the cream the night before and the morning of your bleach, and start on clean, bare skin.

What should I apply after face bleach?

Cool water rinse, then a plain, fragrance-free moisturiser or a little pure aloe gel, and sunscreen the next morning.

Is bleach or glutathione cream better for glowing skin?

They do different jobs. Bleach only lightens facial hair. A glutathione cream with daily sunscreen can help skin look brighter and more even over weeks. Neither changes your natural skin colour.

How often can I bleach my face?

Follow the gap the pack gives and do not bleach often. If your skin reacts each time, stop and choose another method.
