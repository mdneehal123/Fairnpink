Glutathione and niacinamide together: can you use them, and how?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, glutathione and niacinamide can be used together, and many creams contain both. Apply on clean skin, thinnest product first, once or twice a day, and wear sunscreen every morning. You do not need a separate niacinamide serum if your cream already has it.

Yes, glutathione and niacinamide can be used together, and many creams already contain both in one formula. Apply them once or twice a day on clean skin, thinnest product first, and finish with sunscreen every morning.

Neither ingredient is known for clashing with the other, so the real questions are practical ones: in which order to apply separate products, whether you need both as separate products at all, and what else in your routine to keep apart. This guide covers those.

1. What each ingredient does

IngredientWhat it isWhat it is used for in skincare
GlutathioneAn antioxidantUsed in creams made for brighter-looking skin
NiacinamideVitamin B3Helps skin look smoother and more even; supports a comfortable, moisturised feel

Together they suit people who want a brighter, more even look and less dullness, used with sunscreen. Neither changes your natural skin colour. For the third ingredient often paired with them, see niacinamide and alpha arbutin.

2. One product or two?

If you already use a cream that contains both, you do not need a separate niacinamide serum as well. Layering two niacinamide products adds cost and can make sensitive skin flush or sting. One well-made product used every day beats three products used now and then.

If you use separate products, a niacinamide serum and a glutathione cream for example, that is fine too. Follow the order below.

3. How to layer them

  1. Wash with a mild face wash and pat dry.
  2. Apply the thinnest product first: a watery serum, then a lotion, then a cream.
  3. Wait a minute between layers so each one settles.
  4. In the morning, finish with sunscreen. At night, the cream is the last step.

The full order with sunscreen is in glutathione cream with sunscreen.

5. What about vitamin C, retinol and acids?

  • Vitamin C: the old advice that niacinamide and vitamin C must never meet is outdated for most people. If the combination makes your skin flush, use vitamin C in the morning and niacinamide at night. More in alpha arbutin and vitamin C.
  • Retinol: use retinol at night and the glutathione cream in the morning or on non-retinol nights. See glutathione cream with retinol.
  • Strong exfoliating acids and scrubs: do not use them on the same night as several actives; once or twice a week is enough for most skin.

7. Safety tips

  • Patch test any new product on the inner arm or jawline for a day or two.
  • A mild tingle that fades in a minute can happen; lasting burning, itching or redness means stop and wash it off.
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding, under treatment for a skin condition, or considering glutathione tablets or injections? Talk to your doctor or a dermatologist. This guide is only about creams applied to the skin.

FAQ

Can I use glutathione and niacinamide together every day?

Yes, most people can use them once or twice a day. Start once a day at night if your skin is sensitive.

Which should I apply first?

Whichever product is thinner goes first. Usually that is the serum, then the cream.

Do I need a niacinamide serum if my cream has niacinamide?

No. It is usually not needed, and two niacinamide products can irritate sensitive skin.

Will using both make me fair?

No. These ingredients can help skin look brighter and more even over weeks; they do not change your natural skin colour, and no honest cream promises that.

How long should I use them before judging?

Give it at least several weeks of daily use with sunscreen. Skin changes slowly and results differ from person to person.
