At what age can you use glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Adults of any age can use a cosmetic glutathione cream if their skin tolerates it. Most teenagers do not need one; from about 16, it can be used gently for a specific concern like tan, with a patch test and sunscreen. Children should not use it.

Adults of any age can use a cosmetic glutathione cream if their skin tolerates it. For teenagers, there is no strict legal age, but most do not need one: a gentle face wash, moisturiser and sunscreen are enough, and a brightening cream makes sense only from around 16 for a specific concern such as tan or pimple marks, used gently and with a parent's knowledge. Children should not use it.

Age matters less than your skin type, your concern and how carefully you use it. Here is what changes from your teens to your 50s, and what to watch out for at each stage.

2. Teenagers: what parents should know

Many teenagers ask for a glutathione cream because of social media or pressure to look "fair". That pressure is worth talking about honestly: no cream changes the skin colour you were born with, and a teenager does not need one to look healthy. If a teen has a real concern, such as tan from sports or flat dark marks after pimples, a gentle cream can be used:

  1. Patch test on the inner arm for 24 hours, then a small area of the jawline for two nights.
  2. Start every other night, a small pea-sized amount on clean, dry skin.
  3. Sunscreen every morning, SPF 30 or more, especially before school sports or outdoor coaching.
  4. Do not add other actives like acid serums, retinol or scrubs at the same time.
  5. Stop and ask a doctor if there is burning, rash or itching, or if acne gets worse.

Painful or frequent acne in teenagers needs a dermatologist, not a brightening cream. For marks left behind, read glutathione cream for pimple marks.

3. In your 20s and 30s

This is when most people start, often for tan from commuting, dullness after late nights, or dark spots from old pimples. A pea-sized amount once or twice a day on clean skin, with sunscreen every morning, is enough. Do not stack many brightening products together; one good cream used steadily for 8 to 12 weeks is better than switching every two weeks. Read how long glutathione cream takes to work for realistic timelines.

4. In your 40s, 50s and later

Skin usually gets drier, and sun spots and uneven tone show more. A glutathione cream can still help skin look brighter and more even over time. Apply it on slightly damp skin, add a plain moisturiser on top if your face feels tight, and keep up sunscreen. Any new, growing, bleeding or changing dark spot should be checked by a doctor before you put a cosmetic cream on it.

5. Who should not use it, at any age

  • Pregnant or breastfeeding women: ask your doctor before using any new active cream.
  • People with a perfume allergy: the cream contains fragrance.
  • Anyone with broken, sunburnt or freshly waxed skin on that area, until it settles. See face cream after facial, threading or waxing.
  • Anyone under treatment for a skin condition like melasma, eczema or severe acne: ask your dermatologist first.

Glutathione tablets and injections are a different matter. They are not cosmetics, and their use, especially by young people, should only be discussed with a doctor.

FAQ

Can a 16-year-old use glutathione cream?

Only if there is a real concern like tan or pimple marks, after a patch test, every other night at first, with sunscreen daily and a parent's knowledge. Most 16-year-olds do not need one.

Can a 14-year-old use glutathione cream?

It is better not to. At that age a gentle face wash, moisturiser and sunscreen are enough. See a dermatologist for acne or skin worries.

Is glutathione cream safe after 40?

Yes, for most people, with a patch test and daily sunscreen. Drier skin may need a plain moisturiser on top.

Does glutathione cream work better at a younger age?

No. It works slowly at every age. Daily sunscreen and steady use matter far more than age.

Will starting young make my skin fair?

No. No cream changes the skin colour you were born with. A cosmetic cream can only help skin look brighter and more even over time.
