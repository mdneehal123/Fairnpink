Best night cream for glowing skin: how to choose one in India

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

The best night cream for glowing skin has proven ingredients such as niacinamide, alpha arbutin or glutathione, a texture that suits your skin, and a price you can keep paying; use it every night for 4 to 8 weeks with sunscreen each morning.

The best night cream for glowing skin is one with proven ingredients such as niacinamide, alpha arbutin or an antioxidant like glutathione, a texture that suits your skin, and a price you can keep paying every month. A cream only works if you use it every night for several weeks.

1. Check the ingredients, not the front of the box

  • Niacinamide (vitamin B3): helps skin look smoother and more even, and suits most skin types.
  • Alpha arbutin: used to help reduce the look of dark spots, gradually.
  • Glutathione: an antioxidant used in creams for a radiant-looking complexion.
  • Moisturisers such as glycerin: keep skin soft overnight.

Avoid any cream that hides its ingredient list or promises a dramatic change in days. Never use creams with steroids, mercury or hydroquinone unless a doctor prescribes them.

2. Match the texture to your skin

  • Oily skin: a light cream, thin layer.
  • Dry skin: a richer cream, applied on slightly damp skin.
  • Sensitive skin: fewer ingredients, and always a patch test first.

3. Compare price per gram

A cheap jar can be tiny, and a big jar can sit unused. Divide the price by the grams, and think about how long one jar lasts at a pea-sized amount a night.

4. Buy from a seller you can contact

Look for a named company, an address and a working phone or WhatsApp number on the pack or website.

A simple night routine

  1. Wash your face with a mild cleanser and pat it dry.
  2. Apply a pea-sized amount of night cream to face and neck.
  3. Every morning, use sunscreen. Without it, the sun undoes what the cream does at night.
