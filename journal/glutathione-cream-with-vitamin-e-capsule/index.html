Can you use glutathione cream with a vitamin E capsule?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, but do not squeeze the capsule into the jar: apply the glutathione cream first, then press one or two drops of vitamin E oil only on very dry areas at night. Skip the oil on oily or acne-prone faces, and wear sunscreen every morning.

Yes, you can use a vitamin E capsule with glutathione cream, but not by squeezing it into the jar: apply the cream first, then press one or two drops of the capsule oil only on very dry areas. Vitamin E capsule oil is thick and greasy, so it suits dry winter skin, lips and cuticles better than oily or acne-prone faces.

Pricking a vitamin E capsule into a face cream is one of the most shared skincare hacks in India, and the capsules are cheap and easy to buy at any chemist. Here is how to use the oil sensibly with a glutathione cream, and when to leave it out.

1. What is inside a vitamin E capsule

A vitamin E capsule is a soft gelatin shell filled with an oily liquid. That liquid is vitamin E (a form of tocopherol) dissolved in a carrier oil. On the skin, it behaves like a rich, heavy oil: it sits on the surface, softens dry patches and slows water loss. It is not a brightening ingredient, and it does not lighten tan or spots by itself.

2. Why you should not mix it into the jar

  • It changes the cream. A face cream is made to spread and absorb in a certain way. Adding oil makes it greasy and uneven.
  • It can spoil the jar. Opening capsules over the jar and stirring with fingers brings in air, water and germs.
  • You cannot control the amount. Some scoops get a lot of oil, others none.

If you want to combine them, mix one drop with your pea-sized amount of cream in your palm, just before applying, on dry nights only. Layering is usually better.

3. The right order

  1. Wash with a gentle face wash and pat dry, leaving skin a little damp.
  2. Apply a pea-sized amount of the glutathione cream over the face and spread evenly.
  3. Wait 3 to 5 minutes so the cream settles.
  4. Prick one capsule with a clean pin and press one or two drops only on very dry spots: sides of the nose, chin, cheeks in winter. Do not rub hard.
  5. Use the rest on lips, cuticles or heels. Throw away an opened capsule the same day.
  6. Every morning: sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher. Read glutathione cream with sunscreen.

Oil goes last because it sits on top. Putting it first leaves a slippery film that stops the cream spreading well. The same thin-to-thick rule is explained in glutathione cream with aloe vera gel.

5. Vitamin E around the eyes and lips

  • Under the eyes: a tiny dab on the bone below the eye can soften dry skin in winter. Keep it out of the eyes and stop if it causes milia (tiny white bumps). For dark circles, see glutathione cream for dark circles.
  • Lips: capsule oil works well as an overnight lip treatment. Keep the glutathione cream off the lips.

6. Mistakes to avoid

  • Using a whole capsule on the face every night.
  • Adding lemon, haldi or toothpaste to the oil.
  • Wearing the oil under makeup or sunscreen in the day; it can slide and pill. See glutathione cream before makeup.
  • Swallowing capsules for skin without asking a doctor. Vitamin E tablets are a supplement, and the dose question is for your doctor, not a skincare guide.
  • Expecting the oil to fade spots. It makes skin feel softer; spots and tan need daily sunscreen and time.

FAQ

Can I mix a vitamin E capsule in my glutathione cream?

Not in the jar. A drop mixed with your daily amount in your palm is fine on dry nights, but layering the oil on top after the cream usually works better.

Should I apply vitamin E before or after glutathione cream?

After. Apply the cream first, wait a few minutes, then press a drop or two of the oil on dry areas only.

Can I use a vitamin E capsule on my face daily?

Dry skin may use a drop or two most nights in winter. Oily and acne-prone skin should skip it on the face.

Does vitamin E with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes the skin colour you were born with. Vitamin E oil can make dry skin feel softer and look less dull; the cream helps skin look brighter and more even over weeks with sunscreen.

Can I use vitamin E oil in the morning?

It is better at night. In the day, the oil feels greasy in Indian heat and can make sunscreen and makeup slide.
