Can you use raw milk with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, as a separate step: dab cold milk on clean skin with cotton, rinse after 5 to 10 minutes, pat dry, then apply the glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Never mix milk into the jar, skip it with a milk allergy, and use it two or three times a week at most.

Yes, you can use raw milk with glutathione cream, but as two separate steps: dab cold milk on clean skin with cotton, rinse it off after 5 to 10 minutes, pat dry, then apply the cream. Do not mix milk into the jar, and do not leave it on overnight.

Kachcha doodh on cotton is one of the oldest Indian home cleansing habits, often passed down from mothers and grandmothers. Milk naturally contains a little lactic acid and some fat, so it can feel soft and soothing. It is a gentle ritual, not a spot or tan fix. Here is how to fit it around your cream sensibly.

2. The right order

  1. Wash with a gentle face wash, especially if you wore sunscreen or makeup, and pat dry.
  2. Pour 2 to 3 teaspoons of cold milk into a clean bowl. Most people mean unboiled packet milk straight from the fridge. Never dip into the whole packet.
  3. Dab it on the face and neck with a fresh cotton pad, avoiding the eyes and lips. Do not rub hard.
  4. Leave it for 5 to 10 minutes, then rinse well with plain water so no milk is left on the skin, and pat dry.
  5. Apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream on the face and neck.
  6. Every morning: sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, as the last step. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

Two or three evenings a week is plenty. Throw away the milk left in the bowl. For other home steps before the cream, see rose water with glutathione cream and glutathione cream with ubtan, which covers milk-based besan packs.

3. Why not mix milk into the cream

  • It can spoil the jar. Milk is food for germs. Even a few drops can make the cream smell sour within days.
  • It changes the texture. The cream turns runny and patchy.
  • It adds nothing useful. The cream works as it is made; a kitchen ingredient only makes it less predictable.

5. Milk with haldi, besan or honey

Milk is often mixed with besan, haldi or honey into a pack. The same rule applies: use the pack on clean skin, rinse it off before it dries hard, pat dry, then apply the cream. Keep haldi to a small pinch, as it can stain and sometimes irritates. Do not add lemon to milk packs; lemon can sting and react badly with sun.

6. Mistakes to avoid

  • Leaving milk on overnight. It dries, smells sour and can clog pores.
  • Using milk that has been sitting out. Use cold, fresh milk and a clean bowl each time.
  • Skipping sunscreen because you used a home remedy. Tan comes back without daily sun protection. See glutathione cream for sun tan.
  • Expecting change in a few days. Dark spots and tan fade slowly. See how to reduce dark spots on face.

FAQ

Should I apply raw milk before or after glutathione cream?

Before, and rinse it off. Use it as a 5 to 10 minute wash-off step, pat dry, then apply the cream.

Can I mix milk into glutathione cream?

No. Milk spoils quickly and can make the jar smell and go off. Keep them as separate steps.

Is boiled or raw milk better for the face?

Most home tips use cold, unboiled packet milk. Either way, use it fresh, keep it off broken skin and rinse it off well.

Can I use raw milk on my face every day?

Two or three times a week is enough for most people. Daily use can leave oily skin greasy.

Does raw milk with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Used with sunscreen, the cream can help skin look brighter and tan look lighter over weeks.
