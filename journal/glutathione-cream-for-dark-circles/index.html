Can glutathione cream help dark circles under the eyes?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

A glutathione cream may help brown, pigmented dark circles look a little lighter over 8 to 12 weeks with daily sunscreen, using a rice-grain amount on the under-eye bone. It does not change hollows, shadows, puffiness or blue-purple circles.

A glutathione cream can help some dark circles look a little lighter over weeks, mainly the brownish kind caused by pigmentation, if you apply it carefully on the under-eye bone and wear sunscreen every day. It does little for dark circles caused by hollows, shadows, thin skin or puffiness, which are very common and often run in families.

So the honest first step is to work out what kind of dark circles you have. This guide shows you how, how to use a face cream near the eyes without irritation, and what else actually helps.

1. What kind of dark circles do you have?

A simple check: gently stretch the skin under your eye in daylight. If the colour fades a lot, it is mostly vascular or shadow. If the brown stays, pigment is part of it. On Indian skin, brown pigmented circles are common, which is why people ask about creams with glutathione, niacinamide and alpha arbutin.

2. How to apply a face cream near the eyes

  1. Patch test first. Try a little on your inner arm for 24 hours, then on a small spot near the outer cheek for two nights. The under-eye skin is thinner and reacts more easily.
  2. Use a tiny amount. A rice-grain amount is enough for both eyes.
  3. Stay on the bone. Dab it along the under-eye bone, about a finger-width below the lash line. Do not put it on the eyelid, the lash line or inside the inner corner.
  4. Use your ring finger and tap, do not rub. Rubbing and pulling irritate the area and can make it look darker.
  5. Night is easiest. If you use it in the morning, wait a few minutes and then apply sunscreen right up to the under-eye area.
  6. Stop if it stings or waters your eyes. Rinse with cool water and use only a plain, fragrance-free moisturiser there.

The cream contains fragrance, so if your eyes or eyelids react easily to perfumed products, keep it off the under-eye area completely. Read glutathione cream for sensitive skin for a slow-start plan.

3. Sunscreen matters more than the cream

Brown circles get darker with sun, and the under-eye area is often missed with sunscreen. Apply SPF 30 or more gently up to the bone below the eyes every morning, and wear sunglasses outdoors. Without daily sun protection, no cream will make pigmented circles look lighter for long. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

4. Habits that make dark circles look worse

  • Rubbing your eyes, especially with allergies, dust or after long screen time.
  • Late nights and little sleep, which make vascular circles and puffiness more visible.
  • Salty dinners and lying flat, which can make morning puffiness worse; a slightly raised pillow helps some people.
  • Harsh makeup removal, such as scrubbing off kajal and eyeliner. Use a gentle remover on cotton and press, do not drag.
  • Home remedies like lemon or raw potato rubbed hard, which can irritate thin under-eye skin.

6. When to see a doctor

See a doctor or dermatologist if dark circles appear suddenly, only on one side, or come with swelling, itching, flaking or watery eyes, which may be allergy or eczema. Constant tiredness with dark circles can be worth a general health check, for example for low iron. A dermatologist can also tell you if your circles are mainly hollows, which creams cannot change.

FAQ

Can I apply glutathione cream under my eyes?

You can apply a rice-grain amount on the under-eye bone after a patch test, keeping it off the eyelids and lash line. Stop if it stings or makes your eyes water.

How long does it take for dark circles to look lighter?

For brown, pigmented circles, give it 8 to 12 weeks of daily use with sunscreen. Hollows, shadows and blue-purple circles usually do not change with a cream.

Is an eye cream better than a face cream for dark circles?

An eye cream is made and tested for the eye area, which suits sensitive eyes better. A gentle face cream can work on the bone below the eye if your skin tolerates it.

Can glutathione cream remove dark circles permanently?

No. No cream removes dark circles permanently. Sun, sleep, allergies and genetics keep playing a part, so care has to be ongoing.

Do home remedies like potato or cold tea bags help?

A cool compress or chilled tea bags can make puffiness look less for a short time. Rubbing potato or lemon does not reliably help and can irritate the skin.
