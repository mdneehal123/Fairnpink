How to use gua sha with glutathione cream

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Glide the gua sha over a few drops of face oil or a thick moisturiser with light strokes, wipe off the extra, then apply a thin layer of glutathione cream on clean skin. Gua sha may reduce puffiness for a while; it does not brighten skin or remove spots.

To use gua sha with glutathione cream, glide the stone over a slippery layer such as a few drops of face oil or a thick moisturiser, then rinse or wipe off the excess and apply a thin layer of the glutathione cream on clean skin afterwards. Do not scrape over a thin cream that is still soaking in; the stone drags, and you rub most of the cream off.

Gua sha is a flat stone tool, often jade or rose quartz, used with light strokes along the face. Many people like it as a calming, few-minute ritual and find their face looks less puffy in the morning for a while. It does not brighten skin, remove tan or dark spots, or change your natural skin colour, and any "sculpted" look is short-lived.

2. The right order with glutathione cream

  1. Wash your face and hands, and make sure the stone is clean.
  2. Apply a slip layer: two or three drops of a plain face oil, or a thicker moisturiser. The stone should glide, never tug.
  3. Do 3 to 5 minutes of light strokes (see the steps below).
  4. Wipe off the extra oil with a soft tissue, or rinse with lukewarm water if your skin is oily.
  5. Apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream in a thin layer over the face and neck.
  6. Morning: if you do gua sha in the morning, finish with sunscreen. At night, leave the cream on.

Can you use the cream itself as the slip layer? Only if it is thick enough for the stone to glide, and you use a little more than usual. If the stone drags or the cream pills, switch to oil first and cream after. For the cream steps, see how to use the cream.

3. Simple gua sha strokes (3 to 5 minutes)

  1. Neck: from just under the jaw down towards the collarbone, 3 to 5 strokes each side, very light.
  2. Jawline: from the chin out towards the ear, the curved edge of the stone along the jaw.
  3. Cheeks: from beside the nose out towards the ear, holding the stone almost flat.
  4. Under the eyes: only the lightest touch, from the inner corner outwards, or skip this area.
  5. Forehead: from the eyebrows up towards the hairline.

Keep the pressure light: you should not see red streaks or feel pain. Hard scraping can leave redness and small broken capillaries, and on Indian skin any irritation can leave darker marks behind.

Choosing an oil? See jojoba oil and almond oil with glutathione cream. In hot, humid months, skip heavy oils.

5. Cool stone, clean stone

  • Keep it cool, not frozen: a few minutes in the fridge feels nice on a warm Indian night. A frozen stone straight from the freezer can be too harsh; see ice cube with glutathione cream for the same idea.
  • Wash after every use with mild soap and water, dry it and store it in its pouch.
  • Do not share it, and stop using it if it chips; a sharp edge can scratch skin.

6. Who should skip gua sha

  • Active, swollen or painful pimples: the stone can spread bacteria and worsen redness. See acne-prone skin.
  • Sunburn, rashes, cuts, or skin that was just waxed, threaded or bleached; wait a day or two. See face cream after facial, threading and waxing.
  • Visible broken veins, rosacea or very easily flushed skin.
  • After fillers, Botox or other clinic procedures: ask the doctor who did it first.

8. When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if gua sha leaves bruises or redness that lasts, if you get itching or a rash after a new oil or cream, or if you have patches of darkness that keep spreading. Patch test any new product on your inner arm or jawline first.

FAQ

Should I use gua sha before or after glutathione cream?

Before. Do gua sha over a slippery layer of oil or moisturiser, wipe off the extra, then apply the cream on clean skin.

Can I use glutathione cream as a gua sha oil?

Only if it lets the stone glide without dragging. Most creams soak in too fast, so an oil or thick moisturiser works better.

How often should I do gua sha?

A few minutes, three to five times a week, is plenty. Sensitive skin should start once or twice a week.

Can gua sha remove dark circles?

It may make under-eyes look less puffy for a while, but it does not remove dark circles. See glutathione cream for dark circles.

Does gua sha with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. With sunscreen, the cream can help tan and marks look lighter over weeks.
