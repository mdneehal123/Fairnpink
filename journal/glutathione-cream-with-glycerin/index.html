Can you use glycerin with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes. Dilute 2 to 3 drops of plain glycerin with water or rose water, pat it on slightly damp skin, wait a minute, then apply the glutathione cream on top, with sunscreen every morning. It suits dry winter skin; oily skin and humid weather usually do not need it.

Yes, you can use glycerin with glutathione cream: dilute a few drops of plain glycerin with water or rose water, pat it on slightly damp skin, wait a minute, then apply the cream on top. Use it mostly at night in dry winter weather, never mix it into the cream jar, and wear sunscreen every morning.

Glycerin is one of the oldest winter tricks in Indian homes. A small bottle from the chemist costs very little, and many families mix it with gulab jal for cracked lips, dry hands and tight cheeks. It is a humectant: it pulls water into the top layer of the skin. A cream then helps hold that water in. Used the right way, the two make a simple, low-cost winter routine.

2. The right order

  1. Wash with a gentle face wash and lukewarm (not hot) water.
  2. Leave the skin slightly damp. Pat with a towel, do not rub it fully dry.
  3. Dilute the glycerin. Mix 2 to 3 drops of glycerin with about a teaspoon of water or plain rose water in your palm. Neat glycerin feels sticky and can feel tight in dry air.
  4. Pat it on the face and neck, avoiding the eyes. Wait about a minute until it is no longer wet.
  5. Apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream on top to hold the moisture in.
  6. Every morning: sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, as the last step, even in winter. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

If you already use a hydrating serum, you do not need glycerin as well; one water-drawing step is enough. See glutathione cream with hyaluronic acid serum. For using gulab jal on its own, see rose water with glutathione cream.

4. Why not mix glycerin into the jar

  • It changes the cream. Extra glycerin and water make the texture sticky and runny, and the cream may not spread or sink in the same way.
  • It can spoil the jar. Adding water or rose water to a cream brings in germs it was not made to handle.
  • You lose control. Kept separate, you can use glycerin only on dry nights or dry areas, and stop easily if it does not suit you.

5. Mistakes to avoid

  • Using it neat. Pure glycerin feels heavy and sticky, and in very dry air it can leave skin feeling tighter, not softer.
  • Using too much. Two to three drops are enough for the whole face. More just attracts dust and pollution.
  • Skipping the cream on top. Glycerin works best when something seals it in, especially in heater-dried rooms.
  • Adding lemon. The old glycerin-lemon-rose water mix can sting and irritate, especially before going out in the sun.
  • Expecting glycerin to fade tan or spots. It softens skin; tan and dark spots look lighter slowly with daily sunscreen and steady care. See how to reduce dark spots on face.

6. Buying glycerin

Choose plain glycerin, ideally labelled for cosmetic or pharmaceutical use, with no added perfume or colour. Keep the cap closed and the bottle out of the bathroom heat. Mix it fresh in your palm each time; do not store a ready-made mix of glycerin and water for weeks.

FAQ

Should I apply glycerin before or after glutathione cream?

Before. Pat diluted glycerin on damp skin, wait a minute, then apply the cream to hold the moisture in.

Can I mix glycerin into my glutathione cream?

No. Keep them separate so the cream keeps its texture and the jar stays clean.

Can I use glycerin and rose water with glutathione cream?

Yes. A few drops of glycerin mixed with plain rose water in your palm is a good first step on dry winter nights, followed by the cream.

Can I use glycerin on my face every day?

Diluted, most dry and normal skin can use it daily in winter. Oily skin and humid weather usually do not need it.

Does glycerin with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Glycerin keeps skin softer, and the cream can help skin look brighter and more even over weeks with sunscreen.
