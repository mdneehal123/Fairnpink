Can you use cucumber (kheera) with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, as a separate step: dab fresh cucumber juice or lay thin slices on clean skin for about 10 minutes, rinse with plain water, pat dry, then apply the glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Never mix cucumber juice into the jar.

Yes, you can use cucumber with glutathione cream, as two separate steps: dab fresh cucumber juice or lay thin slices on clean skin for about 10 minutes, rinse with plain water, pat dry, then apply a thin layer of the cream. Never mix cucumber juice into the jar, and wear sunscreen every morning.

Kheera slices on tired eyes and grated cucumber on a hot face are some of the oldest Indian home habits, especially in summer and after a long, sunny commute. Cucumber is one of the gentlest kitchen items for the face. It can make skin feel cool and fresh, but its effect is short, and it does not remove tan or change skin colour.

2. The right order

  1. Patch test a little cucumber juice on your inner arm or jawline for 15 minutes first. Cucumber allergy is uncommon but possible.
  2. Wash your face with a mild face wash and pat it dry.
  3. Grate a small piece of washed cucumber and squeeze the juice through a clean cloth, or cut thin slices and chill them for a few minutes.
  4. Dab the juice on with cotton or lay the slices on your face and closed eyes. Leave for about 10 minutes.
  5. Rinse with plain water and pat dry. Wait until the skin is fully dry.
  6. Apply a thin layer of the glutathione cream on the face, away from the eyes and lips.
  7. Sunscreen every morning, SPF 30 or higher.

Make the juice fresh each time and throw away what is left. For the daily cream steps, read how to use the cream.

3. Cucumber for puffy eyes and dark circles

Chilled cucumber slices on closed eyes for 10 minutes are a pleasant way to make tired, puffy eyes feel fresher. Most of that comes from the cold, so a cool, damp cloth or a chilled spoon does much the same. Cucumber does not lighten dark circles, which come from many things, including genes, thin under-eye skin, rubbing, allergies and sleep. Keep the juice out of the eyes themselves, and read glutathione cream for dark circles for an honest view of what a cream can do there.

5. Mixes and habits to skip

  • Cucumber with lemon: lemon is acidic and often stings and dries the face. See lemon with glutathione cream.
  • Cucumber juice mixed into the cream jar: never. Water and food juice let germs grow and spoil the cream.
  • A home-made cucumber toner kept for a week: without preservatives it spoils within a day or so, even in the fridge.
  • Leaving juice on overnight under the cream: there is no benefit, and the cream spreads better on dry skin.
  • Rubbing cucumber over the cream: it only wipes the cream away.

6. Who should take care

  • Anyone allergic to cucumber or melons when eating them. Skip it on the face too.
  • Very dry winter skin. Cucumber is mostly water and can leave skin feeling tighter once it dries. Follow with the cream or a moisturiser; see glutathione cream for dry skin.
  • Sensitive skin that reacts to almost everything; patch test, and see glutathione cream for sensitive skin.
  • Open cuts, picked pimples, or skin right after threading, waxing or bleach. Give it a day or two.

7. Cucumber for tan and oily skin: an honest view

Cucumber feels wonderful after a sunny day, and that comfort is real. It does not remove tan or control oil for long. What helps tan look lighter over weeks is shade, covering up, sunscreen every morning and steady care; read glutathione cream for sun tan. For shine in humid weather, a gentle face wash and a thin cream matter more; see glutathione cream for oily skin.

9. When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if your skin itches, swells or breaks into a rash after cucumber, if swelling around the eyes stays for days, or if dark patches on the cheeks keep spreading (this can be melasma, which needs a doctor).

FAQ

Can I apply glutathione cream after cucumber?

Yes. Rinse the cucumber off, pat your skin fully dry, and apply a thin layer of the cream.

Can I mix cucumber juice into my glutathione cream?

No. Food juice in the jar lets germs grow and changes the cream. Use cucumber only as a separate rinse-off step.

How often can I use cucumber on my face?

Most skin is fine with it several times a week because it is mild. Patch test first and stop if it itches.

Can I use cucumber in the morning?

Yes. Rinse it off, pat dry, then apply the cream and sunscreen as usual.

Does cucumber with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Used with sunscreen, the cream can help tan and marks look lighter over weeks.
