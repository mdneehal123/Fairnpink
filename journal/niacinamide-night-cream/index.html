Niacinamide night cream: benefits and how to use it

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

A niacinamide night cream helps skin look smoother and more even and supports its moisture barrier overnight. It suits most skin types, can be used with vitamin C, and needs about 8 weeks with daily sunscreen.

A niacinamide night cream helps skin look smoother and more even and supports its moisture barrier while you sleep. It suits most skin types, including oily and sensitive skin, and works best used every night for several weeks with sunscreen in the morning.

What niacinamide is

Niacinamide is a form of vitamin B3. It is one of the most widely used and best-tolerated skincare ingredients.

Benefits people use it for

  • More even-looking skin tone
  • Smoother-looking texture
  • Support for the skin's own moisture barrier
  • Less visible shine on oily skin

How to use it at night

  1. Cleanse and pat your face dry.
  2. Apply a pea-sized amount to face and neck.
  3. Leave it on overnight. Use sunscreen the next morning.

Night or day?

Niacinamide works at any time of day, so a niacinamide cream does not have to be night-only. Night is simply when most people are most consistent, and there is no sweat or makeup on top. If your cream feels comfortable under sunscreen, morning use is fine too; Fair N Pink is used morning and night.

Can you use it with vitamin C or retinol?

Yes. The old idea that niacinamide and vitamin C cancel each other out does not hold up for modern products; if your skin flushes with both, use vitamin C in the morning and niacinamide at night. With retinol, niacinamide is often used to keep skin comfortable; start the retinol slowly, on alternate nights.

How long it takes

Skin often feels smoother within a couple of weeks. Changes in how even the tone looks take longer, usually eight weeks or more with daily sunscreen.

Good partners

Niacinamide works well with alpha arbutin for the look of dark spots, and with glutathione in creams for a brighter-looking complexion. Fair N Pink Advance Radiance Cream has all three in one niacinamide and alpha arbutin cream that also works as a glutathione night cream.

Side effects

Most people have none. A few notice mild tingling or redness in the first days. Patch test first.

FAQ

Is niacinamide good in a night cream?

Yes. It is gentle, suits most skin types and supports the skin barrier overnight. It can also be used in the morning.

Can I use a niacinamide night cream every night?

Yes, once a patch test shows your skin is comfortable. Daily use is how it is meant to be used.

Does niacinamide make skin fair?

No. It can help skin look more even and dark marks look lighter over weeks, with sunscreen. It does not change your natural skin colour.

Why does niacinamide make my face feel warm?

Some people feel brief warmth or tingling in the first days. If it settles, carry on; if redness or itching lasts, stop and see a doctor.
