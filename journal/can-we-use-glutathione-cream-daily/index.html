Can we use glutathione cream daily? Morning or night?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Yes. A glutathione face cream is a cosmetic made for daily use, and regular use is what gives it a chance to work. Most people apply it twice a day.

Morning or night?

Both, if your skin is comfortable with it. If you want to use it only once a day, choose night. Your skin is clean, the cream stays on for hours and there is no sun, sweat or makeup in the way.

If you use it in the morning

Always put sunscreen on top. A brightening cream without sunscreen is undone by the sun the same day.

A simple daily routine

  1. Morning: cleanser, cream, sunscreen.
  2. Night: cleanser, cream.

When to cut back

If your skin feels tight, warm or looks red, drop to once a day at night for a week. If that does not settle it, stop and read side effects and safety.

Can you use it for months?

A cosmetic cream can be used as part of your routine for as long as it suits your skin. If you are using any prescription cream, ask your doctor how to combine them.
