Alpha arbutin cream side effects: what to know

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Alpha arbutin is used in creams that help reduce the look of dark spots. It is generally well tolerated in cosmetics, and most people use it without any problem. A small number notice irritation, usually in the first week.

Possible side effects

  • Mild redness or warmth where the cream is applied
  • Itching or dryness, more often on sensitive skin
  • Rarely, an allergic reaction to the cream as a whole

When a cream irritates, the cause is often another ingredient such as fragrance, not the alpha arbutin itself.

How to lower the risk

  1. Patch test on your inner arm for 24 hours.
  2. Start once a day at night, then move to twice a day.
  3. Use sunscreen every morning.
  4. Do not combine it with strong peels or scrubs on the same day.

Who should ask a doctor first

Anyone pregnant or breastfeeding, and anyone under treatment for melasma or another skin condition.

If your skin reacts

Wash it off, stop using it and let your skin rest. If it does not settle in a day or two, see a dermatologist.
