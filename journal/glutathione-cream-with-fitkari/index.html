Can you use fitkari (alum) with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Better not as a face routine step. Fitkari (alum) dries and can sting skin and does not fade dark spots or tan. If you use it on shaving nicks, rinse it off, moisturise, and apply glutathione cream later, once skin has stopped stinging.

It is better not to use fitkari (alum) on your face as part of a glutathione cream routine: alum is a strong astringent that can leave skin dry, tight and stinging, and it does nothing for dark spots or tan. If you already use an alum block after shaving, rinse it off, let skin calm down, moisturise, and apply your glutathione cream later, never on freshly alum-rubbed skin.

Fitkari is the clear crystal block sold in kirana and pooja shops, and many Indian homes use it after shaving, to "tighten" pores, or in home packs for pimples and dark spots. It does tighten the feel of skin for a while, which is why it seems to work. That tight feel is mostly a drying, drawing effect, not a change in pores or skin tone.

1. What fitkari can and cannot do

Claim you may hearWhat is true
"Closes pores"It gives a temporary tight feel; pores do not open and close like doors
"Stops bleeding from shaving nicks"It can help small nicks stop bleeding, which is why barbers use it
"Removes dark spots and tan"No. Alum does not fade marks or tan
"Makes skin fair"No. Nothing you rub on skin changes natural skin colour
"Clears pimples"No. It can dry the surface but also irritate; see a dermatologist for painful acne

2. Why alum and a face cream routine do not mix well

  • Dryness and tightness: alum draws moisture from the surface. Dry, irritated skin often looks duller and can feel itchy.
  • Stinging: on freshly shaved, scrubbed or broken skin, alum stings, and any cream on top can sting more.
  • Hidden irritation: daily rubbing can leave skin red or flaky, and irritation can make dark marks on brown skin look darker over time.
  • Hygiene: one wet block passed from face to face or left damp on the sink is not clean.

3. If you use fitkari after shaving: the safer order

  1. Shave with a sharp, clean blade along the direction of hair growth.
  2. Rinse with cool water. Only if you have small nicks, touch a wet alum block lightly to those spots, not the whole face.
  3. Rinse again after a minute so no alum stays on skin, and pat dry.
  4. Apply a plain, fragrance-free moisturiser or aloe vera gel; see glutathione cream with aloe vera gel.
  5. Apply your glutathione cream at night, or at least a few hours later, once skin has stopped stinging. If skin still stings, skip the cream that day.
  6. Every morning: broad-spectrum sunscreen of SPF 30 or more.

Full shaving routine: glutathione cream after shaving.

4. Fitkari in home face packs

Recipes that mix fitkari powder with rose water, lemon or besan for spots are best skipped. Lemon and alum together are especially harsh. Never add alum, or any kitchen or pooja ingredient, into your cream jar. If you want a gentle home pack, compare rose water or multani mitti with glutathione cream instead, and read why lemon is a bad idea.

6. Using fitkari on underarms or feet

Some people use alum as a simple deodorant or on sweaty feet. That is a separate use from face care. Do not apply it on freshly shaved or waxed underarms, and stop if it stings or itches.

7. When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist for razor bumps that keep coming back, painful or pus-filled pimples, a rash after using alum, or dark patches that keep spreading. Patch test any new product on the jawline first.

FAQ

Can I apply glutathione cream after fitkari?

Not straight away. Rinse the alum off, moisturise, and apply the cream later once skin has stopped stinging, ideally at night.

Can I mix fitkari powder into my glutathione cream?

No. It can irritate skin and spoil the cream. Keep the jar clean and use only the cream from it.

Does fitkari remove dark spots?

No. Alum does not fade dark spots or tan. Daily sunscreen and a steady routine for weeks help marks look lighter.

Is fitkari good for oily skin?

It dries the surface for a while, but stripped skin can feel worse. A gentle face wash and a light cream are kinder for oily skin.

Does glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. It does not change your natural skin colour. With sunscreen, it can help tan and dark marks look lighter over weeks.
