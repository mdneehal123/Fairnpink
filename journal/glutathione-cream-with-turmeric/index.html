Can you use turmeric (haldi) with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, as a separate step: use a small pinch of haldi in a besan, curd or milk pack, rinse after about 10 minutes, pat dry, then apply the glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Never mix haldi into the jar; it stains and does not change skin colour.

Yes, you can use turmeric with glutathione cream, but as two separate steps: a small pinch of haldi in a wash-off pack on clean skin, rinsed off after about 10 minutes, then the cream on dry skin. Do not mix haldi into the jar, and do not leave it on overnight.

Haldi is part of Indian skin care from the haldi ceremony before weddings to everyday besan-haldi packs, and wedding season is here now. It can feel like a nice ritual and many people enjoy it, but turmeric stains, can irritate some skin and does not change skin colour. Here is how to use it sensibly around your cream.

2. The right order

  1. Wash with a gentle face wash and pat dry.
  2. Make the pack: one or two teaspoons of besan, curd or milk, with only a small pinch of haldi. More haldi means more yellow, not more benefit.
  3. Apply a thin layer away from the eyes, lips and eyebrows. Old clothes and a towel you do not mind staining help.
  4. Rinse after about 10 minutes, before it dries hard and cracks. Use lukewarm water and soft circles, not hard scrubbing.
  5. Remove any yellow tint with a gentle face wash or a cotton pad with a little milk or plain moisturiser, then rinse and pat dry.
  6. Apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream on the face and neck.
  7. Every morning: sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, as the last step. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

Once a week is enough for most people. For the besan pack itself and a full pre-wedding ubtan routine, see glutathione cream with ubtan.

3. Which haldi to use, and what to avoid

FormUse before the cream?
Plain haldi powder from a sealed pack, a small pinchYes, in a wash-off pack
Kasturi manjal (wild turmeric, traditionally used on skin in the South)Yes, same way; it usually stains less
Loose powder of unknown qualityBetter not; it may contain added colour
Raw turmeric root pasteStains strongly; use a very thin layer and rinse well, or skip it
Turmeric with lemon, baking soda or toothpasteNo; these often sting and dry the skin. See lemon with glutathione cream

4. Why not mix haldi into the cream

  • It stains. The cream stays on your face all night and would leave a yellow tint on the skin and the pillow.
  • It can spoil the jar. Powders and wet fingers bring in moisture and germs.
  • It adds nothing useful. The cream works as it is made; haldi belongs in a pack you wash off.

6. Haldi ceremony and wedding days

If you have a haldi ceremony coming up, the paste used there is usually thick and stays on longer. Skip your glutathione cream that night if the skin feels hot, tight or itchy, and use a plain moisturiser instead. Go back to the cream the next night once the skin is calm. Do a haldi pack test on the jaw a week or two before the function, not the day before. For wider planning, see glutathione cream before makeup.

FAQ

Should I apply haldi before or after glutathione cream?

Before, as a wash-off pack. Rinse it after about 10 minutes, pat dry, then apply the cream.

Can I mix turmeric into glutathione cream?

No. It stains the skin and pillow and can spoil the jar. Keep them as separate steps.

How do I remove the yellow tint after a haldi pack?

Wash with a gentle face wash, or wipe with a cotton pad and a little milk or plain moisturiser, then rinse. Using only a small pinch prevents most staining.

How often can I use haldi with glutathione cream?

Once a week is enough for most people. The cream itself can be used as usual.

Does haldi with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Used with sunscreen, the cream can help skin look brighter and tan look lighter over weeks.
