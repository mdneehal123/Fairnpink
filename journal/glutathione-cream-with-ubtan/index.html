Can you use glutathione cream with ubtan?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes. Use ubtan as a gentle 10 to 15 minute wash-off pack on clean skin, rinse it off softly, then apply the glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Keep ubtan to once or twice a week and skip the cream that night if your skin feels warm or red.

Yes, you can use glutathione cream with ubtan: use the ubtan as a gentle wash-off pack on clean skin, rinse it off, pat dry and then apply the cream. Keep ubtan to once or twice a week, rub it off softly instead of scrubbing, and never mix it into the cream jar.

Ubtan, the classic paste of besan (gram flour), haldi and milk, curd or rose water, is part of many Indian homes and almost every wedding season, from pre-wedding glow plans to the haldi ceremony. Here is how to fit it around a glutathione cream without irritating your skin.

1. Ubtan and glutathione cream do different jobs

An ubtan gives a nice fresh look for an evening. It does not fade tan or dark spots on its own. That slower change needs daily sunscreen and weeks of steady care.

2. The right order on an ubtan night

  1. Wash the face with a gentle face wash and pat dry.
  2. Apply the ubtan in an even layer, avoiding the eyes and lips. Keep it slightly moist, not dry and cracking.
  3. Leave it for 10 to 15 minutes.
  4. Wet your fingers and loosen it with soft circles, then rinse with lukewarm water. Do not rub hard like a scrub.
  5. Pat dry and wait a few minutes so any tingling settles.
  6. Apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream. If the skin feels warm or red, skip the cream that night and use a plain moisturiser.
  7. Next morning: sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

3. A simple, gentle ubtan

  • 2 teaspoons besan
  • A small pinch of haldi (more stains the skin yellow for hours)
  • Enough rose water, plain curd or milk to make a smooth paste

Make it fresh each time and throw away what is left. Skip lemon, baking soda, toothpaste and coarse grains: they sting, dry the skin and are a common reason for redness when an active cream goes on afterwards. If you want something even gentler, read glutathione cream with aloe vera gel.

5. Which skin types suit ubtan

  • Oily skin: besan with rose water suits most people; once or twice a week. See glutathione cream for oily skin.
  • Dry skin: use curd or milk instead of rose water, keep it short, and moisturise well after. See glutathione cream for dry skin.
  • Sensitive skin: patch test the ubtan on the jaw first, and do not use it on the same night as the cream for the first few weeks. See glutathione cream for sensitive skin.
  • Active pimples: do not rub ubtan over them.

6. Mistakes to avoid

  • Mixing ubtan powder or haldi into the cream jar.
  • Scrubbing hard to "remove tan".
  • Daily ubtan in the weeks before a wedding.
  • Leaving the pack on until it cracks and pulls the skin.
  • Believing ubtan or any cream will make skin fair. Neither changes your natural skin colour.

FAQ

Should I apply ubtan before or after glutathione cream?

Before, and wash it off. Ubtan is a wash-off pack; the cream goes on clean, dry skin after rinsing.

Can I use ubtan and glutathione cream on the same night?

Yes, if your skin is calm after rinsing. If it feels warm, red or tingly, skip the cream that night.

How often can I use ubtan with glutathione cream?

Once or twice a week is enough. Daily packs and scrubbing can dry and irritate the skin.

Will ubtan with glutathione cream make me fair before my wedding?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Ubtan gives a fresh look for the day, and the cream can help skin look brighter and more even over 8 to 12 weeks with sunscreen.

Can I apply haldi with glutathione cream?

Use only a small pinch in a wash-off ubtan, never mixed into the cream. Too much haldi stains the skin yellow for hours.
