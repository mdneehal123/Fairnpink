Glutathione cream and moisturiser: which first, and do you need both?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Apply the glutathione cream first and a plain moisturiser on top, only where skin still feels dry. Oily, normal and combination skin often need no separate moisturiser; dry skin may need one at night in winter. Sunscreen always goes last in the morning.

Apply the glutathione cream first and the moisturiser second: the cream goes on clean skin so it sits closest to it, and a plain moisturiser on top seals in water if your skin still feels dry. Many people with normal, oily or combination skin do not need a separate moisturiser at all, because a glutathione cream is already a moisturising cream.

The exception is a very thin, watery moisturiser such as a gel or lotion: thin layers usually go before thicker ones. The rule of thumb is thinnest to thickest, and actives close to the skin.

2. The right order, step by step

  1. Wash your face with a mild face wash and pat it dry, leaving it very slightly damp if your skin is dry.
  2. Thin serum or gel first (for example a hyaluronic acid serum or aloe gel), if you use one.
  3. Glutathione cream next: a pea-sized amount spread thinly over the face and neck.
  4. Wait 2 to 3 minutes so the cream settles.
  5. Moisturiser on top, only where skin feels dry: cheeks, around the nose, chin.
  6. Morning only: sunscreen last, after both have set. Sunscreen always goes on top of everything else.

For the full daily routine, read how to use the cream. If you also use a serum, see glutathione cream with hyaluronic acid serum and with aloe vera gel.

3. Why cream first, moisturiser second

A glutathione cream carries the ingredients you are using for a brighter, more even look, so it makes sense to put it directly on clean skin. A moisturiser is mainly there to help skin hold on to water. Put on top, it adds comfort without blocking the cream. If you reverse the order and put a thick moisturiser underneath, the cream sits on a greasy layer and spreads unevenly.

That said, there is no harm if you sometimes use them the other way round. Order matters less than using both gently, every day, with sunscreen.

4. Choosing a moisturiser to pair with it

  • Plain is better: pick one without added fragrance, essential oils or more brightening actives. You already have those in the glutathione cream.
  • Match it to the weather: a light gel or lotion in summer and monsoon, a richer cream in winter.
  • Helpful ingredients: glycerin, hyaluronic acid and ceramides help skin hold moisture. Petroleum jelly or shea butter are heavy sealing layers best kept to very dry patches; see petroleum jelly with glutathione cream.
  • Skip doubling up: a second "brightening" or "glow" cream on top adds cost and irritation risk, not faster results.

6. If products pill or roll off

Pilling (little rolls of product on the skin) usually means too much product, too many layers, or not waiting between steps. Use less of each, wait 2 to 3 minutes between layers, and press rather than rub the top layer. Silicone-heavy primers and some sunscreens pill more over rich creams, so keep mornings light.

8. When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if your skin stays very dry, cracked or scaly even with a moisturiser, if you get many new bumps or an itchy rash after adding a product, or if you have eczema or another skin condition. Patch test any new moisturiser first, and add one new product at a time so you know what suits you.

FAQ

Can I apply moisturiser after glutathione cream?

Yes. Apply the glutathione cream first, wait 2 to 3 minutes, then a plain moisturiser on dry areas.

Can glutathione cream replace my moisturiser?

For many people with normal, oily or combination skin, yes. Dry skin may still need a moisturiser on top at night, especially in winter.

Should I use moisturiser in the morning with glutathione cream?

Usually not needed. A thin layer of the cream and sunscreen is enough for most skin; add a light gel only if your skin feels tight.

Can I mix moisturiser and glutathione cream in my palm?

It is better not to. Mixing dilutes the cream and makes it hard to know how much you are using. Layer them instead.

Does using a moisturiser with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. With daily sunscreen, the cream can help tan and marks look lighter over weeks.
