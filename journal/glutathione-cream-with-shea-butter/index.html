Can you use shea butter with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, on dry skin. Apply the glutathione cream first, wait a few minutes, then press a rice-grain amount of shea butter on dry patches at night, with sunscreen every morning. Oily and acne-prone skin usually does better without it; use shea on lips, hands and heels instead.

Yes, you can use shea butter with glutathione cream if your skin is dry: apply the cream first, wait a few minutes, then warm a tiny amount of shea butter between your fingers and press it only onto dry patches at night. Oily and acne-prone skin usually does better without it, and sunscreen every morning still matters most.

Shea butter comes from the nuts of the shea tree, which grows in West Africa. In India it is sold as raw or refined shea butter, and it is also a common ingredient in body butters, lip balms and winter creams. It is thick and rich, much heavier than a face oil, so a little goes a long way. It suits north Indian winters, room heaters and very dry cheeks; it is usually too heavy for humid coastal weather.

2. The right order

  1. Wash your face with a mild face wash and pat it dry.
  2. Apply a thin layer of the glutathione cream on the face and neck.
  3. Wait 3 to 5 minutes so the cream settles.
  4. Scrape a rice-grain-sized amount of shea butter with a clean spatula or the back of a clean spoon, not your fingers, so the tub stays clean.
  5. Warm it between your fingertips until it melts into a thin film.
  6. Press it onto dry patches only: cheeks, around the nose, chin and jawline. Skip the forehead and nose if they get oily.
  7. Use it at night. In the morning, a light moisturiser and sunscreen are enough.

Cream first, butter last: a rich butter seals in what is under it, so putting it first would stop the cream from sitting on your skin. For the daily cream steps, read how to use the cream.

4. Raw vs refined shea butter

  • Raw (unrefined) shea is ivory to yellowish, has a nutty, smoky smell and can feel slightly grainy. Some people like it; others find the smell strong on the face.
  • Refined shea is white, smooth and nearly odourless. It is easier to use on the face.
  • Read the label: for the face, pick a tub that says only shea butter (Butyrospermum parkii butter), without fragrance, colour or added essential oils.
  • Grainy texture happens when shea melts in summer heat and sets again. It is usually still usable; warm it between your fingers before applying.
  • Store it with the lid closed, away from sun and heat, and throw it away if it smells sharp or sour.

5. Shea butter for lips, hands and heels

Even if your face is too oily for shea butter, it is very useful elsewhere in winter. A thin layer on lips before sleep, on dry knuckles after washing utensils, and on cracked heels under socks helps those areas feel softer. The glutathione cream is a face cream; keep it on the face and neck, and use shea butter for the rest. For dark knuckles, see glutathione cream for dark knuckles.

6. Shea butter vs other rich options

Shea butter is in the same family as ghee, petroleum jelly and castor oil: all heavy, sealing layers best kept for very dry skin and cold nights. If you already have one of those at home, you do not need to buy shea. Compare them in petroleum jelly with glutathione cream, ghee with glutathione cream and glutathione cream in winter.

7. What not to do

  • Do not mix shea butter into the cream jar. It changes the cream and can spoil it.
  • Do not apply it under the cream. It would block the cream from reaching your skin.
  • Do not use a thick layer. A rice-grain amount is enough for both cheeks.
  • Do not use it before makeup or in the day on the face; read glutathione cream before makeup.
  • Do not expect it to fade spots or tan. Daily sunscreen does far more; see glutathione cream with sunscreen.

9. When to see a dermatologist

Stop the shea butter and see a dermatologist if you get many new bumps or whiteheads, if your skin turns red, itchy or swollen, or if dryness comes with cracking, scaling or patches that do not settle with a simple moisturiser. Patch test on the jawline for 2 to 3 nights first, and read glutathione cream for sensitive skin if you react easily.

FAQ

Can I apply shea butter after glutathione cream?

Yes, on dry skin. Apply the cream first, wait a few minutes, then press a rice-grain amount of shea butter on dry patches at night.

Is shea butter good for oily skin on the face?

Usually not. It is rich and heavy and can feel greasy or cause bumps on oily skin. Use it on lips, hands and heels instead.

Can I use shea butter in summer?

Most people do not need it on the face in an Indian summer. Keep it for dry winter nights, AC rooms or very dry patches.

Raw or refined shea butter: which is better for the face?

Refined shea is smoother and almost odourless, so many people find it easier on the face. Raw shea is fine too if the smell does not bother you and you patch test first.

Does shea butter with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Used with sunscreen, the cream can help tan and marks look lighter over weeks.
