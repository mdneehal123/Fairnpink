Can you use kumkumadi oil with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes. Apply the glutathione cream first, wait a few minutes, then press 2 to 3 drops of kumkumadi oil on top at night only, with sunscreen every morning. It suits dry winter skin best; oily and acne-prone skin can usually skip the oil.

Yes, you can use kumkumadi oil with glutathione cream: apply the cream first, then press 2 to 3 drops of the oil on top, at night only. Oil goes last because it sits on the skin and slows water loss; putting it first stops the cream from spreading and settling well.

Kumkumadi tailam is a traditional Ayurvedic face oil, usually made with saffron (kesar) and several herbs in an oil base, and it has become one of the most popular night oils in India. Here is how to pair it sensibly with a glutathione cream, and when to leave it out.

Kumkumadi oil is often described as good for varna (complexion). In plain terms, it can make skin feel softer and look more radiant. It does not change the skin colour you were born with, and neither does any cream.

2. The right order at night

  1. Wash with a gentle face wash and pat dry.
  2. Apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream over the face and neck.
  3. Wait 3 to 5 minutes so it settles.
  4. Warm 2 to 3 drops of kumkumadi oil between your fingertips and press them gently over dry areas or the whole face if your skin is dry.
  5. Use an old pillow cover; saffron-based oils can stain.
  6. Every morning: wash, then sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

This is the same thin-to-thick rule we explain in glutathione cream with vitamin E capsule.

4. How to start both safely

  • One at a time. If both are new, use the cream alone for a week or two, then add the oil a few nights a week. If something reacts, you will know which one.
  • Patch test each on the jaw or inner arm for 24 to 48 hours.
  • Read the label. Herbal oils vary a lot between makers. Check the full ingredient list, the base oil and the use-before date.
  • Keep it away from the eyes.

5. Mistakes to avoid

  • Oil first, cream second. The cream will not spread well over oil.
  • Using lots of drops. More oil does not mean more glow; it means greasy skin and a stained pillow.
  • Mixing oil into the cream jar. It changes the texture and can spoil the jar.
  • Wearing the oil in the day. It feels heavy in Indian heat and can make sunscreen slide.
  • Expecting the oil to fade spots. Dark spots and tan need sunscreen and weeks of steady care. See how to reduce dark spots on face.

6. Winter is the best season for it

From November to February, especially in North India, dry air, heaters and hot baths leave skin tight and flaky. That is when a few drops of oil on top of the cream make the most sense. In humid monsoon months or coastal cities, many people can drop the oil and use the cream alone. Our glutathione cream in winter guide has a full cold-season routine.

FAQ

Should I apply kumkumadi oil before or after glutathione cream?

After. Apply the cream first, wait a few minutes, then press 2 to 3 drops of oil on top at night.

Can I use kumkumadi oil and glutathione cream every night?

Dry skin often can in winter. Normal and combination skin may prefer a few nights a week; oily and acne-prone skin can usually skip the oil.

Can I use kumkumadi oil in the morning?

It is better at night. In the day it feels greasy and can make sunscreen and makeup slide.

Will kumkumadi oil with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Together they can help skin feel softer and look brighter and more even over weeks, with sunscreen.

Is kumkumadi oil good for pimples?

Heavy oils can trigger bumps on acne-prone skin. Skip it on breakout areas, and see a dermatologist for painful or frequent acne.
