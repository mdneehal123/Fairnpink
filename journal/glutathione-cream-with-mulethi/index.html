Can you use mulethi (licorice) with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, as a separate step: apply a simple mulethi powder pack on clean skin, rinse after 10 to 15 minutes, pat dry, then the glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Never mix powder into the jar; a home pack will not remove spots or change skin colour.

Yes, you can use mulethi with glutathione cream, but as a separate step: apply a simple mulethi powder pack on clean skin, rinse it off after 10 to 15 minutes, pat dry, then apply the glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Never stir mulethi powder into the cream jar.

Mulethi is the Hindi name for licorice root, a common Ayurvedic ingredient sold as a fine brown powder. Licorice root extract is also used in many cosmetic creams for an even-looking tone. But the powder from a kitchen or Ayurvedic store is not the same as a purified cosmetic extract, so a home pack is a gentle extra, not a brightening treatment, and it does not change your natural skin colour.

2. How to use mulethi with the cream

  1. Patch test first: apply a little of the pack on your jawline for 10 minutes, rinse, and wait a day.
  2. Wash your face with a mild face wash and pat it dry.
  3. Mix the pack: about half a teaspoon of mulethi powder with enough rose water, plain curd or aloe gel to make a smooth paste.
  4. Apply a thin layer, avoiding the eyes and lips.
  5. Rinse off after 10 to 15 minutes, before it dries hard and starts pulling at the skin. Do not scrub it off.
  6. Pat dry, wait a few minutes, then apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream.
  7. Next morning: sunscreen as usual.

Once or twice a week is plenty. For the cream steps on other days, see how to use the cream. Our guides on rose water and curd explain those mixers on their own.

3. Kitchen powder vs cosmetic licorice extract

  • Mulethi powder is the whole dried root, ground. How much of the useful parts it contains varies from pack to pack, and it can be gritty.
  • Licorice root extract in a cream is purified and made for skin, so it is more consistent and easier to use every day.
  • If you like the idea of licorice for an even-looking tone, a cream or serum that lists licorice extract is more reliable than a home pack.
  • Buy powder in a sealed pack with a date, and keep it dry. Throw it away if it smells musty or gets damp.

4. Who should skip it

  • Sensitive or reactive skin: any herbal pack can sting. See glutathione cream for sensitive skin.
  • Freshly waxed, threaded or bleached skin: wait a day or two; read face cream after facial, threading and waxing.
  • Active pimples, cuts or rashes: keep the pack away from these areas.
  • Anyone allergic to licorice: skip it entirely.

5. Common mistakes

  • Mixing mulethi into the cream jar. Powder and water from the paste can spoil the cream. Keep them separate.
  • Adding lemon or baking soda to make it "stronger". These can irritate skin; see lemon with glutathione cream.
  • Leaving the pack on until it cracks. A dry, tight pack can leave skin rough. Rinse while it is still slightly damp.
  • Layering many brightening packs. Mulethi, haldi, potato juice and besan on the same night can dry skin out. Pick one pack per week; see turmeric with glutathione cream.
  • Skipping sunscreen. Sun is the main reason tan and spots come back.

6. Mulethi tea and supplements are a different matter

Mulethi is also taken as tea, churna or tablets for other reasons. Eating or drinking it does not lighten spots, and licorice taken by mouth is not suitable for everyone, for example people with high blood pressure or during pregnancy. Ask your doctor before taking mulethi by mouth; this guide is only about a wash-off face pack.

Brown patches on both cheeks, the forehead or upper lip may be melasma and need a dermatologist's plan. Also stop and see a dermatologist if a pack causes burning, itching, swelling or a rash.

FAQ

Can I mix mulethi powder with glutathione cream?

No. Use mulethi as a wash-off pack first, then apply the cream on clean, dry skin.

How often can I use a mulethi face pack?

Once or twice a week is enough. Daily packs are not needed and can dry out sensitive skin.

Can I leave mulethi on my face overnight?

No. Rinse it off after 10 to 15 minutes, then use the cream as your overnight step.

Is mulethi good for dark spots?

A home pack will not remove dark spots. Spots fade slowly over weeks with sunscreen and a steady routine.

Does mulethi with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. With sunscreen, the cream can help tan and marks look lighter over weeks.
