Can you use glutathione cream with a hyaluronic acid serum?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, glutathione cream and hyaluronic acid serum work well together: apply 2 to 3 drops of serum on slightly damp skin, wait a minute, then the cream on top, with sunscreen every morning. The cream holds in the water the serum draws to the skin.

Yes, glutathione cream and a hyaluronic acid serum work well together: apply the serum first on slightly damp skin, then the cream on top to hold that water in. Hyaluronic acid adds hydration and the cream helps skin look brighter and more even over weeks, so they do different jobs and rarely clash.

Hyaluronic acid serums are now one of the most common serums in Indian bathrooms. Used the wrong way, especially in dry winter air or an AC room, they can leave skin feeling tighter, not softer. Here is the simple way to pair one with a glutathione cream.

2. The right order

  1. Wash with a gentle face wash. Pat dry lightly so the face is still a little damp.
  2. Apply 2 to 3 drops of hyaluronic acid serum and press it in with your fingertips.
  3. Wait about a minute, until it no longer feels wet.
  4. Apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream over the face and neck.
  5. Morning only: finish with sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

Thin first, thick last. The same order works for other serums, as explained in niacinamide and alpha arbutin.

3. Why damp skin and a cream on top matter

Hyaluronic acid holds water. On damp skin, it holds the water from your wash. On bone-dry skin in dry air, it has less to work with, and without a cream on top that water evaporates quickly, so the face can feel tight an hour later. The cream acts as a light lid. In North Indian winters, heater rooms and all-day AC offices, this step matters most.

5. Choosing a hyaluronic acid serum

  • Keep it simple. A plain hydrating serum is enough. You do not need a second brightening serum if your cream already has brightening actives.
  • Check the full ingredient list. Avoid serums with strong fragrance if your skin is sensitive.
  • Be careful with mixed acid serums. Some "hyaluronic" serums also contain exfoliating acids. Those are not plain hydrators; use them on separate nights.
  • Higher price is not always better. Look at the ingredient list and the amount, not the label.

6. Mistakes to avoid

  • Using lots of drops. More serum can feel sticky and make the cream pill. Two or three drops is plenty.
  • Skipping the cream on top in dry air.
  • Applying the serum after the cream. It will not sink in well.
  • Expecting the serum to fade spots. It makes skin look plumper and fresher; tan and spots need steady sunscreen and time. See how long glutathione cream takes to work.
  • Starting both on the same day. Introduce one at a time, a week apart, so you know what suits you.

FAQ

Should I apply hyaluronic acid before or after glutathione cream?

Before. Apply the serum on slightly damp skin, wait about a minute, then apply the cream on top.

Can I use hyaluronic acid and glutathione cream every day?

Yes, if your skin is comfortable. Many people use the serum morning and night and the cream once or twice a day, with sunscreen every morning.

Can I use hyaluronic acid with niacinamide and alpha arbutin?

Yes. Hyaluronic acid is a gentle hydrator and usually sits well under creams that contain niacinamide and alpha arbutin.

Why does my skin feel tight after hyaluronic acid serum?

Usually because it went on dry skin in dry air with nothing on top. Apply it on damp skin and follow with a cream.

Will hyaluronic acid with glutathione make my skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Together they can help skin look plumper, brighter and more even over time.
