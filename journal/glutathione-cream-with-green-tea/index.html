Can you use green tea with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, as a separate step: pat cooled, plain green tea on clean skin as a toner or compress, let it dry for a minute, then apply the glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Make the tea fresh, keep it in the fridge for a day at most, and never mix it into the jar.

Yes, you can use green tea with glutathione cream: pat cooled, plain green tea on clean skin as a toner or compress, let it dry for a minute, then apply a thin layer of the cream. Make the tea fresh, never mix it into the jar, and wear sunscreen every morning.

Green tea is in many face washes and creams, and a cup of cooled green tea is a common home toner in India. Green tea contains antioxidants called polyphenols, which are why skincare brands use it. A home brew is much weaker and less stable than a made-for-skin product, so treat it as a gentle, refreshing step rather than a spot or tan remover.

2. How to make a green tea toner

  1. Use plain green tea: one tea bag or one teaspoon of leaves, with no lemon, honey, mint or added flavour.
  2. Steep it in a cup of hot water for 2 to 3 minutes, then remove the bag or strain the leaves.
  3. Let it cool fully to room temperature, or chill it in the fridge for a cooler feel.
  4. Pour it into a clean, dry bottle or bowl with a lid.
  5. Keep it in the fridge and use it within 24 hours. Throw it away if it smells sour or looks cloudy.

3. The right order with the cream

  1. Patch test the tea on your jawline for a day if your skin is sensitive.
  2. Wash your face with a mild face wash and pat it dry.
  3. Pat the cooled tea on with clean cotton, or lay a soaked cotton pad or soft cloth on the face for 5 minutes as a compress.
  4. Let it dry for a minute. Do not rinse; it is only tea.
  5. Apply a thin layer of the glutathione cream on the face and neck.
  6. Sunscreen every morning, SPF 30 or higher. This matters more than any toner.

4. Green tea bags and ice cubes for puffy eyes

Used, cooled green tea bags laid on closed eyes for 5 to 10 minutes are a popular trick for puffy mornings. The cool, damp bag can make puffiness look less for a while. It does not change dark circles caused by thin skin, shadows or genes. Squeeze out extra liquid so it does not drip into the eyes, and keep the cream away from the eyelids; read glutathione cream for dark circles. You can also freeze cooled tea in an ice tray and wrap a cube in a thin cloth for a quick cool press in summer.

6. Green tea for oily skin and pimples: an honest view

A cool green tea toner can feel refreshing on an oily face in humid weather. It does not stop oil or clear pimples, and a weak home brew is not a substitute for proper acne care. Keep your routine light; read glutathione cream for oily skin and glutathione cream for acne-prone skin. Painful, deep or frequent pimples need a dermatologist.

Drinking green tea is a food choice. It does not replace sunscreen or a skincare routine, and it has caffeine, so do not overdo it.

8. When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if your skin burns, itches or breaks into a rash after any new step, if redness lasts more than a day, or if dark patches on the cheeks keep spreading, which can be melasma.

FAQ

Can I apply glutathione cream after green tea toner?

Yes. Let the tea dry on your face for a minute, then apply a thin layer of the cream.

Can I mix green tea into my glutathione cream?

No. Liquid in the jar can spoil the cream. Keep green tea as a separate step.

How long can I keep homemade green tea toner?

About a day in the fridge, in a clean closed bottle. Make a fresh batch after that.

Can I use green tea on my face every day?

Yes, a plain, fresh, cooled toner is gentle enough for daily use on most skin. Stop if it stings.

Does green tea with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Used with sunscreen, the cream can help tan and marks look lighter over weeks.
