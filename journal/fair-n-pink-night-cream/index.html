Can Fair N Pink be used as a night cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Yes. Fair N Pink Advance Radiance Cream is a day and night cream, and many people use it mainly as a night cream. Night is a good time for it: your skin is clean, and the cream stays on for hours without sun, sweat or makeup.

How to use it at night

  1. Wash off the day's dirt, sweat and sunscreen with a mild cleanser.
  2. Pat your face dry.
  3. Take a pea-sized amount and massage it gently over face and neck.
  4. Leave it on overnight. Wash your face as usual in the morning.

Is night-only use enough?

It can be. If your skin is oily, sensitive or new to the cream, using it only at night is a sensible way to start. If your skin is comfortable after a week, you can add a morning application under sunscreen.

What to do in the morning

Use sunscreen every morning, whether or not you apply the cream. Sun exposure during the day undoes what any brightening cream does at night.

Can you layer it with other night products?

Keep it simple. Use the cream as your last step. Avoid strong peels or scrubs on the same night, and if you use a prescription cream, ask your doctor how to combine them.
