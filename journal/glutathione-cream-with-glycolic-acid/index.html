Can you use glycolic acid with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes. Use a glycolic face wash before the cream, or a leave-on glycolic product on 1 to 3 nights a week with the glutathione cream on the other nights. Start slowly, keep retinol on different nights, and wear sunscreen every morning.

Yes, you can use glycolic acid with glutathione cream: the simplest way is a glycolic face wash followed by the cream, or a leave-on glycolic toner or serum on 1 to 3 nights a week with the cream on the other nights. Start slowly, never stack it with other strong acids or retinol on the same night, and wear sunscreen every morning, because glycolic acid makes skin more sensitive to the sun.

Glycolic acid is an AHA (alpha hydroxy acid) found in many face washes, toners, serums and peel pads sold in India. It loosens dull surface cells so skin feels smoother. Used too often, though, it can sting, peel and leave a red, raw barrier, and Indian skin can then develop darker marks from that irritation. Pairing it with a gentle brightening cream works best when the acid is kept light.

2. Three ways to combine them

What you haveHow to use it with the cream
Glycolic face washWash at night (not every day if it stings), pat dry, then the cream. The wash is rinsed off, so this is the gentlest option.
Glycolic toner or pads1 to 3 nights a week: toner, wait 10 to 15 minutes, then a thin layer of cream. On the other nights, cream only.
Glycolic serum or peelStart once a week, on a night with no cream; use the cream on the other nights. Go up slowly only if skin stays calm.

3. A simple start plan

  1. Patch test both on the jaw or inner arm for 24 to 48 hours.
  2. Weeks 1 to 2: glycolic product one night a week; glutathione cream on the other nights and in the morning.
  3. Weeks 3 to 4: if there is no stinging, redness or peeling, go up to two nights a week.
  4. After that: stay at two, or at most three, nights a week. More is not better.
  5. Every morning: sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, as the last step. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

4. What not to stack on glycolic nights

  • Retinol. Use retinol and glycolic acid on different nights. See glutathione cream with retinol.
  • Salicylic acid leave-ons. A salicylic face wash is usually fine; two leave-on acids on one night are too much for most skin. See glutathione cream with salicylic acid.
  • Scrubs, lemon, baking soda or bleach. These add more irritation on top of an acid.
  • Freshly threaded, waxed or bleached skin. Wait a few days. See face cream after facial, threading or waxing.

5. Winter and summer care

In dry North Indian winters and heater-dried rooms, cut glycolic use to once a week or pause it, and put a plain moisturiser on top of the cream if skin feels tight. See glutathione cream for dry skin. In summer and on beach or hill trips, the sun matters even more after an acid: reapply sunscreen when outdoors and skip glycolic the night before long sun exposure.

6. Signs you are overdoing it

  • Stinging when you apply a plain moisturiser or even water.
  • Shiny, tight skin that looks polished but feels raw.
  • Flaking, redness or new small bumps.
  • New dark patches where skin was irritated.

If you see these, stop the acid, use only a gentle wash, a plain moisturiser and sunscreen for a week or two, then restart at once a week, or not at all. Sensitive skin may do better without glycolic acid; see glutathione cream for sensitive skin.

FAQ

Can I apply glutathione cream after glycolic acid?

After a glycolic face wash, yes. After a leave-on toner, wait 10 to 15 minutes and use a thin layer. After a serum or peel, use the cream on a different night.

How many times a week can I use glycolic acid?

Start once a week and stay at two or three nights at most. Dry winter skin may need less.

Can I use glycolic acid in the morning?

It is better at night. If you use it, sunscreen every morning is a must, because it makes skin more sensitive to the sun.

Is glycolic acid safe for Indian skin?

Mild glycolic products suit many people, but over-use can irritate and leave darker marks. Start slow and stop if skin stings or peels.

Will glycolic acid with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Together, used gently with sunscreen, they can help skin feel smoother and look brighter and more even.
