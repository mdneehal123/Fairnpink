Can you use sandalwood (chandan) with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, as a separate step: mix chandan powder with rose water or milk, leave the pack on for about 10 minutes, rinse, pat dry, then apply the glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Never mix chandan into the jar, and do not put sandalwood essential oil straight on the face.

You can use sandalwood with glutathione cream as a short wash-off pack before the cream: mix a little chandan powder with rose water or milk, leave it for about 10 minutes, rinse it off, pat dry and then apply the cream. Do not mix chandan into the jar, and do not put sandalwood essential oil straight on your face.

Chandan packs are one of the oldest Indian beauty habits, loved for the cool feel and the scent, and haldi-chandan ubtan is everywhere in wedding season. A pack like this can leave skin feeling fresh and smooth. Here is how to fit it around a glutathione cream without irritating your skin.

2. The right order on a pack day

  1. Patch test the pack first on the side of the jaw. Any stinging, redness or itching means it is not for your face.
  2. Wash with a gentle face wash and pat dry.
  3. Mix about 1 teaspoon of chandan powder with enough rose water or cold milk to make a smooth paste. Make it fresh each time.
  4. Apply a thin layer, avoiding the eyes and lips, and leave it for about 10 minutes. Rinse while it is still slightly damp; a pack that dries and cracks pulls at the skin.
  5. Rinse with plain water, using your fingers gently. Do not scrub the paste off.
  6. Pat dry, wait a minute, then apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream on the face and neck.
  7. Every morning: sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, as the last step. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

Once or twice a week is plenty. If you use other packs, see glutathione cream with multani mitti and rose water with glutathione cream; they follow the same rinse-first rule.

3. Chandan powder vs sandalwood oil

These are not the same thing on the face.

  • Chandan powder is used in small amounts in a wash-off pack, so it touches skin for only a few minutes.
  • Sandalwood essential oil is a strong, concentrated fragrance oil. Put straight on the face, it can sting or cause a rash in some people. Do not dab it neat on skin, and do not add it to your cream.
  • "Sandal" creams and soaps often contain added perfume. If your skin is sensitive, a fragrance-heavy product is a common reason for redness.

5. Chandan in wedding season

Haldi-chandan ubtan is part of many pre-wedding rituals. If you are using a glutathione cream in the weeks before a wedding, keep any ubtan or chandan pack gentle, rinse it off, and do not try a new pack in the last week before the day. For the full method, see glutathione cream with ubtan and glutathione cream with turmeric. On the function day itself, see glutathione cream before makeup.

6. Mistakes to avoid

  • Mixing chandan powder into the jar. It makes the cream gritty and can spoil a small 10 g jar.
  • Adding lemon to "boost" the pack. Raw lemon often stings and can leave dark marks in the sun.
  • Leaving the pack on until it cracks, or sleeping in it.
  • Buying very cheap "pure sandal" powder with no label. Choose a packed powder with an ingredient list and a date.
  • Expecting tan to go after one pack. Tan and dark spots fade slowly. See how long glutathione cream takes to work.

FAQ

Should I use the chandan pack before or after glutathione cream?

Before. Rinse the pack off, pat dry, then apply the cream. A pack on top of the cream would wipe it away.

Can I mix chandan powder into glutathione cream?

No. It makes the cream gritty and can spoil the jar. Keep the pack as a separate wash-off step.

How often can I use a chandan pack with glutathione cream?

Once or twice a week is enough for most skin. Daily packs can leave skin dry and tight.

Can I apply sandalwood oil with glutathione cream?

Not neat on the face. Sandalwood essential oil is a strong fragrance oil that can irritate. Stick to a powder pack that you rinse off.

Does chandan with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. A chandan pack leaves skin feeling fresh; used with sunscreen, the cream can help skin look brighter and tan look lighter over weeks.
