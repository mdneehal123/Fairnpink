How long does glutathione cream take to work?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Give a glutathione cream four to eight weeks of regular use before you judge it. Skin renews itself roughly once a month, so nothing applied to the surface can show a real change in a few days.

Week by week

  • Week 1: skin usually feels softer and better moisturised. That is the cream base at work, not a change in tone.
  • Weeks 2 to 3: skin may start to look fresher, especially if you are also using sunscreen daily.
  • Week 4: the first fair check. Compare with a photo from day one, taken in the same daylight.
  • Week 8: dark spots and marks take the longest. Some fade, some do not.

What slows results down

  • Skipping sunscreen. This is the most common reason people see nothing.
  • Using the cream only now and then.
  • Scrubbing, bleaching or picking at the skin, which causes new marks.

Does using more make it faster?

No. A pea-sized amount twice a day is enough. More cream only finishes the jar sooner.

What if nothing changes?

Results differ from person to person, and a cosmetic cannot change your natural skin tone. If dark patches have not changed after two to three months of careful use with sunscreen, see a dermatologist. See also how to use Fair N Pink cream.
