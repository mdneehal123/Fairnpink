Niacinamide night cream: benefits and how to use it

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

A niacinamide night cream helps skin look smoother and more even and supports its moisture barrier overnight. It suits most skin types; use a pea-sized amount nightly and sunscreen each morning.

A niacinamide night cream helps skin look smoother and more even and supports its moisture barrier while you sleep. It suits most skin types, including oily and sensitive skin, and works best used every night for several weeks with sunscreen in the morning.

What niacinamide is

Niacinamide is a form of vitamin B3. It is one of the most widely used and best-tolerated skincare ingredients.

Benefits people use it for

  • More even-looking skin tone
  • Smoother-looking texture
  • Support for the skin's own moisture barrier
  • Less visible shine on oily skin

How to use it at night

  1. Cleanse and pat your face dry.
  2. Apply a pea-sized amount to face and neck.
  3. Leave it on overnight. Use sunscreen the next morning.

Good partners

Niacinamide works well with alpha arbutin for the look of dark spots, and with glutathione in creams for a brighter-looking complexion. Fair N Pink Advance Radiance Cream has all three in one niacinamide and alpha arbutin cream.

Side effects

Most people have none. A few notice mild tingling or redness in the first days. Patch test first.
