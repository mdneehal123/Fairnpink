Can you use tulsi with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, carefully. Use tulsi only as a short rinse-off pack mixed with curd, rose water or multani mitti, leave it on 5 to 10 minutes, rinse, pat dry and then apply the cream. Patch test first, and skip it on dry or sensitive skin.

Yes, you can use tulsi with glutathione cream, carefully: use tulsi only as a short, rinse-off face pack, wash it off, pat dry and then apply the cream. Never mix tulsi into the cream jar, and always patch test first, because crushed tulsi leaves can sting some skin.

Tulsi (holy basil) grows in many Indian homes and is often added to home packs, especially with neem for oily skin. The leaves have a strong, warm smell from natural aromatic oils. Those same oils are why fresh paste can tingle or irritate. A tulsi pack is a refreshing extra; it does not fade dark spots or tan, and it does not change your natural skin colour.

2. Patch test first

  1. Put a little of the pack on the jawline or behind the ear.
  2. Leave it for 10 minutes, then rinse.
  3. Watch the spot for 24 hours. If it turns red, itchy or burning, do not use tulsi on your face.

Read glutathione cream for sensitive skin if you react easily to new products.

3. A simple tulsi pack

  1. Wash 8 to 10 fresh tulsi leaves well, or use 1 teaspoon of plain tulsi powder from a sealed pack.
  2. Grind the leaves smooth with a little water, then mix with a base to soften it: plain curd for normal skin, rose water or multani mitti for oily skin.
  3. Wash your face with a mild face wash.
  4. Apply a thin layer, away from the eyes and lips.
  5. Leave it on for 5 to 10 minutes only. Rinse sooner if it tingles.
  6. Rinse with lukewarm water and pat dry. Do not scrub.

Do not put pure tulsi leaf paste or tulsi essential oil straight on the face; mixing with a soft base and keeping the time short makes it gentler. For packs that pair well, see glutathione cream with multani mitti, glutathione cream with curd and rose water with glutathione cream.

4. The right order on pack nights

  1. Tulsi pack, rinsed off.
  2. Pat dry and wait 5 minutes, a little longer than usual, so any tingle settles.
  3. Apply a thin layer of the glutathione cream. If your face still feels warm or stings, use a plain moisturiser that night instead.
  4. Next morning: sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher.

6. What not to do

  • Do not mix tulsi paste or powder into the cream jar.
  • Do not use tulsi essential oil undiluted on the face.
  • Do not leave the pack on until it dries and cracks.
  • Do not add lemon or toothpaste to "boost" it; see lemon with glutathione cream.
  • Do not expect it to fade marks. Sunscreen every morning and steady skincare over weeks matter far more.

FAQ

Can I apply glutathione cream after a tulsi pack?

Yes, once the pack is rinsed off and your skin feels calm and dry. If it still tingles, use a plain moisturiser that night.

Can I mix tulsi into glutathione cream?

No. Keep the jar clean and use tulsi only as a rinse-off pack.

Is tulsi good for oily skin?

Many people with oily skin like how a short tulsi pack leaves the skin feeling fresh. Keep it to once a week and patch test first.

Can I use tulsi and neem together?

Yes, in a small rinse-off pack for oily skin, but both can be strong, so keep it short and skip it on dry or sensitive skin.

Can tulsi make skin fair?

No. It does not change your natural skin colour. Used with sunscreen, the cream can help tan and marks look lighter over weeks.
