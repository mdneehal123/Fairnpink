Can I use glutathione cream with sunscreen?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Yes, and you should. Sunscreen is the most important partner for any cream used for brighter-looking skin, because sun exposure is the main cause of dullness, tanning and dark patches.

Which goes first?

The cream goes first, on clean skin. Sunscreen always goes last in the morning, so it sits on top and is not rubbed away.

How long to wait

Give the cream a minute or two to settle, then apply sunscreen. You do not need a long gap.

What kind of sunscreen

  • Broad spectrum, which covers both UVA and UVB
  • SPF 30 or higher for daily use
  • A texture you like, because the best sunscreen is the one you actually wear every day

How much and how often

Use about two finger-lengths for the face and neck. Reapply every two to three hours if you are outdoors, and after sweating or washing your face.

Do you need sunscreen indoors?

If you sit near a window or go out during the day, yes. At night you do not need it: use the cream alone. See the full morning and night routine.
