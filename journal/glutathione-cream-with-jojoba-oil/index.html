Can you use jojoba oil with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes. Apply the glutathione cream first, wait a few minutes, then press 1 to 2 drops of jojoba oil on dry areas at night, with sunscreen every morning. It suits dry winter skin; oily skin in humid weather can usually skip it, and never mix it into the jar.

Jojoba oil comes from the seed of the jojoba plant. It is technically a liquid wax rather than a true oil, and it feels lighter and less greasy than coconut or castor oil. That makes it a popular choice in India for people who want a little extra softness in winter without a heavy, shiny layer.

2. The right order

  1. Wash your face with a mild face wash and pat it dry.
  2. Apply a thin layer of the glutathione cream on the face and neck.
  3. Wait 3 to 5 minutes for the cream to settle.
  4. Warm 1 to 2 drops of jojoba oil between your fingertips.
  5. Press it gently onto dry areas only: cheeks, around the nose, the chin or the jawline. Do not rub hard.
  6. Use it at night. In the morning, a light moisturiser and sunscreen are enough.

Putting the cream on first lets it sit directly on the skin; the oil on top then helps slow water loss. For the daily cream steps, read how to use the cream.

4. Jojoba vs other oils

Compared with coconut oil and castor oil, jojoba oil feels lighter and less sticky, so it is easier to use on the face in Indian weather. Almond oil is similar for dry skin but should be skipped with a nut allergy. Kumkumadi oil is a blend with several herbs and a stronger smell, which some sensitive skins do not like. Read glutathione cream with almond oil, glutathione cream with coconut oil and kumkumadi oil with glutathione cream to compare.

5. How to buy and store jojoba oil

  • Check the label for 100% jojoba seed oil (Simmondsia chinensis seed oil), not a "jojoba-scented" blend with mineral oil or fragrance.
  • Cold-pressed jojoba is golden; refined jojoba is clear. Both work as a face oil.
  • Pick a small bottle with a dropper; you only need a few drops a night.
  • Keep it capped, away from sunlight and the bathroom heater, and check the expiry date.
  • Throw it away if the smell changes.

6. What not to do

  • Do not mix jojoba oil into the cream jar. It changes the cream and can spoil it.
  • Do not use it under the cream; the oil layer can keep the cream from sitting on the skin.
  • Do not add essential oils like tea tree or lemon oil to it for the whole face; see glutathione cream with tea tree oil.
  • Do not use it before makeup on oily skin; it can make foundation slide. See glutathione cream before makeup.
  • Do not expect it to fade spots or tan. Sunscreen every morning does far more.

8. When to see a dermatologist

Stop the oil and see a dermatologist if your skin turns red, itchy or swollen, if you get many new bumps, or if dryness comes with cracks, scaling or itching that does not settle with a simple moisturiser. Patch test any new oil on the jawline for 2 to 3 nights first, and read glutathione cream for sensitive skin if you react easily.

FAQ

Can I apply jojoba oil after glutathione cream?

Yes. Apply the cream first, wait a few minutes, then press 1 to 2 drops of jojoba oil on dry areas at night.

Can I mix jojoba oil with glutathione cream in my palm?

It is better to layer them: cream first, oil on top. Never mix oil into the jar itself.

Is jojoba oil good for oily skin?

It is lighter than most oils, but oily skin usually does not need extra oil, especially in humid weather. Skip it or use a single drop only on dry patches.

Can I use jojoba oil every night?

Yes, if your skin is dry and you have no reaction. On nights your skin does not feel tight, you can skip it.

Does jojoba oil with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Used with sunscreen, the cream can help tan and marks look lighter over weeks.
