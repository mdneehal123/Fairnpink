Can you use rose water with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes. Pat a plain rose water on clean skin first, wait about a minute, then apply the glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Do not mix rose water into the jar, and patch test both if your skin is sensitive.

Yes, you can use rose water with glutathione cream: splash or pat the rose water on clean skin first, let it dry for a minute, then apply the cream. Rose water is a light toner, so it goes before the cream, never mixed into the jar.

Gulab jal is in almost every Indian home, in face packs, ubtan and as a cooling splash in summer. It pairs easily with a glutathione cream if you choose a plain one and keep expectations realistic. Here is how.

2. The right order

  1. Wash with a gentle face wash and pat dry.
  2. Rose water: pour a little on a clean cotton pad and wipe gently, or splash a few drops with clean hands. A spray bottle works too.
  3. Wait about a minute until the skin is just slightly damp, not dripping.
  4. Apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream on the face and neck.
  5. Morning only: sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, as the last step. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

Applying the cream on slightly damp skin helps it spread easily, which is useful in dry winter air. It follows the same thin-to-thick order we explain in glutathione cream with hyaluronic acid serum.

3. How to choose rose water

  • Read the label. Look for a short list: rose distillate or rose water, with a preservative. Plain is best.
  • Avoid added alcohol and strong perfume if your skin is dry or sensitive; they can leave it tight or itchy.
  • Check the use-before date and close the bottle after use. Homemade rose water spoils quickly, so make small amounts and keep them in the fridge for a few days only.
  • Keep it clean. Do not dip used cotton back into the bottle.

5. Mistakes to avoid

  • Mixing rose water into the cream jar. Adding water can spoil the jar and changes the texture.
  • Soaking the face. Applying cream on dripping-wet skin makes it slide around and look patchy.
  • Adding lemon or other kitchen items to the rose water. Lemon can irritate the skin, especially in the sun.
  • Using rose water instead of washing. It does not take off sunscreen, makeup or sweat; wash first.
  • Expecting rose water to fade spots. Dark spots and tan need sunscreen and weeks of steady care. See how to reduce dark spots on face.

6. Rose water in face packs and ubtan

Many people use rose water to mix besan, multani mitti or sandalwood packs. That is fine as a separate wash-off step: use the pack, rinse it off, pat dry, then follow the order above. Our guide to glutathione cream with ubtan covers how often and when to skip the cream after a pack.

FAQ

Should I apply rose water before or after glutathione cream?

Before. Rose water is a light toner, so apply it on clean skin, wait about a minute, then apply the cream.

Can I mix rose water with glutathione cream in my palm?

It is better to keep them as separate steps. Mixing thins the cream and makes it harder to spread evenly.

Can I use rose water and glutathione cream every day?

Yes, most people can use both morning and night, with sunscreen every morning, once a patch test is clear.

Does rose water with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Rose water refreshes the skin, and the cream can help skin look brighter and more even over weeks. Neither changes your natural skin colour.

Is rose water good for oily skin with glutathione cream?

Yes, a plain rose water feels light and refreshing on oily skin. Follow with a thin layer of cream and sunscreen in the morning.
