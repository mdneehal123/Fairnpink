Can you use multani mitti with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, as a separate step: apply a thin multani mitti pack, rinse after about 10 minutes before it cracks, pat dry, then apply the glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Use the pack once or twice a week at most and never mix it into the jar.

Yes, you can use multani mitti with glutathione cream as two separate steps: apply a thin multani mitti pack on clean skin, rinse it off after about 10 minutes while it is still slightly damp, pat dry, then apply the cream. Use the pack once or twice a week at most, and never mix the clay into the cream jar.

Multani mitti, or fuller's earth, is a clay that soaks up oil. That makes it a favourite for oily skin and hot, sticky weather, but it can also leave skin tight and dry if it is left on too long or used too often. Following it with a cream is the right instinct. Here is how to do it well.

2. The right order

  1. Wash with a gentle face wash and pat dry.
  2. Mix a little multani mitti with plain water or rose water into a smooth paste. Make only enough for one use.
  3. Apply a thin layer on the face, avoiding the eyes, lips and any dry patches.
  4. Leave it for about 10 minutes. Rinse while it is still slightly damp; do not wait for it to crack.
  5. Rinse with lukewarm water, using your fingertips softly. Do not scrub it off.
  6. Pat dry and apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream on the face and neck within a minute or two.
  7. Every morning: sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, as the last step. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

If your skin feels warm, tight or stings after the pack, skip the cream that night and use a plain moisturiser or aloe vera gel instead. Go back to the cream the next night. For a gentler pack before the cream, see glutathione cream with ubtan.

4. Why not mix multani mitti into the cream

  • It spoils the jar. Clay and water bring in dirt and germs a leave-on cream is not made to handle.
  • It fights the cream. Clay soaks up oil and moisture, so the mix would dry your skin and leave a gritty film.
  • It adds nothing useful. The cream works as made; a pack is best as a separate, rinse-off step.

5. Mistakes to avoid

  • Letting the pack dry and crack. That pulls too much moisture from the skin and can leave it red and tight.
  • Adding lemon, baking soda or bleach powder. These can sting and irritate, especially before the sun.
  • Using it every day. Daily clay packs dry most skin out, and dry skin can then make more oil.
  • Putting it on active pimples and scrubbing. Be gentle; for flat marks left after pimples, see glutathione cream for pimple marks.
  • Expecting the pack to fade tan or spots. Those fade slowly with daily sunscreen and steady care. See how to reduce dark spots on face.

6. Buying multani mitti

Choose plain multani mitti powder with no added perfume or colour, from a sealed pack. Store it dry with the lid closed, and use a clean, dry spoon. Mix it fresh each time and throw away any leftover paste.

FAQ

Should I apply multani mitti before or after glutathione cream?

Before, and rinse it off. Use it as a short wash-off pack, pat dry, then apply the cream.

Can I mix multani mitti with glutathione cream?

No. Keep them separate. Clay can spoil the cream and dries the skin, which works against it.

How many times a week can I use multani mitti?

Once or twice a week for oily skin, less for normal skin, and usually not at all for dry or sensitive skin.

Does multani mitti remove tan?

There is little good evidence that it does. Tan looks lighter over weeks mainly with daily sunscreen and less sun; a cream like ours can help skin look more even.

Does multani mitti with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. The pack leaves skin feeling clean, and the cream can help skin look brighter and more even over weeks with sunscreen.
