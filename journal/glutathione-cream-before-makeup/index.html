Can you apply glutathione cream before makeup?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, glutathione cream can go under makeup: apply a thin layer on clean skin, wait 3 to 5 minutes, then sunscreen, then primer and foundation. Use less cream on oily skin to avoid pilling and shine, and remove makeup before your night routine.

Yes, you can apply a glutathione cream before makeup. Use a thin layer on clean skin, give it three to five minutes to settle, then apply sunscreen, and only then your primer, foundation or compact.

Most problems people have with cream under makeup come from using too much or not waiting, not from the cream itself. Too much product makes foundation slide, patch or roll into little balls (pilling), especially in humid Indian weather. A small amount and a short pause usually fix it.

1. The right order, step by step

StepWhat to doWait before the next step
1. CleanseWash with a mild face wash and pat dryAbout a minute

The rule of thumb is thinnest to thickest, and sunscreen always last in skincare, just before makeup. Makeup with SPF is not a replacement for sunscreen, because people rarely apply enough foundation or compact to get the protection on the label. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

2. How much cream to use under makeup

Use less than you would at night. For most people, half a pea-sized amount is enough on oily skin and one pea-sized amount on dry skin. Dot it on the forehead, cheeks, nose and chin, then spread outwards. If your face still looks shiny after five minutes, you used too much; gently press a clean tissue on it rather than wiping. The general how to use page shows the basic morning and night steps.

3. Fixing common problems

  • Pilling (little balls when you apply foundation): use less cream, wait longer between layers, and pat products on instead of rubbing back and forth.
  • Makeup sliding by noon: use a thinner layer of cream, choose a gel or matte sunscreen, and use a primer and compact on the T-zone only. Our guide to glutathione cream for oily skin has more tips.
  • Patchy, dry-looking foundation: skin may be dry; apply the cream on slightly damp skin and add a plain moisturiser before sunscreen. See glutathione cream in winter.
  • Stinging under makeup: stop using the cream in the morning, keep it for night only, and see side effects and safety if it continues.

4. For long days, weddings and functions

Indian weddings and festivals often mean ten or more hours of makeup in heat, crowds and lights. On such days:

  1. Keep the same cream you already use; never try a new cream, facial or bleach in the last few days before an event.
  2. Use the thinnest layer of cream that feels comfortable, or skip it in the morning and rely on moisturiser and sunscreen if your makeup artist prefers a bare base.
  3. Carry blotting paper and a compact for touch-ups instead of adding more cream.
  4. If you are getting threading or a facial before the event, read face cream after facial, threading or waxing for how long to wait.

A cream helps skin feel comfortable and look smoother under makeup on the day. Changes in dark spots or tan take eight weeks or more of daily use with sunscreen, so start your routine well before the date.

5. At night: take it all off first

Makeup, sunscreen and sweat left on overnight can clog pores and make skin look dull. Remove makeup gently with a cleansing balm, oil or micellar water, then wash with a mild face wash. Once your face is clean and dry, apply a pea-sized amount of the cream as your night cream. Read Fair N Pink as a night cream for the night routine.

7. When to see a dermatologist

If you get itching, swelling, burning or a rash after a cream or makeup product, stop all new products and see a dermatologist. The same applies to frequent breakouts under makeup, which may need proper acne care rather than a change of cream.

FAQ

Should glutathione cream go before or after sunscreen?

Before. The cream goes on clean skin first, then sunscreen on top, then makeup.

How long should I wait after the cream before foundation?

Three to five minutes after the cream, and a few more minutes after sunscreen, until your skin no longer feels wet.

Can I mix the cream into my foundation?

It is better not to. Mixing changes the foundation's coverage and how it wears, and the cream spreads unevenly. Apply them as separate thin layers.

Can I use it as a primer?

It works as the moisturising step, not as a primer. If you want a smoother, longer-lasting base, add a primer after sunscreen.

Will the cream make my makeup look lighter or fairer?

No. It does not change your skin colour or your foundation shade; choose a foundation that matches your natural skin tone.
