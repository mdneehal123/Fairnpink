Can you use orange peel powder with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, as a separate step: apply a smooth orange peel powder paste on clean skin, rinse it off gently after about 10 minutes without scrubbing, pat dry, then apply the glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Patch test first and never mix it into the jar.

Yes, you can use orange peel powder with glutathione cream, but as two separate steps: mix a little powder into a smooth paste, apply it on clean skin, rinse it off gently after about 10 minutes, pat dry, then apply the cream. Do not scrub with it, do not mix it into the jar, and wear sunscreen every morning.

Santre ke chilke ka powder, made from dried orange peel, is a favourite Indian home pack, often mixed with besan, curd or rose water. It can leave skin feeling fresh and clean. It does not remove tan or change skin colour, and because the powder is a little gritty and the peel has natural oils, it can irritate some skin, so it needs a gentle hand.

2. The right order

  1. Patch test the paste on your inner arm or jawline for 15 minutes first, and the cream on the jaw for 2 to 3 days.
  2. Wash your face with a mild face wash and pat it dry.
  3. Mix half to 1 teaspoon of fine orange peel powder with a liquid from the table below into a smooth, soft paste. Sieve out coarse bits.
  4. Apply a thin layer on the face with fingertips, away from the eyes and lips, and leave it for about 10 minutes. Rinse before it dries hard.
  5. Rinse with plain water, pressing gently. Do not rub it off like a scrub.
  6. Pat dry and apply the glutathione cream as a thin layer.
  7. Sunscreen every morning, SPF 30 or higher.

Once a week is enough for most skin. Daily cream steps are on how to use the cream.

4. Mixes and habits to skip

  • Scrubbing with orange peel powder: rubbing a gritty powder often leaves tiny scratches and redness, and on brown skin irritation can leave darker patches, not lighter ones.
  • Orange peel with lemon: too harsh together. See lemon with glutathione cream.
  • Fresh orange peel rubbed on the face, or orange peel oil: these are stronger than the dried powder and more likely to sting.
  • Going in the sun with the pack on, or with citrus left on the skin: rinse fully before any sun.
  • Powder mixed into the cream jar: never. Powder and water let germs grow and change the cream.

5. Who should skip orange peel packs

  • Sensitive skin that reddens easily, or anyone who reacts to citrus; see glutathione cream for sensitive skin.
  • Very dry or flaky skin, common in north Indian winters. If you use it, mix it with milk and keep it short.
  • Active, inflamed pimples. Spreading a gritty pack over them can irritate them; see glutathione cream for pimple marks for the marks left afterwards.
  • Right after threading, waxing, bleach or a facial. Give the skin a day or two.

6. Orange peel for tan: an honest view

Orange peel is often shared as a "tan removal" or glow pack because oranges are linked with vitamin C. Dried peel powder in a 10 minute pack is not the same as a skincare product made to deliver vitamin C, and there is no reliable evidence that it removes tan. What helps tan look lighter over weeks is shade, covering up, sunscreen every morning and steady care; read glutathione cream for sun tan and how to reduce dark spots on face.

8. When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if your skin burns, swells, itches or breaks into a rash after an orange peel pack, if dark patches on the cheeks keep spreading (this can be melasma), or if pimples get worse. A home pack is not the answer for these.

FAQ

Can I apply glutathione cream after an orange peel face pack?

Yes. Rinse the pack off fully, pat your skin dry and apply a thin layer of the cream. Skip it that night if your skin stings or looks red.

Can I use orange peel powder as a daily scrub?

It is better not to. Daily scrubbing with a gritty powder irritates skin. Use it as a gentle wash-off pack once a week.

Can I mix orange peel powder into my glutathione cream?

No. Powder in the jar changes the cream and lets germs grow. Keep it as a separate step.

Is orange peel powder good for oily skin?

Many people with oily skin like how fresh it feels. The effect is short; a gentle face wash and a thin cream matter more.

Does orange peel powder with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Used with sunscreen, the cream can help tan and marks look lighter over weeks.
