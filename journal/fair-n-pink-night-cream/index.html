Can Fair N Pink be used as a night cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes. Fair N Pink Advance Radiance Cream is a day and night cream. At night, apply a pea-sized amount to clean skin and leave it on, and use sunscreen every morning.

Yes. Fair N Pink Advance Radiance Cream is a day and night cream, and many people use it mainly as a night cream. Night is a good time for it: your skin is clean, and the cream stays on for hours without sun, sweat or makeup.

How to use it at night

  1. Wash off the day's dirt, sweat and sunscreen with a mild cleanser.
  2. Pat your face dry.
  3. Take a pea-sized amount and massage it gently over face and neck.
  4. Leave it on overnight. Wash your face as usual in the morning.

Day cream or night cream: why one cream can do both

Night creams are often richer, and day creams often add sunscreen. Fair N Pink has no sunscreen in it, so it works the same way at either time: in the morning you add a separate sunscreen on top, and at night you simply leave it on. You do not need to buy a separate night cream to go with it.

When to apply it before bed

Apply it 15 to 20 minutes before you lie down, so a thin layer has time to settle and less of it ends up on your pillow. A thin layer is enough; a thick one does not work faster and can feel heavy by morning. Change your pillow cover often if you have acne-prone skin.

Is night-only use enough?

It can be. If your skin is oily, sensitive or new to the cream, using it only at night is a sensible way to start. If your skin is comfortable after a week, you can add a morning application under sunscreen.

What to do in the morning

Use sunscreen every morning, whether or not you apply the cream. Sun exposure during the day undoes what any brightening cream does at night.

Can you layer it with other night products?

Keep it simple. Use the cream as your last step. Avoid strong peels or scrubs on the same night, and if you use a prescription cream, ask your doctor how to combine them.

FAQ

Can I use Fair N Pink cream every night?

Yes. It is made for daily use. If your skin is sensitive, start every other night for the first week, then every night if it feels comfortable.

Should I wash it off in the morning?

Yes, wash your face as usual in the morning, then apply a fresh thin layer under sunscreen if you use it in the day too.

Can I apply rose water before it at night?

Yes. Let the rose water dry for a minute, then apply the cream. See rose water with glutathione cream.

Can I use it with another night cream?

You usually do not need two. If very dry skin wants more, a plain, fragrance-free moisturiser on top is enough; avoid adding another brightening cream on the same night.
