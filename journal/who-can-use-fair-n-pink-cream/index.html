Who can use Fair N Pink cream? Men, oily skin, dry skin and sensitive skin

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Men and women over 18 with oily, dry, combination or sensitive skin can use Fair N Pink Advance Radiance Cream after a patch test. Oily skin uses a thin layer, dry skin applies it on damp skin, and sensitive skin starts at night only.

Men and women over 18 with oily, dry, combination or sensitive skin can use Fair N Pink Advance Radiance Cream, after a 24-hour patch test. What changes from person to person is how much to apply and when; anyone pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18 or under treatment for a skin condition should ask a doctor first. Can men use it?

Yes. Skin is skin. Use the same pea-sized amount, morning and night. If you shave, wait a few minutes after shaving before you apply it, because freshly shaved skin stings more easily. On the beard area, spread a thin layer and work it in against the hair so it reaches the skin. Men who ride a bike or work outdoors get the most out of the morning sunscreen step. Oily skin

Use a very thin layer. If your face feels heavy or shiny by midday, keep the cream for night and use only sunscreen in the morning. Wash your face with a mild cleanser first so the cream goes on clean skin.

Dry skin

Apply it while your skin is still slightly damp after washing, which helps hold moisture in. In winter or in air conditioning you can add a plain moisturiser on top at night.

Combination skin

Use a normal amount on the cheeks and a lighter touch on the forehead and nose.

Sensitive skin

The cream contains fragrance, so a patch test matters more for you. Try it on your inner arm for 24 hours. Then start with once a day at night for the first week before moving to twice a day.

Who should ask a doctor first

  • Anyone pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Anyone under 18
  • Anyone with active acne, eczema, melasma or another skin condition under treatment

Summer, winter and the weather in between

The same skin can need a different routine through the year. In humid summer and the monsoon, oily and combination skin often feels better with a thinner layer, or with the cream at night only. In dry North Indian winters or long hours in air conditioning, even oily skin may welcome a plain moisturiser on top at night. Sunscreen stays every morning in every season, including cloudy days.

What it will not do on any skin type

Despite the word "Fair" in the name, it is a cosmetic cream that helps skin look brighter and more even, and tan and dark spots look lighter over weeks with daily sunscreen. It does not change the skin colour you were born with, on anyone.

FAQ

Can men use Fair N Pink cream?

Yes. It is not a cream for women only. Men use the same pea-sized amount morning and night, applied a few minutes after shaving, with sunscreen every morning.

Can teenagers use Fair N Pink cream?

We suggest it for adults. Under 18, a gentle face wash, a plain moisturiser and sunscreen are usually enough; ask a doctor before adding a brightening cream. See at what age can we use glutathione cream.

Is Fair N Pink cream good for sensitive skin?

Many people with sensitive skin can use it, but it contains fragrance, so patch test on your inner arm for 24 hours and start once a day at night. Stop if redness, itching or burning lasts.

Can I use it during pregnancy or breastfeeding?

Ask your doctor first. We do not suggest starting any new brightening cream during pregnancy or breastfeeding without medical advice.

Will it suit very dark or very light skin?

Yes. It is made for every skin tone. On any tone, the aim is a brighter, more even look, not a lighter skin colour.
