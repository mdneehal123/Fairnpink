Can you use glutathione cream with aloe vera gel?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, glutathione cream and aloe vera gel can be used together: apply the thinner aloe gel first, wait a minute or two, then the cream, with sunscreen every morning. Do not mix aloe into the jar, and patch test fresh plant gel first.

Yes, you can use glutathione cream with aloe vera gel: apply the thinner aloe gel first on clean skin, let it sink in for a minute or two, then apply the cream. Do not mix them together in the jar, and keep sunscreen every morning.

Aloe vera is in many Indian homes, either as a plant on the balcony or a tube of gel in the bathroom cupboard. It feels cooling and light, which makes it a natural partner for a cream in hot weather. Here is how to pair them sensibly.

2. The right order

  1. Wash with a gentle face wash and pat dry.
  2. Apply a thin layer of aloe vera gel, about the size of a coin for the whole face.
  3. Wait 1 to 2 minutes until it no longer feels wet or sticky.
  4. Apply a pea-sized amount of the glutathione cream and spread evenly.
  5. Morning only: finish with sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher. Read glutathione cream with sunscreen.

Thin, watery products go first and creams after, the same rule as in our guide to niacinamide and alpha arbutin. If the two pill or roll into little balls, use less of each and wait a little longer between them.

3. Fresh plant gel or packaged gel?

Fresh gel from the plantPackaged aloe gel
ProsFree, no added perfumeConsistent, preserved, easy to use
ConsThe yellow sap near the skin of the leaf can irritate; spoils within a day or twoSome contain fragrance, colour or alcohol; check the list
How to useCut a leaf, let the yellow sap drain for 10 minutes, scoop only the clear inner gel, keep any extra in the fridge and use it within a dayPick a simple, fragrance-free gel if your skin is sensitive

Whichever you use, patch test aloe on its own first. Some people are sensitive to aloe, especially the fresh sap.

5. Through the Indian year

  • Summer and monsoon: aloe plus a thin cream layer feels lighter than a cream alone in sticky weather.
  • After a day in the sun: cool aloe gel on its own that night is fine; go back to the cream once skin feels calm.
  • Winter: aloe alone can feel drying as it evaporates; keep it thin and follow with the cream and a moisturiser. See glutathione cream in winter.
  • After threading or waxing: plain aloe can feel soothing, but wait about a day before the glutathione cream. See face cream after facial, threading or waxing.

6. Mistakes to avoid

  • Mixing aloe into the cream jar. Water and fresh gel can spoil the cream. Mix a little in your palm if you like, but never in the jar.
  • Adding lemon, baking soda or toothpaste to aloe. These irritate and can leave skin patchy.
  • Using aloe instead of sunscreen. Aloe has no sun protection.
  • Expecting aloe to lighten spots. It can make skin feel soothed and look fresher, but spots and tan need steady sunscreen and time.

FAQ

Should I apply aloe vera gel before or after glutathione cream?

Before. Aloe gel is thinner, so it goes on first; wait a minute or two, then apply the cream.

Can I mix aloe vera gel and glutathione cream?

A little in your palm just before applying is fine, but never add aloe to the jar. Layering them separately usually works better.

Can I use fresh aloe vera from the plant with glutathione cream?

Yes, if you drain the yellow sap, use only the clear inner gel and patch test first. Keep fresh gel in the fridge and use it within a day.

Can I use aloe vera gel and glutathione cream every day?

Yes, if your skin is comfortable. Many people use aloe in the morning under sunscreen and the cream at night.

Will aloe vera with glutathione make my skin fair?

No. Neither changes the skin colour you were born with. Together they can help skin feel soothed and look brighter and more even over time.
