Can you use honey with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, as a separate step: spread a thin layer of plain honey on clean skin, rinse after 10 to 15 minutes, pat dry, then apply the glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Never mix honey into the jar, skip it with a bee-product allergy, and use it once or twice a week.

Yes, you can use honey with glutathione cream, but as two separate steps: spread a thin layer of plain honey on clean skin, rinse it off after 10 to 15 minutes, pat dry, then apply the cream. Do not stir honey into the jar, and do not leave it on overnight.

Shahad is one of the most loved Indian home masks, especially once the air turns dry in November and December. Honey is a humectant, which means it helps the skin hold on to water, so it can leave the face feeling softer and looking less dull for the evening. It is a comfort step, not a tan or spot fix. Here is how to fit it around your cream.

2. The right order

  1. Wash with a gentle face wash, especially if you wore sunscreen or makeup, and pat dry.
  2. Take about half a teaspoon of honey into a clean bowl with a dry spoon. Never dip your fingers into the honey jar.
  3. Spread a thin layer on the face with clean fingers, away from the eyes, eyebrows and hairline. A thick layer only drips.
  4. Leave it for 10 to 15 minutes, sitting somewhere cool so it does not run.
  5. Rinse with lukewarm water using soft circles until the skin no longer feels sticky, then pat dry.
  6. Apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream on the face and neck.
  7. Every morning: sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, as the last step. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

Once or twice a week is plenty. For other home steps before the cream, see raw milk with glutathione cream and glutathione cream with ubtan.

3. Why not mix honey into the cream

  • It can spoil the jar. Adding a food product and wet fingers brings in water and germs, and a 10 g jar has little room for that.
  • It changes the texture. The cream turns sticky, pulls on the skin and spreads unevenly.
  • It adds nothing useful. The cream works as it is made; honey belongs in a mask that you wash off.

5. Honey with milk, haldi or besan

Honey is often mixed with a little milk, curd, a pinch of haldi or besan into a pack. The rule stays the same: use the pack on clean skin, rinse it off before it dries hard, pat dry, then apply the cream. Keep haldi to a small pinch, as it can stain and sometimes irritates. Do not add lemon, sugar scrubs or toothpaste; lemon can sting and react badly with sun, and gritty scrubs before an active cream often cause redness. See lemon with glutathione cream for why lemon is best kept off the face.

6. Mistakes to avoid

  • Leaving honey on overnight. It sticks to the pillow, collects dust and can attract ants.
  • Using flavoured or syrup-type honey. Use plain honey with nothing added.
  • Rubbing hard to get it off. Lukewarm water and patience work better than scrubbing.
  • Skipping sunscreen because you used a home remedy. Tan and dark spots come back without daily sun protection.
  • Expecting change in a few days. Dark spots and tan fade slowly. See how long glutathione cream takes to work.

FAQ

Should I apply honey before or after glutathione cream?

Before, and rinse it off. Use it as a 10 to 15 minute mask, pat dry, then apply the cream.

Can I mix honey into glutathione cream?

No. It makes the cream sticky and can spoil the jar. Keep them as separate steps.

Can I leave honey on my face overnight?

It is better not to. Honey is sticky, transfers to the pillow and collects dust.

How often can I use a honey mask with glutathione cream?

Once or twice a week is enough for most people. The cream itself can be used as usual.

Does honey with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Honey helps skin feel softer; used with sunscreen, the cream can help skin look brighter and tan look lighter over weeks.
