Can you use tomato with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, as a separate step: dab fresh tomato juice or pulp on clean skin, rinse after 5 to 10 minutes, pat dry, then apply the glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Patch test first, never mix it into the jar, and do not expect it to remove tan.

Yes, you can use tomato with glutathione cream, but as two separate steps: dab a little fresh tomato pulp or juice on clean skin, rinse it off after 5 to 10 minutes, pat dry, then apply the cream. Do not mix tomato into the jar, do not rub tomato slices hard on your face, and patch test first, because tomato is acidic and can sting.

Rubbing half a tamatar on the face is one of the most shared Indian "tan removal" tips, and many people try it after a sunny trip or before a function. It can leave skin feeling fresh, but there is no reliable evidence that it removes tan, and it does not change skin colour. Here is how to use it sensibly around your cream.

2. The right order

  1. Patch test a little tomato juice on the side of the jaw for 10 minutes, rinse, and watch for 24 hours. Burning or redness means skip it.
  2. Wash with a gentle face wash and pat dry.
  3. Blend or mash a small ripe tomato and take a spoon of the pulp or strained juice into a clean bowl. Use it fresh; throw away what is left.
  4. Dab it on with clean fingers or a cotton pad, away from the eyes, lips and any open pimples. Do not rub a cut tomato on the face; the seeds and edges scratch.
  5. Rinse after 5 to 10 minutes with lukewarm water, or at once if it stings, and pat dry.
  6. Wait a few minutes, then apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream on the face and neck.
  7. Every morning: sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, as the last step. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

Once a week is enough. For other kitchen steps people use the same way, see glutathione cream with potato juice and raw milk with glutathione cream.

4. Why not mix tomato into the cream

  • It can spoil the jar. Fresh juice brings in water and germs, and a 10 g jar has little room for that.
  • It would sit on your skin all night. An acidic food left on for hours is far more likely to irritate than a short rinse-off step.
  • It adds nothing useful. The cream works as it is made.

5. Who should skip it

  • Sensitive or easily red skin. See glutathione cream for sensitive skin.
  • Skin that was just threaded, waxed, bleached or exfoliated, or that has open pimples or sunburn. See face cream after facial, threading or waxing.
  • Very dry winter skin that already feels tight; acid on dry skin often stings.
  • Anyone with a known tomato allergy.

6. Honest expectations about tan

Tan on the face and arms fades as skin renews itself and when you stop adding new sun. Tomato does not speed this up in any reliable way. What helps most is daily sunscreen, covering up in strong sun and steady use of a cream over weeks. See glutathione cream for sun tan and how long glutathione cream takes to work.

FAQ

Should I apply tomato before or after glutathione cream?

Before, and rinse it off. Use it for 5 to 10 minutes on clean skin, pat dry, then apply the cream.

Can I mix tomato juice into glutathione cream?

No. It can spoil the jar and would leave acidic juice on your skin all night. Keep them as separate steps.

Can I rub tomato on my face every day?

It is better not to. Once a week is enough; daily acidic juice often leaves skin dry or irritated.

Does tomato remove tan?

There is no reliable evidence that it does. Sunscreen every day and patience do more for tan than any kitchen remedy.

Does tomato with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Used with sunscreen, the cream can help skin look brighter and tan look lighter over weeks.
