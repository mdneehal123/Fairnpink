Alpha arbutin cream: what it does and how to use it

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Alpha arbutin cream is used to help dark spots and uneven tone look lighter. It is generally well tolerated and can be used morning or night; use it daily with sunscreen for 8 weeks or more before judging.

Alpha arbutin cream is used to help reduce the look of dark spots, marks and uneven tone. It works gradually, is generally well tolerated, and needs eight weeks or more of daily use with sunscreen.

What alpha arbutin is

Alpha arbutin is a skincare ingredient used in products for more even-looking skin. It is considered gentler than harsh options such as hydroquinone, which should only be used under a doctor's care. On labels you may also see plain "arbutin" or "beta arbutin": related ingredients, but alpha arbutin is the form most often chosen for face creams and serums.

A higher percentage on a serum does not mean faster results; daily use and sunscreen matter more. Using a cream and a serum with the same ingredient on the same night adds irritation, not speed.

How to apply it

  1. Patch test on your inner arm for 24 hours.
  2. Apply a pea-sized amount to clean skin, once a day at night for the first week.
  3. If your skin is comfortable, use it morning and night, with sunscreen every morning.

How long it takes

Four weeks for a first check, eight weeks or more for dark spots. Some marks fade, some do not.

Morning or night?

Both work. Alpha arbutin does not make skin more sensitive to the sun the way some acids do, so it can be used in the morning under sunscreen. Night is the easiest time to be consistent, because the cream stays on clean skin for hours. Fair N Pink is used morning and night.

What it pairs well with

Niacinamide and glutathione. Fair N Pink Advance Radiance Cream contains all three in one cream. Alpha arbutin can also be used with vitamin C and kojic acid; with retinol or exfoliating acids, use them on different nights at first.

Mistakes that slow it down

  • Skipping sunscreen: daily sun darkens marks faster than any cream fades them.
  • Picking pimples, which leaves new marks.
  • Rubbing in lemon, baking soda or harsh scrubs.
  • Switching products every two weeks before any can show a change.

Who should be careful

Anyone pregnant or breastfeeding, anyone under treatment for a skin condition, and anyone with very sensitive or fragrance-allergic skin should ask a doctor first. See alpha arbutin side effects.

FAQ

Can I use alpha arbutin cream every day?

Yes, once a patch test shows your skin is comfortable. It is generally well tolerated and is meant for daily use, with sunscreen every morning.

Does alpha arbutin remove dark spots permanently?

No cream removes spots permanently. Alpha arbutin can help them look lighter over weeks, and new spots can form with sun, pimples or friction, so sunscreen and gentle care keep the results going.

Can I use alpha arbutin cream on underarms or knees?

A face cream can be used on small areas like knees and elbows after a patch test. Underarm skin is thin and easily irritated by shaving and deodorant, so patch test there separately, and see a doctor for velvety, thick dark skin.

Is alpha arbutin safe in pregnancy?

Ask your doctor before using any brightening cream during pregnancy or breastfeeding.
