How to reduce dark spots on the face: a simple routine

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Dark spots usually come from sun exposure, old pimples or irritation. They fade slowly, and they fade faster when you stop the things that caused them. This routine is simple enough to keep up, which matters more than how many products are in it.

1. Sunscreen every morning

This is the step that decides everything else. Sunlight darkens existing spots and creates new ones. Use a broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning, including on cloudy days, and reapply if you are outdoors for long.

2. A gentle cleanser

Wash your face morning and night with a mild cleanser. Scrubbing hard or using harsh soaps irritates the skin, and irritated skin often leaves darker marks behind.

3. A cream with proven ingredients

Look for niacinamide and alpha arbutin, which are used to help skin look more even and to reduce the look of dark spots. Fair N Pink Advance Radiance Cream contains both, with glutathione. Apply a pea-sized amount twice a day.

4. Hands off

Do not pick or squeeze pimples. Most marks left after acne come from picking.

How long will it take?

Expect four to eight weeks before you can see a difference, and longer for older or deeper marks. Take a photo on day one in daylight and compare in the same light a month later. Some spots may not change with cosmetics at all.

What to avoid

  • Creams that promise results in days
  • Products with no ingredient list or no brand owner named on the pack
  • Home remedies such as lemon juice, which can irritate skin and make marks darker

If spots are spreading, changing shape or appeared suddenly, see a dermatologist.
