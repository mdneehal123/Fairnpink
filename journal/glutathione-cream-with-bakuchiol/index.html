Can you use bakuchiol with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes. At night, apply a thin bakuchiol serum on clean, dry skin, wait a minute, then a thin layer of glutathione cream. Start 2 or 3 nights a week after a patch test, and wear sunscreen every morning. Raw babchi oil is not the same; keep it off your face.

Yes, you can use bakuchiol with glutathione cream. Apply a thin bakuchiol serum first on clean, dry skin at night, wait a minute, then a thin layer of glutathione cream on top, and wear sunscreen every morning.

Bakuchiol is a plant compound taken from the seeds of the babchi plant (Psoralea corylifolia). In skincare it is sold as a gentler "plant alternative to retinol" for smoother-looking, more even-looking skin. It is a separate product: Fair N Pink Advance Radiance Cream has L-glutathione, niacinamide (vitamin B3) and alpha arbutin, and no bakuchiol or retinol.

If a bottle says "babchi oil" rather than bakuchiol, it is not the same thing. Do not put raw babchi oil on your face. For retinol specifically, read glutathione cream with retinol.

2. The right order at night

  1. Wash your face with a gentle face wash and pat dry fully.
  2. Bakuchiol serum: two or three drops for the whole face, pressed in.
  3. Wait about a minute so it settles.
  4. Glutathione cream: a thin layer over the face and neck. If your skin is dry, add a plain moisturiser on top; see glutathione cream and moisturiser.
  5. Every morning: gentle wash, a thin layer of cream or moisturiser, then broad-spectrum sunscreen of SPF 30 or more.

If your bakuchiol comes as a thick cream, use it on alternate nights instead of layering two creams on top of each other.

4. Who it suits, and who should skip it

  • Normal, combination and oily skin: a light bakuchiol serum under a thin cream usually sits well, even in humid weather. See combination skin.
  • Dry skin: add a moisturiser on top, especially in winter; read glutathione cream in winter.
  • Already using retinol, glycolic or salicylic acid: you do not need bakuchiol too. Too many active steps at once is a common cause of redness.
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding: ask your doctor before starting any new active product.

5. What to expect, honestly

Bakuchiol is used for smoother-looking skin and a more even look over many weeks. It does not change your natural skin colour, and it does not remove tan or dark spots on its own. With glutathione, niacinamide and alpha arbutin at night and sunscreen every morning, marks can look lighter slowly, over weeks to months. For the timeline of the cream, read how long glutathione cream takes to work.

6. Signs to slow down

  • Stinging that lasts more than a few minutes, redness or itching: stop the bakuchiol for a few nights, then try fewer nights a week.
  • New small bumps: the serum or the layering may be too heavy for you; try alternate nights.
  • Rash or swelling: stop both products and see a dermatologist.

7. When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist for painful or cystic acne, patches on both cheeks that may be melasma, a rash after any product, or before starting actives in pregnancy. Patch test any new product first.

FAQ

Can I use bakuchiol and glutathione cream together every night?

Many people can after a few weeks of building up. Start with 2 or 3 nights a week and increase only if your skin stays comfortable.

Which goes first, bakuchiol or glutathione cream?

A thin bakuchiol serum goes first, then the cream. If both are creams, use them on alternate nights.

Can I use bakuchiol in the morning?

Night is simpler. If you use it in the morning, always follow with sunscreen.

Is babchi oil the same as bakuchiol?

No. Raw babchi seed oil can irritate and make skin more sensitive to sun. Do not apply it to your face without a doctor's advice.

Does glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. It does not change your natural skin colour. With sunscreen, it can help tan and dark marks look lighter over weeks.
