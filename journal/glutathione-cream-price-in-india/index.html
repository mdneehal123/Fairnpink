Glutathione cream price in India: what you are paying for

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Glutathione face creams in India cost from a few hundred to several thousand rupees; compare the net weight, the full ingredient list and who sells it. Fair N Pink Advance Radiance Cream, 10 g with niacinamide and alpha arbutin, costs ₹999.

Glutathione face creams in India are sold at very different prices, from a few hundred rupees to several thousand. The name on the front is the same, but what is in the jar and who stands behind it are not.

What decides the price

  • Net weight. Jars range from small to large. Always check the grams before you compare two prices.
  • The other ingredients. A cream that also contains niacinamide and alpha arbutin costs more to make than one with glutathione alone.
  • Packaging. Heavy jars and boxes add to the cost.
  • The seller. Buying from the brand costs what the brand sets. Resellers add or cut margins.

How to compare fairly

  1. Read the full ingredient list, not only the front of the box.
  2. Check the net weight and how long a jar will last you.
  3. Check that the brand owner is named on the pack and can be contacted.
  4. Be careful with very cheap creams that carry no ingredient list at all.
