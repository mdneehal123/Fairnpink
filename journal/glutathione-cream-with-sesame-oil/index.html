Can you use sesame oil (til ka tel) with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, on dry skin: apply the glutathione cream first, wait a few minutes, then press one or two drops of plain cold-pressed sesame oil on dry areas at night, with sunscreen every morning. Oily and acne-prone faces usually skip it; avoid it if you are allergic to sesame.

Yes, you can use sesame oil with glutathione cream if your skin is dry: apply the cream first on clean skin, wait a few minutes, then press one or two drops of plain sesame oil over dry areas at night, with sunscreen every morning. Oily and acne-prone faces usually do better without it, and the oil should never be mixed into the jar.

Sesame oil, or til ka tel, is used in many Indian homes for winter body massage and in Ayurvedic abhyanga. It is a rich oil that helps seal moisture into dry skin. It does not brighten skin or change your natural skin colour; any glow after a massage is from moisture and warmth, and it fades.

2. How to use sesame oil with the cream on your face

  1. Patch test first: a drop of oil on your jawline for 2 to 3 nights.
  2. Wash your face with a mild face wash and pat it dry.
  3. Apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream in a thin layer.
  4. Wait 5 to 10 minutes for it to settle.
  5. Warm one or two drops of sesame oil between your fingertips and press them over dry areas such as the cheeks and jawline. Skip an oily forehead and nose.
  6. Next morning: face wash, then sunscreen. Keep oil off your face in the day; it can make sunscreen slide and feel greasy.

Oil goes last because it seals in what is underneath. For the cream steps, see how to use the cream.

3. Winter body massage and abhyanga

Many people use til ka tel for a warm oil massage before a bath in winter. That works well alongside the cream:

  • Body: massage warm (not hot) oil into arms, legs, elbows and knees before your bath, then wash with a mild soap and lukewarm water.
  • Face: if you oil your face too, wash it off in the bath, pat dry and apply the cream afterwards on clean skin.
  • Do not heat the oil too much. Test it on your wrist first; hot oil can burn.
  • Hair oiling: keep heavy hair oil away from the forehead overnight, as it can lead to bumps along the hairline.

For dry winter routines, read glutathione cream in winter and glutathione cream for dry skin.

4. Cold-pressed oil vs cooking oil

  • Choose plain, cold-pressed sesame oil (often sold as kachi ghani or wood-pressed) with no added fragrance.
  • Do not use toasted sesame oil, the dark brown, strong-smelling kind used for cooking; it is not meant for skin.
  • Do not reuse frying oil. Old, heated oil can irritate skin.
  • Smell it before use. A sour or sharp smell means it has gone rancid. Keep the bottle closed and away from heat.

5. Who should skip it

  • Oily or acne-prone skin: sesame is a heavy oil for the face, especially in humid weather. A thin layer of cream is enough; see glutathione cream for oily skin and acne-prone skin.
  • Sesame allergy: sesame is a common food allergen. If you react to sesame in food, do not put it on your skin.
  • Freshly waxed, threaded or bleached skin: wait a day or two; see face cream after facial, threading and waxing.
  • Summer and monsoon: most faces do not need any oil on top in hot, sticky months.

6. Sesame vs other oils

Sesame is richer than jojoba and lighter-smelling than mustard oil. You only need one oil, not several. Compare coconut oil, almond oil and mustard oil with glutathione cream if you are choosing one for winter.

8. When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if oil leads to new bumps or pimples that do not settle, if you get itching, rash or swelling, or if you have patches of darkness that keep spreading. Patch test any new oil first.

FAQ

Should I apply sesame oil before or after glutathione cream?

After. Apply the cream on clean, dry skin first, then a drop or two of oil on top at night.

Can I use sesame oil on my face every night?

Only very dry skin usually needs it, and mostly in winter. Many people need it just a few nights a week, on the cheeks.

Can I mix til ka tel into the cream jar?

No. It changes the texture and can spoil the cream sooner. Layer it on top instead.

Is sesame oil good for oily skin?

Usually not on the face. It is a rich oil; oily skin does better with a thin layer of cream alone.

Does sesame oil with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. With sunscreen, the cream can help tan and marks look lighter over weeks.
