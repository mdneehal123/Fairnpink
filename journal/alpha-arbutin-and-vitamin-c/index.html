Can you use alpha arbutin and vitamin C together?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, alpha arbutin and vitamin C can be used together: a vitamin C serum in the morning under sunscreen and an alpha arbutin cream after it or at night. Layer thinnest first, patch test, and judge dark spots after eight weeks or more.

Yes, alpha arbutin and vitamin C can be used together, and many people use them in the same routine for more even-looking skin. The simplest way is a vitamin C serum in the morning under sunscreen and an alpha arbutin cream at night, or both in the morning with the thinner product first.

Why the pair is popular

Both ingredients are used for the look of dark spots, marks and dullness, but they do different jobs. Alpha arbutin is used to help reduce the look of dark spots and uneven tone and is generally gentle. Vitamin C is an antioxidant that helps skin look fresher and supports your sunscreen against daily sun and pollution. Neither one changes your natural skin colour; together they simply help the skin you have look brighter and more even over weeks.

Which goes first?

Layer from thinnest to thickest. A watery vitamin C serum goes on first, then a cream with alpha arbutin, then sunscreen in the morning. Let each layer settle for a minute or two. You do not need to wait longer, and there is no need to mix them in your palm.

A simple routine using both

  1. Patch test each product on your inner arm for 24 hours before using it on your face.
  2. Morning: wash with a mild face wash, pat dry, apply a few drops of vitamin C serum, let it settle, then a pea-sized amount of your alpha arbutin cream, and finish with sunscreen.
  3. Night: wash off sunscreen and sweat, then apply only the alpha arbutin cream. This keeps nights gentle.
  4. Start slowly with vitamin C if you are new to it: every other morning for the first two weeks.
  5. Check at four weeks, and judge dark spots at eight weeks or more.

Who should be careful

  • Sensitive skin or rosacea: strong vitamin C serums can sting. Try a gentler one, use it on alternate days, or skip it.
  • Using retinol or exfoliating acids: keep vitamin C for the morning and those for the night, not stacked together. See using a glutathione cream with retinol.
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding, or under care for a skin condition: ask your doctor before adding new actives.
  • Allergic to perfumed products: check labels; our cream contains fragrance.

Mistakes that hold back results

  • Skipping sunscreen. Daily sun keeps creating new tan and marks, which undoes the work of both ingredients. Read why sunscreen matters.
  • Using an old, darkened vitamin C serum. If it has turned dark orange or brown, it has likely oxidised; replace it.
  • Adding lemon, toothpaste or baking soda. These home hacks can irritate skin and leave marks looking worse.
  • Changing products every week. Give one routine at least eight weeks before you decide.
  • Keeping serums in a hot bathroom. In Indian summers, store skincare in a cool, shaded spot with lids closed.

What to expect

Over the first few weeks, skin may look fresher and feel smoother. Dark spots, if they respond, fade slowly over two to three months; some marks change little. Melasma and patches that spread or itch need a dermatologist, not just a cosmetic routine. For more on spots, see how to reduce dark spots on the face.

FAQ

Can I mix alpha arbutin and vitamin C in my palm?

It is better to layer them, serum first and cream second. Mixing can dilute both unevenly.

Can I use alpha arbutin and vitamin C at night?

You can, but vitamin C is usually more useful in the morning under sunscreen. A gentle night routine with only your alpha arbutin cream is easier on skin.

Is alpha arbutin better than vitamin C for dark spots?

They are not competitors. Alpha arbutin is aimed at the look of dark spots; vitamin C is mainly an antioxidant. Many routines use both. Read more in our alpha arbutin cream guide.

Can I use niacinamide too?

Yes. Niacinamide, alpha arbutin and vitamin C are commonly used in the same routine. See niacinamide and alpha arbutin together.
