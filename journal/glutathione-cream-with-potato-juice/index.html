Can you use potato juice with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, as a separate step: dab fresh potato juice on clean skin, rinse after 10 to 15 minutes, pat dry, then apply the glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Never mix it into the jar; potato juice alone has little evidence for fading tan or spots.

Yes, you can use potato juice with glutathione cream, but as two separate steps: dab fresh potato juice on clean skin, rinse it off after 10 to 15 minutes, pat dry, then apply the cream. Do not mix potato juice into the jar, and do not expect it to fade tan or spots on its own.

Aloo ka ras is one of the most shared Indian home tips for tan and dark circles. There is little good evidence that it changes either, but as a cool, short wash-off step it is harmless for most skin. Here is how to fit it around your cream sensibly.

2. The right order

  1. Wash with a gentle face wash and pat dry.
  2. Make fresh juice: wash and peel one small potato, grate it, and squeeze the juice through a clean cloth into a clean bowl.
  3. Dab it on with a cotton pad, avoiding the eyes and lips.
  4. Leave it for 10 to 15 minutes, then rinse with plain water and pat dry.
  5. Apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream on the face and neck.
  6. Every morning: sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, as the last step. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

Two or three times a week is plenty. Throw away any leftover juice; it turns brown and starts to spoil within hours. For other home steps before the cream, see rose water with glutathione cream and glutathione cream with ubtan.

3. Why not mix it into the cream

  • It can spoil the jar. Fresh juice brings water, starch and germs into a cream that is meant to stay clean.
  • It changes the texture. The cream turns runny and uneven.
  • It does not add anything useful. The cream works as made; adding a kitchen juice only makes it less predictable.

4. Under-eye use

Many people put cold potato slices on their eyes. A cool slice can make the area feel fresher, much like any cold compress, but it does not change hollows, thin skin or shadows. Keep raw juice away from the eyes themselves. Our guide to glutathione cream for dark circles explains which kinds of circles a cream may help, and which it cannot.

6. Mistakes to avoid

  • Adding lemon to the potato juice. Lemon can irritate skin, especially before going into the sun.
  • Using green or sprouted potatoes. Use a fresh, firm potato and cut away any green parts.
  • Leaving it on overnight. It dries, feels tight and can smell.
  • Skipping sunscreen because you used a home remedy. Tan comes back without daily sun protection. See glutathione cream for sun tan.
  • Expecting results in a few days. Dark spots and tan fade slowly. See how to reduce dark spots on face.

FAQ

Should I apply potato juice before or after glutathione cream?

Before, and rinse it off. Use it as a 10 to 15 minute wash-off step, pat dry, then apply the cream.

Can I mix potato juice with glutathione cream?

No. Keep them separate. Mixing can spoil the cream and makes it runny.

Does potato juice remove tan?

There is little good evidence for it. Tan looks lighter over weeks mainly with daily sunscreen and less sun; a cream like ours can help it look more even.

Does potato juice with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. The cream can help skin look brighter and more even over weeks with sunscreen.

How often can I use potato juice on my face?

Two or three times a week is enough. Make it fresh each time and throw away what is left.
