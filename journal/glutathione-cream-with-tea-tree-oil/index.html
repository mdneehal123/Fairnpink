Can you use tea tree oil with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, as a spot step: apply the glutathione cream on the face, then dab tea tree oil diluted in aloe gel or a light oil only on a pimple, with sunscreen every morning. Never use it neat, never mix it into the jar, and skip it on sensitive skin.

Yes, you can use tea tree oil with glutathione cream, but only as a diluted spot step: apply the cream on your face, then dab a little diluted tea tree oil only on a pimple. Never put neat tea tree oil on your skin, never mix it into the jar, and wear sunscreen every morning.

Tea tree oil is an essential oil that many people in India keep for pimples, often after seeing it in face washes and spot gels. It is strong, and it is one of the essential oils most often linked with skin irritation and allergy, especially when used undiluted or when the bottle is old. Used carefully, it can sit next to a gentle glutathione cream without trouble.

2. How to dilute tea tree oil

  1. Buy pure tea tree oil in a small dark glass bottle with a dropper, and check the expiry date.
  2. Mix 1 drop of tea tree oil with about a teaspoon of plain aloe vera gel or a light carrier oil, in a clean small bowl or the cap of a bottle.
  3. Patch test a little of the mix behind your ear or on your jawline once a day for 2 days.
  4. Make a tiny fresh batch each time, or keep a pre-mixed bottle tightly capped and away from light.

Tea tree oil that is old or left open to air and sunlight is more likely to irritate. If it smells different from when you bought it, throw it away.

3. The right order with the cream

  1. Wash your face with a mild face wash and pat it dry.
  2. Apply a thin layer of the glutathione cream on the face and neck, going around the pimple rather than over it.
  3. Wait a few minutes for the cream to settle.
  4. Dab the diluted tea tree mix only on the pimple with a clean cotton bud, once a day at night.
  5. Do not cover the whole face with tea tree oil, and keep it away from the eyes, lips and nostrils.
  6. Sunscreen every morning, SPF 30 or higher, so the mark left behind does not darken in the sun.

5. Who should skip tea tree oil

  • Sensitive, dry or eczema-prone skin; see glutathione cream for sensitive skin.
  • Anyone who has had a rash from tea tree oil, other essential oils or fragranced products.
  • Skin that is broken, peeling or freshly threaded, waxed or bleached.
  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding: ask your doctor before using essential oils on the skin.
  • Children: keep the bottle out of reach. Tea tree oil is harmful if swallowed.

6. Pimple marks: an honest view

Tea tree oil is a spot step for a pimple, not a fix for the flat brown or dark marks pimples leave behind on Indian skin. Those marks fade slowly, over weeks, with sunscreen every morning, no picking, and steady care. 8. When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist for painful, deep or frequent pimples, pimples that leave pits or scars, or acne that keeps coming back along the jaw with irregular periods. Stop tea tree oil and get help if your skin turns red, swollen, itchy or blistered.

FAQ

Can I apply glutathione cream after tea tree oil?

Apply the cream first on the face, then dab diluted tea tree oil only on the pimple. That way the oil stays on the spot.

Can I mix tea tree oil into my glutathione cream?

No. It changes the cream and makes the whole face more likely to sting. Keep it as a separate spot step.

Can I put tea tree oil directly on a pimple?

Not neat. Always dilute it first, about 1 drop in a teaspoon of aloe gel or a light oil, and patch test.

How long does tea tree oil take on a pimple?

It varies, and some pimples settle on their own in a few days anyway. If a pimple is painful or lasts more than a couple of weeks, see a dermatologist.

Does tea tree oil with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Used with sunscreen, the cream can help tan and marks look lighter over weeks.
