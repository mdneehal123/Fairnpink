Glutathione cream for a dark nose and dark sides of the nose

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

A glutathione cream can help a dark nose, its sides or the bridge look a little lighter and more even over 8 to 12 weeks with a thin daily layer, sunscreen on the nose every morning and no squeezing, scrubbing or rubbing. A spreading patch across the cheeks needs a dermatologist.

A glutathione cream can help a dark nose, dark sides of the nose or a dark bridge look a little lighter and more even over 8 to 12 weeks, if you use a thin layer daily, wear sunscreen on the nose every morning and stop the rubbing and picking that cause most of the darkness. It will not change your natural skin colour, and the nose is often naturally a shade deeper than the cheeks.

The nose sticks out, so it gets the most sun on the face. It is also where spectacles sit, where we wipe and blow during colds, and where many people squeeze blackheads. All of these can leave darker, rougher skin over time. Fix the cause first and any cream has a much better chance.

2. How to use the cream on the nose

  1. Wash your face with a mild face wash and lukewarm water. Pat dry gently.
  2. Take a pea-sized amount for the whole face and neck, as usual. Put a light dot on the nose, its sides and the bridge.
  3. Spread a thin layer with your fingertip. Do not rub hard; the skin beside the nostrils is thin.
  4. Keep it out of the nostrils and off the inner corner of the eyes.
  5. Morning: sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, with an extra dab on the nose. The nose is the first place sunscreen wears off.
  6. Be patient. Look for change over 8 to 12 weeks, not days.

3. What to stop doing

  • Squeezing blackheads with nails or tools; it leaves dark marks and enlarged pores.
  • Daily scrubs, pore strips or lemon on the nose; see lemon with glutathione cream.
  • Toothpaste, baking soda or bleach on dark patches; they irritate and can make them darker.
  • Rubbing the nose with rough towels or handkerchiefs.
  • Skipping sunscreen because the nose "is small". It is the most sun-exposed spot on the face.

4. Oily nose vs dry nose sides

Many people have an oily nose tip with dry, flaky skin right beside the nostrils. For the oily tip, a very thin layer of cream and a gel sunscreen work well; see glutathione cream for oily skin. For dry sides, apply the cream on slightly damp skin and add a plain moisturiser on top at night in winter; see glutathione cream for dry skin.

7. When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if the darkness spreads across the cheeks and nose in a butterfly or patchy shape (this can be melasma), if the sides of the nose are red, greasy and flaky for weeks, if a spot on the nose changes in size or colour, bleeds or does not heal, or if a cream leaves your skin red, burning or itchy.

FAQ

How can I lighten the dark sides of my nose?

Use sunscreen on the nose daily, stop squeezing and rubbing, moisturise dry patches and use a brightening cream in a thin layer. With steady care the area can look more even over 8 to 12 weeks.

Can I apply glutathione cream on my nose daily?

Yes, a thin layer as part of your normal face routine, kept out of the nostrils. Stop if it stings or turns red.

Why is my nose darker than my face?

Most often sun, because the nose sticks out, plus friction from glasses, rubbing or squeezing. A slightly deeper shade on the nose is also normal for many people.

Do spectacle marks on the nose go away?

They usually fade slowly once pressure from the pads drops. Check the fit of your frames, use soft pads and moisturise the area.

Can glutathione cream make my nose fair?

No. It does not change your natural skin colour. With sunscreen, it can help darker patches from sun and rubbing look lighter and more even over weeks.
