Can you use oats with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes. Make a soft, rinse-off pack with finely ground plain oats and curd, milk or rose water, leave it on for about 10 minutes, rinse, pat dry and then apply the cream. Use the pack once or twice a week and never mix oats into the jar.

Yes, you can use oats with glutathione cream: make a soft, rinse-off oats pack, leave it on for about 10 minutes, rinse, pat dry and then apply the cream. The pack goes first and is washed off; never mix oats into the cream jar.

Oats (jai) have long been used in home packs and baths for dry, itchy or tight skin, because finely ground oats turn soft and creamy with water and feel soothing. An oats pack is a comfort step, especially in winter. It does not fade dark spots or tan by itself, and it does not change your natural skin colour.

2. A simple oats face pack

  1. Grind 1 to 2 teaspoons of plain rolled oats into a fine powder in a dry mixer jar. Plain oats only: not masala, flavoured or sugary instant oats.
  2. Mix with enough liquid to make a soft paste: plain curd or milk for dry skin, rose water or plain water for oily skin. A few drops of honey are optional for dry skin.
  3. Let it sit for 2 to 3 minutes so the oats soften.
  4. Wash your face with a mild face wash.
  5. Apply a thin layer, avoiding the eyes and lips.
  6. Leave it on for about 10 minutes; rinse before it dries hard.
  7. Rinse with lukewarm water using soft circles of the fingertips. Do not scrub.

Make a fresh pack each time and throw away leftovers; a paste made with curd or milk spoils quickly. For other kitchen packs that sit well with the cream, read glutathione cream with curd, glutathione cream with honey and glutathione cream with besan.

3. The right order on pack nights

  1. Oats pack, rinsed off.
  2. Pat the face dry with a clean towel and wait 2 to 3 minutes.
  3. Apply a thin layer of the glutathione cream on the face and neck.
  4. Add a plain moisturiser on top if your skin still feels dry.
  5. Next morning: sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, every day.

For the daily steps, see how to use the cream and glutathione cream with sunscreen.

5. Oats in winter and after sun

From November to February, cold air, hot baths and heaters leave many faces tight and flaky, especially around the nose and on the cheeks. An oats and curd pack on a Sunday night, followed by the cream and a moisturiser, is a simple way to make winter skin feel comfortable. In summer, after a day outdoors, a cool oats and rose water pack can feel soothing on warm, tanned skin. Tan still fades slowly over weeks with sunscreen and shade; read glutathione cream for sun tan for an honest plan.

6. What not to do

  • Do not mix oats into the cream jar. It spoils the cream and you cannot rinse it off.
  • Do not use oats as a hard scrub. Coarse pieces rubbed hard can leave skin red. Grind them fine and use soft circles.
  • Do not add lemon, baking soda or toothpaste. They sting and dry the skin; see lemon with glutathione cream.
  • Do not use the pack every day. Once or twice a week is enough.
  • Do not skip sunscreen and expect the pack to fade tan.

8. When to see a dermatologist

Stop and see a dermatologist if your skin turns red, itchy or swollen after the pack or the cream, if dryness comes with cracks or scaling that does not settle, or if dark patches keep spreading. Patch test the pack on the jawline for 10 minutes first and watch for 24 hours.

FAQ

Can I apply glutathione cream after an oats face pack?

Yes. Rinse the pack off, pat the face dry, wait a couple of minutes and then apply a thin layer of the cream.

Can I mix oats powder into glutathione cream?

No. Keep the cream jar clean. Use oats only as a rinse-off pack and apply the cream separately.

How often can I use an oats pack?

Once or twice a week is plenty for most skin types.

Which oats should I use on the face?

Plain rolled oats ground to a fine powder. Avoid flavoured, masala or sugary instant oats.

Can an oats pack make skin fair?

No. It helps skin feel soft and calm but does not change your natural skin colour. Used with sunscreen, the cream can help tan and marks look lighter over weeks.
