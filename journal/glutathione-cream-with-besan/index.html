Can you use besan with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, as a separate step: apply a thin besan pack on clean skin, rinse it off gently after about 10 minutes before it dries hard, pat dry, then apply the glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Never mix besan into the jar, and do not expect it to remove tan.

Yes, you can use besan with glutathione cream, but as two separate steps: apply a thin besan pack on clean skin, rinse it off gently after about 10 minutes, before it dries hard, pat dry, then apply the cream. Do not mix besan into the jar, do not scrub with it, and wear sunscreen every morning, because that is what does most of the work on tan.

Besan (gram flour) is the base of most Indian home ubtans and face packs, used before weddings, festivals and after sunny trips. A gentle besan pack can leave skin feeling clean and soft. It does not remove tan or change skin colour, but it fits well as a weekly step next to a cream.

1. The right order

  1. Patch test the besan mix on your inner arm or jawline for 15 minutes first, and the cream on the jaw for 2 to 3 days.
  2. Wash your face with a mild face wash and pat it dry.
  3. Mix 1 to 2 teaspoons of besan with a liquid from the table below into a smooth, soft paste. No lumps.
  4. Apply a thin layer on the face, away from the eyes and lips, and leave it for about 10 minutes. Rinse before it cracks.
  5. Rinse with plain water using soft, circular fingertips. Do not rub it off like a scrub.
  6. Pat dry and apply the glutathione cream as a thin layer.
  7. Sunscreen every morning, SPF 30 or higher.

Once or twice a week is enough. For a full ubtan routine, read glutathione cream with ubtan.

3. Mixes to skip

  • Besan with lemon: too acidic and drying on the face. See lemon with glutathione cream.
  • Besan with baking soda or toothpaste: these can burn and irritate.
  • Besan with sugar or coarse powder as a daily scrub: rubbing often makes darkness look worse, not better.
  • Besan mixed into the cream jar: never. Powder and water let germs grow and change the cream.

4. Who should skip besan packs

  • Very dry or flaky skin, common in north Indian winters. Besan absorbs oil and can leave dry skin tighter. If you do use it, mix it with milk and keep it short. See glutathione cream for dry skin.
  • Sensitive skin that reddens easily. Patch test first, or skip packs; see glutathione cream for sensitive skin.
  • Active, inflamed pimples. Spreading and rinsing a pack over them can irritate them.
  • Right after threading, waxing, bleach or a facial. Give the skin a day or two.
  • Anyone allergic to chickpeas or gram.

5. Besan for tan: an honest view

Besan packs are popular after summer trips, and skin often feels smoother afterwards. That comes mostly from washing and a little gentle cleansing. There is no reliable evidence that besan removes tan. What helps tan look lighter over weeks is shade, covering up, sunscreen every morning and steady care; read glutathione cream for sun tan.

7. When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if your skin burns, swells, itches or breaks into a rash after a besan pack, if dark patches on the cheeks keep spreading (this can be melasma), or if pimples get worse. A home pack is not the answer for these.

FAQ

Can I apply glutathione cream after a besan face pack?

Yes. Rinse the pack off fully, pat your skin dry, and apply a thin layer of the cream. Skip it that night if your skin stings or feels tight.

Can I mix besan into my glutathione cream?

No. Powder and water in the jar let germs grow and change the texture. Use besan only as a separate wash-off pack.

How often can I use a besan pack?

Once or twice a week is enough for most skin. Dry or sensitive skin may do better with once a week or less.

Is besan good for oily skin?

Many people with oily skin like how clean it feels, because besan soaks up oil. It does not control oil for long, so a gentle face wash and a thin cream matter more.

Does besan with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Used with sunscreen, the cream can help tan and marks look lighter over weeks.
