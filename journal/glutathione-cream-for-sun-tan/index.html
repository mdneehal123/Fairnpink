Glutathione cream for sun tan: what it can do and a simple routine

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

A glutathione cream can help sun tan look lighter over a few weeks when used with sunscreen every morning and less time in strong sun. It does not take tan away overnight, protect you from the sun or change your natural skin colour.

A glutathione cream can help sun tan look lighter over a few weeks, but only alongside daily sunscreen and less time in strong sun. Tan is your skin's response to sunlight, so stopping new tan every morning matters more than anything you apply at night.

In India, tan builds on bike rides, school runs, cricket grounds, beach trips and even through car windows, and it shows most on the face, neck, arms and feet. Here is what a glutathione cream can and cannot do for tan, and a routine that fits real Indian weather.

1. What sun tan is, in plain words

When UV rays reach the skin, it makes more melanin, the pigment that gives skin its colour, as a natural shield. That extra pigment is the tan. Skin slowly sheds and renews its top layers over about a month, so a tan usually fades on its own over weeks once sun exposure drops, as long as you are not tanning again every day.

That is the key point: tan is temporary and is different from your natural skin colour. No cream changes the skin colour you were born with, and any product that promises to make you "fair" or "gora" is making a claim you should not trust. Read our note on the name for more on this.

3. A simple tan routine

Morning

  1. Wash with a gentle face wash and pat dry.
  2. Apply a pea-sized amount of cream on the face and neck.
  3. Apply sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, about two finger-lengths for face and neck. This is the most important step.
  4. Reapply sunscreen every 2 to 3 hours if you are outdoors, after sweating, or after wiping your face.

Night

  1. Wash off sunscreen, sweat and dust.
  2. Apply a pea-sized amount of cream and let it settle.
  3. Add a plain moisturiser on top if your skin feels dry, especially in winter.

More on choosing and layering sunscreen is in glutathione cream with sunscreen.

4. Simple sun habits that do more than any cream

  • Cover up: a cap or wide hat, a cotton dupatta or scarf, full sleeves and sunglasses.
  • Plan outdoor time: the sun is strongest from late morning to mid-afternoon.
  • Bike and scooter riders: a full-face helmet or a scarf across the face, plus sunscreen on the neck and hands.
  • Do not forget winter and cloudy days. UV still reaches the skin in winter, on hazy days and at hill stations.
  • Beach, pool and hill trips: water, sand and snow reflect sunlight, so reapply often.

5. What to expect, honestly

Results differ for everyone and depend most on how much sun you still get. Our guide on how long glutathione cream takes to work explains the timelines in more detail.

6. Mistakes to avoid

  • Lemon, baking soda or bleach on tan. They can irritate the skin and leave it patchier.
  • Hard scrubbing. Rough scrubs do not lift tan and can leave dark marks on Indian skin.
  • Skipping sunscreen. Without it, you add new tan every day and no cream can keep up.
  • Using many new products at once. If skin reacts, you will not know which one caused it.
  • Applying cream on sunburnt skin. If skin is red, hot or peeling, use only a plain moisturiser until it settles.

FAQ

Can glutathione cream remove sun tan?

It can help tan look lighter over weeks, used with sunscreen every morning. It does not take tan away overnight, and it is not a replacement for sunscreen.

How long does sun tan take to fade?

Light tan often looks lighter within a few weeks once you protect your skin from the sun. Deeper tan can take a couple of months, and results differ for everyone.

Should I use glutathione cream in the morning or at night for tan?

Both work. In the morning, always follow it with sunscreen. At night, apply it on clean skin after washing off the day.

Can I use glutathione cream on tanned hands and arms?

A face cream comes in a small jar, so it is not practical for large areas. For hands and arms, sunscreen and covering up help most.

Will glutathione cream make me fair?

No. It can help tan and dark spots look lighter and skin look more even, but it does not change your natural skin colour.
