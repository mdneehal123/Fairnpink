Glutathione cream for pimple marks: what helps, what does not, and how long it takes

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

A cream with glutathione, niacinamide and alpha arbutin can help flat dark pimple marks look lighter over 8 to 12 weeks or more with daily sunscreen. It does not help pitted or raised scars and is not an acne treatment; see a dermatologist for those.

A glutathione cream with niacinamide and alpha arbutin can help the brown or dark marks left after pimples look lighter, gradually, over eight to twelve weeks or more, as long as you wear sunscreen every morning. It will not help pitted scars, and it is not a treatment for active acne.

Most "pimple marks" on Indian skin are not true scars at all. They are flat dark patches where the skin made extra pigment after a pimple healed. These fade with time and sun protection, and a good cream can help that along. Knowing which kind of mark you have saves you money and disappointment.

1. First, check which kind of mark you have

What you seeWhat it usually isWhat helps
Flat brown, dark or black patchDark mark (extra pigment after a pimple)Sunscreen daily, a cream with niacinamide or alpha arbutin, time
Flat pink or red patchRed mark (small blood vessels after inflammation)Gentle care, sunscreen and patience; brightening creams do little for redness
Dip, hole or uneven surfacePitted scar (lost tissue)A dermatologist; no cream can fill a scar
Raised, firm bumpRaised scarA dermatologist
New red, painful bumpsActive acne, not a markCalm the acne first; see a dermatologist if painful or frequent

Run a finger over the mark. If it is perfectly flat and only the colour is different, it is a dark or red mark. If you feel a dip or a bump, it is a scar.

2. Why dark marks are so common on Indian skin

Brown and darker skin tones make pigment easily, so any inflammation, from a pimple to a scratch or a bad reaction to a product, can leave a dark patch behind. Then Indian sun keeps that patch dark. That is why the same mark can fade quickly for one person and stay for months for another who rides a scooter to work without sunscreen.

Picking and squeezing pimples makes marks darker and longer-lasting. So does scrubbing them hard, or putting lemon, toothpaste or baking soda on them.

3. How the ingredients help

  • Alpha arbutin: used in creams to help reduce the look of dark spots and uneven tone, gradually. Read more in alpha arbutin cream.
  • Niacinamide (vitamin B3): helps skin look more even and supports the moisture barrier, and suits oily, acne-prone skin well.
  • Glutathione: an antioxidant used for a brighter, more radiant-looking complexion.
  • Sunscreen: not an ingredient in the cream, but the single biggest factor. Without it, marks keep getting darker in the sun.

Why these three work well together is explained in niacinamide and alpha arbutin.

4. A simple routine for dark pimple marks

  1. Morning: wash with a mild face wash, pat dry, apply a pea-sized amount of cream over the whole face (not just on the marks).
  2. Sunscreen: SPF 30 or more on top, two finger-lengths for the face and neck. Reapply if you are outdoors for long.
  3. Night: wash off sunscreen and dirt, then a pea-sized amount of cream again.
  4. Hands off: do not pick new pimples; each one you squeeze can leave a new mark.
  5. Take a photo in the same light every two weeks. Marks fade too slowly to notice in the mirror day to day.

Step-by-step pictures are on how to use the cream, and sunscreen layering is covered in glutathione cream with sunscreen.

5. How long pimple marks take to look lighter

Skin renews itself roughly every four to six weeks, so be patient. A realistic guide:

  • Weeks 1 to 4: skin feels smoother and looks fresher; marks look about the same.
  • Weeks 4 to 8: newer, lighter-brown marks may start to look softer.
  • Weeks 8 to 12 and beyond: a more even look for many people. Older, darker marks can take several months and may only lighten partly.

7. Mistakes that keep marks dark

  • Skipping sunscreen on cloudy days or at home near windows.
  • Using several new products at once, which can irritate skin and create new marks.
  • Home bleach or harsh scrubs on acne-prone skin.
  • Creams that promise to remove marks in a few days or do not print an ingredient list; some contain steroids. See how to reduce dark spots on the face for what to avoid.
  • Stopping after two weeks because nothing seems to change.

8. When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if your acne is painful, cystic or leaves new marks every month, if you have pitted or raised scars, or if marks do not change at all after three months of sunscreen and gentle care. Dermatologists can treat active acne and scars in ways a face cream cannot.

FAQ

Does glutathione cream remove pimple marks?

No cream removes marks overnight. A cream with glutathione, niacinamide and alpha arbutin can help dark pimple marks look lighter over eight weeks or more, with daily sunscreen.

Can I apply the cream only on the marks?

Apply it over the whole face. Spot-only use often leaves a lighter ring around a darker spot and does nothing for the next mark.

Can I use it while I still have pimples?

Yes, on unbroken skin, but do not put it on open or bleeding pimples. If you get frequent or painful acne, see a dermatologist first.

Why are my pimple marks getting darker?

Usually sun exposure, picking, or irritation from harsh products. Daily sunscreen and leaving pimples alone make the biggest difference.

Will it help acne scars?

Not pitted or raised scars. A cream works on the colour of flat marks only; scars need a dermatologist.
