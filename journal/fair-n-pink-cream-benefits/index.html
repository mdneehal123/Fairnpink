Fair N Pink cream benefits: what it does and what it does not

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Fair N Pink Advance Radiance Cream is a daily face cream. People use it to help their skin look brighter, more even and better moisturised. This page explains where those benefits come from and where they stop.

What it is used for

  • Helping dull skin look brighter and fresher
  • Helping uneven tone look more even over time
  • Keeping skin moisturised and soft, morning and night

Where the benefits come from

The cream is built around three ingredients. Glutathione is an antioxidant used in creams made for brighter-looking skin. Niacinamide, or vitamin B3, helps skin look smoother and more even. Alpha arbutin is used to help reduce the look of dark spots. You can read about each on the ingredients page.

What it does not do

It does not change your natural skin tone. No cosmetic cream does, whatever the label says. It is also not a treatment for melasma, acne or any other skin condition. For those, see a dermatologist.

What decides whether you see a benefit

  • Sunscreen. Sun exposure is the main cause of dullness and dark patches. Without daily sunscreen, a brightening cream is working against the sun and losing.
  • Consistency. Twice a day, every day. Skin renews itself over weeks.
  • Time. Judge it after four to eight weeks, comparing photos taken in the same light.

Results differ from person to person. See how to use the cream for the full routine.
