Can you use saffron (kesar) with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, as a separate step: soak 3 to 4 kesar strands in milk or rose water, dab it on clean skin for 10 to 15 minutes, rinse, pat dry, then apply the glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Never mix kesar into the jar; it does not change skin colour.

Yes, you can use saffron (kesar) with glutathione cream: soak a few strands in milk or rose water, dab it on clean skin as a short pack, rinse, pat dry, then apply the cream. Keep kesar as a separate step and never stir strands or kesar water into the jar.

Kesar milk and kesar packs are a familiar part of Indian bridal and festival skincare, and the wedding season is when most people try them. Saffron can make a pack feel special and skin feel soft. It does not remove tan or dark spots, and it does not change your natural skin colour.

2. How to make a simple kesar pack

  1. Take 3 to 4 strands of saffron. More does not help and only costs more.
  2. Soak them in 2 teaspoons of cold milk or plain rose water for at least 2 hours, or overnight in the fridge with a lid. The liquid turns golden.
  3. Add a teaspoon of plain curd, honey or chandan powder if you want a thicker pack.
  4. Use it the same day. Throw away any milk pack that is left over.

3. The right order with the cream

  1. Patch test the kesar mix on your inner arm or behind the ear and check the next day.
  2. Wash your face with a mild face wash and pat it dry.
  3. Dab the kesar mix on with clean fingertips or cotton, away from the eyes.
  4. Leave it for 10 to 15 minutes, then rinse with plain water.
  5. Pat dry and wait a minute or two.
  6. Apply a thin layer of the glutathione cream, away from the eyes and lips.
  7. Sunscreen every morning, SPF 30 or higher.

Rinse-off steps go first and the cream after, so the cream sits on clean skin. For the daily cream steps, read how to use the cream.

4. Real vs fake kesar

Saffron is costly, so cheap packets are often coloured threads, corn silk or dyed flower parts. A fake can stain skin, and its dye may irritate. Simple checks at home:

  • Colour in water: real strands release a slow golden-yellow colour; a fast deep red or orange cloud is a warning sign.
  • The strand: real strands keep their red colour after soaking; fake ones often turn pale or white.
  • Smell: real kesar has a strong, honey-like smell; fakes smell of little or nothing.
  • Price: a very cheap large packet is almost never pure saffron.

6. Who should be careful

  • Sensitive skin that reacts to new things; patch test and see glutathione cream for sensitive skin.
  • Anyone with a milk allergy: use rose water instead of milk.
  • Broken skin, active pimples, or skin right after threading, waxing or bleach; see face cream after threading or waxing.
  • Pregnancy: this guide is about a rinse-off face pack only, not eating saffron; ask your doctor about anything you take by mouth.

7. Kesar for glow: an honest view

Saffron is often shared online as a way to "make skin fair" before a wedding. That is not true: kesar does not change your natural skin colour, and there is no reliable evidence that a home kesar pack fades tan or spots. It can be a lovely, calming ritual and leave skin feeling soft. Tan and dark marks look lighter slowly, over weeks, with sunscreen every morning and steady care; read glutathione cream for sun tan and how to reduce dark spots on face.

9. When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if your skin itches, burns or breaks into bumps after a kesar pack, if a yellow or orange stain does not wash off and the skin feels sore, or if dark patches on the cheeks keep spreading (this can be melasma, which needs a doctor).

FAQ

Can I apply glutathione cream after a kesar pack?

Yes. Rinse the pack off, pat your face dry, wait a minute or two, then apply a thin layer of the cream.

Can I mix kesar into my glutathione cream?

No. Strands and kesar water in the jar invite germs and can spoil the cream. Keep kesar as a separate step.

How often can I use a kesar face pack?

Once or twice a week is plenty. Use 3 to 4 strands each time.

Can I leave kesar milk on my face overnight?

It is better not to. Milk left on skin for hours can smell, feel sticky and may cause bumps. Rinse it off after 10 to 15 minutes.

Does kesar with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Used with sunscreen, the cream can help tan and marks look lighter over weeks.
