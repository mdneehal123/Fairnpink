How to buy Fair N Pink cream online

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

You can buy Fair N Pink Advance Radiance Cream online on this website, which is the brand's own store. Ordering takes about a minute and you can pay online or with Cash on Delivery.

How to order

  1. Open the store page and choose a pack of 1, 2 or 3.
  2. Tap Buy now and fill in your name, mobile number and address.
  3. Choose to pay online or with Cash on Delivery.
  4. To pay online, tap the Pay button and complete the payment. For Cash on Delivery, tap Send order on WhatsApp and we confirm by reply.

Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery is available. You pay the regular pack price to the courier when the parcel arrives.

Paying online

Paying online saves ₹100 on one jar, ₹150 on a pack of 2 and ₹250 on a pack of 3. You can pay by UPI, card or netbanking in a secure Razorpay window, and you see a payment ID as soon as it succeeds. We never ask for your UPI PIN or card details.

Delivery

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours, except on Sundays and national holidays, and reach most pincodes in 3 to 7 working days. Shipping is free on prepaid orders. See the shipping policy.

Cancellations and damaged parcels

You can cancel any time before dispatch. If a jar arrives damaged, message us within 24 hours with an opening video. Details are in the cancellation and refund policy.

Why buy from the brand

Every order placed here is packed and sent by Fair N Pink, so you know the jar is genuine and sealed. See how to identify original Fair N Pink.
