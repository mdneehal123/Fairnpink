Can you use argan oil with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes. Apply the glutathione cream first, wait a few minutes, then press 1 to 2 drops of pure argan oil on dry areas at night, with sunscreen every morning. Use pure argan kernel oil, not an argan hair serum, and oily skin in humid weather can usually skip it.

Yes, you can use argan oil with glutathione cream: apply the cream first, wait a few minutes, then press 1 to 2 drops of pure argan oil only on dry areas at night. Never mix the oil into the jar, and wear sunscreen every morning.

Argan oil comes from the kernels of the argan tree, which grows in Morocco. In India it is sold mostly as a hair oil and in hair serums, so many people already have a bottle at home and wonder whether it can go on the face too. Pure argan oil is a medium-weight oil: lighter than coconut, castor or olive oil, a little richer than jojoba. It suits dry winter skin well; oily skin in humid weather usually does not need it.

2. The right order

  1. Wash your face with a mild face wash and pat it dry.
  2. Apply a thin layer of the glutathione cream on the face and neck.
  3. Wait 3 to 5 minutes so the cream settles into the skin.
  4. Warm 1 to 2 drops of argan oil between your fingertips. For the whole face, 2 drops is plenty.
  5. Press it gently onto dry areas: cheeks, around the nose, chin and jawline. Do not drag or rub hard.
  6. Use it at night. In the morning, a light moisturiser and sunscreen are enough; oil under sunscreen can make it slide off in heat.

Cream first, oil second: the cream sits directly on the skin, and the thin oil layer on top helps slow water loss overnight. For the daily cream steps, read how to use the cream.

4. Pure argan oil vs "argan" hair serums

This is the most common mistake. Many bottles sold in India with "argan" on the front are hair serums made mostly of silicones, mineral oil and fragrance, with only a little argan oil. They are fine for hair, but they are not meant for the face and can clog pores or irritate.

  • Read the ingredient list: it should say only argan kernel oil (Argania spinosa kernel oil).
  • Skip bottles with fragrance, "parfum", mineral oil or long silicone names (ending in -cone or -siloxane) if you want it for the face.
  • Cold-pressed cosmetic argan is pale gold with a light, nutty smell. A strong roasted smell means culinary argan, which is not meant for skin.
  • Buy a small dropper bottle; you only need a couple of drops a night, and oil goes stale once opened.
  • Store it capped, away from sun and the bathroom geyser, and throw it away if the smell turns sharp or rancid.

5. Argan vs other oils

If you already have another oil at home, you do not need to buy argan. Jojoba is lighter and suits normal-to-combination skin; coconut and castor oil are heavier and better kept to lips, heels and very dry patches; almond and olive oil are richer and suit very dry winter skin. Compare them in jojoba oil with glutathione cream, almond oil with glutathione cream and coconut oil with glutathione cream.

6. What not to do

  • Do not mix argan oil into the cream jar. It changes the cream and can spoil it.
  • Do not apply the oil under the cream. The oil layer can stop the cream from sitting on the skin.
  • Do not add lemon or essential oils to it for the face.
  • Do not use it before makeup on oily skin; read glutathione cream before makeup.
  • Do not expect it to fade spots or tan. Sunscreen every morning does far more; see glutathione cream with sunscreen.

8. When to see a dermatologist

Stop the oil and see a dermatologist if your skin turns red, itchy or swollen, if you get many new bumps or whiteheads, or if dryness comes with cracking, scaling or patches that do not settle with a simple moisturiser. Patch test any new oil on the jawline for 2 to 3 nights first, and read glutathione cream for sensitive skin if you react easily.

FAQ

Can I apply argan oil after glutathione cream?

Yes. Apply the cream first, wait a few minutes, then press 1 to 2 drops of pure argan oil on dry areas at night.

Can I use my argan hair serum on my face?

Better not. Most hair serums are mostly silicones and fragrance, made for hair. Use only pure argan kernel oil on the face.

Is argan oil good for oily skin?

It is lighter than many oils, but oily skin usually does not need extra oil, especially in humid weather. Skip it or use one drop only on dry patches.

Can I use argan oil in the morning?

It is better at night. In the day, oil under sunscreen can make it slide off with sweat; use a light moisturiser and sunscreen instead.

Does argan oil with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Used with sunscreen, the cream can help tan and marks look lighter over weeks.
