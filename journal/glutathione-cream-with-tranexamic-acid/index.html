Can you use tranexamic acid with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes. Apply 2 to 3 drops of tranexamic acid serum on clean, dry skin first, wait a minute or two, then a thin layer of glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Start the serum once a day; patches on both cheeks may be melasma and need a dermatologist.

Yes, you can use a tranexamic acid serum with glutathione cream: apply a few drops of the serum on clean, dry skin first, wait a minute or two, then apply a thin layer of the glutathione cream, and finish with sunscreen in the morning. The two are commonly used in the same routine for dark spots and uneven-looking tone, and they are not known to clash.

Tranexamic acid (often shortened to TXA on labels) has become a popular ingredient in Indian serums for dark spots and tan. On the skin, it is used as a cosmetic ingredient to help spots and patches look lighter over time. It is not a quick fix, and it does not change your natural skin colour.

2. The right order

  1. Wash your face with a mild face wash and pat it fully dry.
  2. Apply 2 to 3 drops of tranexamic acid serum on the face, or only on the darker areas if the label suggests spot use.
  3. Wait 1 to 2 minutes until the serum has sunk in.
  4. Apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream in a thin layer over the face.
  5. Morning: sunscreen last, after the cream has set. At night, stop after the cream.

The rule is thinnest to thickest: watery serum first, cream next, sunscreen last. For the daily steps with the cream, see how to use the cream, and for sunscreen tips read glutathione cream with sunscreen.

3. Morning, night or both?

Many tranexamic acid serums are gentle enough for morning and night, but check the label. A simple plan that suits most people:

TimeWhat to use
MorningFace wash, tranexamic acid serum (if your label allows daytime use), thin layer of cream, sunscreen
NightFace wash, tranexamic acid serum, glutathione cream
If your skin feels dry or tinglyUse the serum once a day only, at night

Start with the serum once a day for the first week and see how your skin feels before going to twice a day.

4. Who it suits, and who should skip it

  • Oily or combination skin: a watery serum plus a thin layer of cream is usually enough; skip extra moisturisers in humid weather. See glutathione cream for oily skin.
  • Dry skin: add a plain moisturiser on top at night if your face feels tight; see glutathione cream and moisturiser.
  • Sensitive skin: patch test the serum on the jawline for 3 nights before using it on the whole face. See glutathione cream for sensitive skin.
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding: ask your doctor before starting any new active serum or cream.
  • Anyone taking tranexamic acid tablets: tablets are a prescription medicine and a separate matter from a cosmetic serum. Do not start, stop or change any tablets without your doctor.

5. Do not stack too many brighteners

Tranexamic acid, glutathione, niacinamide, alpha arbutin, vitamin C and kojic acid are all popular for uneven-looking tone. More actives do not mean faster results; they mainly raise the chance of dryness and irritation. A serum plus a cream is plenty. If you also use a vitamin C serum, use it in the morning and tranexamic acid at night, rather than layering three products. Read alpha arbutin and vitamin C and glutathione and niacinamide together for how the other pairs work.

6. Realistic expectations

Skin renews slowly, so spots and tan usually take about 8 to 12 weeks of steady use, with sunscreen every single morning, before they look lighter. Results vary from person to person, and dark spots can come back with new sun. Read how long glutathione cream takes to work and how to reduce dark spots on the face for a full plan.

8. When to see a dermatologist

Brown or grey-brown patches on both cheeks, the forehead or the upper lip may be melasma, which often needs a dermatologist's plan rather than a serum alone. Also see a dermatologist if a spot grows, changes colour or bleeds, or if the serum causes burning, rash or swelling. Patch test any new product first.

FAQ

Should I apply tranexamic acid before or after glutathione cream?

Before. The serum is thinner, so it goes on clean skin first, then the cream on top.

Can I use tranexamic acid and glutathione cream every day?

Many people can, once their skin is used to both. Start the serum once a day and always wear sunscreen in the morning.

Can I use tranexamic acid with niacinamide?

Yes, they are commonly used together. Fair N Pink already contains niacinamide, so you do not need a separate niacinamide serum.

Will tranexamic acid remove tan in a week?

No. Tan and dark spots fade slowly over weeks, and only with daily sunscreen and shade.

Does tranexamic acid with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. With sunscreen, they can help spots and tan look lighter over weeks.
