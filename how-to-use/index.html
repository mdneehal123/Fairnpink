The ritual

How to use Fair N Pink cream

Twice a day, a pea-sized amount, on clean skin. The whole routine takes under a minute.

Use Fair N Pink Advance Radiance Cream twice a day: wash your face, pat it dry, and spread a pea-sized amount over the face and neck in a thin layer. In the morning finish with sunscreen; at night leave it on. Patch test on your inner arm for 24 hours before the first use.

Morning

  1. Wash your face and pat it dry.
  2. Take a pea-sized amount of cream.
  3. Spread a thin layer over face and neck.
  4. Finish with sunscreen before you go out.

Night

  1. Wash off the day's dirt, sweat and sunscreen.
  2. Take a pea-sized amount of cream.
  3. Massage gently until it is absorbed.
  4. Leave it on overnight.
Your skin, your routine

Adjusting it for your skin type

  • Oily skin: a very thin layer; if it feels heavy in the day, use it at night only and a gel sunscreen in the morning.
  • Dry skin: apply on slightly damp skin, and add a plain moisturiser on top at night in winter.
  • Sensitive skin: it contains fragrance, so patch test first and start once a day at night for a week.
  • Men: the same routine; apply a few minutes after shaving, not straight after.

More detail in who can use Fair N Pink cream.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Using a thick layer: more cream does not work faster.
  • Skipping sunscreen: daily sun darkens spots faster than any cream can help them fade.
  • Layering strong products (retinol, peels, scrubs) on the same night when you are starting.
  • Judging after a few days: give it at least four weeks, and eight for dark spots.
What to expect

How long does it take to work?

Skin renews itself slowly. This is a general guide, and results differ from person to person.

  1. Week 1

    Settling in

    Use it morning and night after a patch test. Skin usually feels softer and better moisturised.

  2. Week 2

    Building the habit

    Keep to twice a day with sunscreen every morning. Skin may start to look fresher.

  3. Week 4

    First real check

    Compare your skin with a photo from day one, in the same light.

  4. Week 8

    Dark spots take longest

    Marks fade slowly and some may not change. No cream changes your natural skin tone.

Patch test first

Try a small amount on your inner arm for 24 hours before using it on your face.

Use sunscreen daily

Sun exposure is the main cause of dullness and dark patches. No cream can keep up without it.

Give it time

Skin changes slowly and results differ from person to person. Use it consistently for a few weeks.

Good to know

Getting the most from one jar

How much to use

A pea-sized amount is enough for the face and neck. Using more does not make it work faster, and a 10 g jar lasts longer when you keep to a thin layer.

With other products

Use it after washing your face and before sunscreen in the morning. At night it goes on last. If you use a prescription cream, ask your doctor before adding a new product.

Precautions

Keep the cream away from your eyes and from broken skin. If your skin turns red, itches or burns, wash it off and stop using it. If the reaction does not settle, see a dermatologist. Store the jar closed, in a cool, dry place.

Questions

How to use Fair N Pink cream: common questions

How many times a day should I apply Fair N Pink cream?

Twice a day, morning and night, on clean skin. If you have sensitive or very oily skin, start once a day at night for the first week.

Should I wash off Fair N Pink cream?

No. It is a leave-on cream. Leave the night layer on until morning, then wash your face and apply a fresh thin layer under sunscreen.

Can I apply Fair N Pink cream under makeup?

Yes. Use a thin layer, let it absorb for a few minutes, apply sunscreen, then makeup. See glutathione cream before makeup.

Can I use it on my neck and hands?

Yes, on the neck as part of the routine, and on small areas such as hands or knees after a patch test. A 10 g jar is sized for the face and neck.

How long does one jar last?

It depends on how much you use and how many areas you cover. A pea-sized amount twice a day on the face and neck is enough; a thin layer makes the jar last longer.

Does Fair N Pink cream make skin fair?

No. It helps skin look brighter and more even, and tan and dark spots look lighter over weeks with daily sunscreen. It does not change your natural skin colour.
