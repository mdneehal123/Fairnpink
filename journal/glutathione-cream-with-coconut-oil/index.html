Can you use coconut oil with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, for dry skin. Apply the glutathione cream first, wait a few minutes, then press a small drop of coconut oil on dry patches at night only, with sunscreen every morning. Oily and acne-prone skin should usually skip coconut oil on the face.

Yes, you can use coconut oil with glutathione cream if your skin is dry: apply the cream first, wait a few minutes, then press a small drop of coconut oil only on dry patches, at night. 2. The right order at night

  1. Wash with a gentle face wash and pat dry.
  2. Apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream on the face and neck.
  3. Wait 3 to 5 minutes so the cream settles.
  4. Take a very small amount of coconut oil, about a drop, and warm it between your fingertips until it melts.
  5. Press it gently on the driest areas only: outer cheeks, around the nose, chin, or flaky patches. Do not massage it over the whole face.
  6. Next morning: wash it off, apply a thin layer of cream if you like, then sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

Oil always goes last, because it sits on top and slows down water loss from the layers underneath. If it goes first, the cream spreads unevenly on a greasy surface. The same cream-then-oil order is used in our guides to almond oil with glutathione cream and kumkumadi oil with glutathione cream.

4. Coconut oil vs almond oil on the face

Both are home favourites, but they feel different. Coconut oil is thicker and turns solid in a cool room, so it sits heavily on the skin. Sweet almond oil stays liquid and spreads more lightly. If you are unsure which suits you, the lighter oil, in a smaller amount, is usually the safer start. Neither is needed if your skin feels comfortable with the cream alone.

5. Choosing and storing coconut oil

  • Use plain coconut oil with coconut as the only ingredient. Skip hair oils with added perfume, amla, camphor or other extras on the face.
  • Keep the bottle clean. Scoop with a clean spoon instead of dipping in fingers, especially if the oil has gone solid.
  • Check the smell. A sour or sharp smell means it has gone rancid; throw it away.
  • Never mix oil into the cream jar. It changes the texture and can make the jar spoil sooner.

6. Mistakes to avoid

  • Using coconut oil as a daytime base. Oil gives no sun protection and can make sunscreen slide and collect dust.
  • Using too much. A thick layer usually ends up on the pillow and can cause bumps.
  • Rubbing it on active pimples. Keep oil away from breakouts. For flat marks left behind, see glutathione cream for pimple marks.
  • Expecting coconut oil to fade tan or spots. Those fade slowly with daily sunscreen and steady care over weeks. See glutathione cream for sun tan.

FAQ

Should I apply coconut oil before or after glutathione cream?

After. Apply the cream first, wait a few minutes, then press a small drop of coconut oil on dry areas only.

Can I mix coconut oil with glutathione cream?

It is better to keep them as separate steps. Mixing makes the cream greasy and harder to spread in a thin, even layer, and oil should never go into the jar.

Is coconut oil good for oily skin with glutathione cream?

Usually not. Oily and acne-prone skin often gets bumps from coconut oil; a thin layer of cream is normally enough moisture.

Can I leave coconut oil on my face overnight?

A small drop on dry patches can stay overnight if your skin likes it. Wash it off in the morning before sunscreen.

Does coconut oil with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Coconut oil softens dry skin, and the cream can help skin look brighter and more even over weeks. Neither changes your natural skin colour.
