Glutathione cream for combination skin: how to use it on an oily T-zone and dry cheeks

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, combination skin can use glutathione cream: put a very thin layer on the oily T-zone and a little more on drier cheeks, mostly at night, with sunscreen every morning. It can help skin look brighter and more even over 8 to 12 weeks.

Yes, combination skin can use a glutathione cream. The trick is to apply it unevenly on purpose: a very thin layer on the oily T-zone (forehead, nose and chin) and a little more on the cheeks, which are usually drier. Used this way, with sunscreen every morning, it can help skin look brighter and more even over 8 to 12 weeks without making the T-zone greasy.

Combination skin is very common in India, and it changes with the season: oilier in summer and the monsoon, drier and tighter on the cheeks in winter and in AC rooms. Here is how to fit a cream into that.

1. How to tell if you have combination skin

Wash your face with a mild face wash, pat dry and wait about an hour without applying anything. If the forehead and nose look shiny but the cheeks feel normal or a little tight, you have combination skin. If the whole face is shiny, read glutathione cream for oily skin instead.

2. How much cream goes where

AreaHow it usually feelsHow much cream
ForeheadShiny by middayA very thin layer, just what is left on your fingers
Nose and sides of the noseOiliest; open-looking poresA trace only, or skip in humid weather
ChinOily, sometimes with breakoutsA thin layer
CheeksNormal to dry, tight after washingMost of the pea-sized amount
NeckUsually normalA little extra if you use it there

In total, a pea-sized amount is enough for the whole face. Start on the cheeks, then spread what is left on your fingers across the T-zone.

3. A simple routine for combination skin

  1. Night: wash with a gentle face wash and lukewarm water, pat dry, and apply the cream as in the table above.
  2. Morning: rinse or wash, apply a thin layer of cream on the cheeks only (or skip it in humid weather), then sunscreen SPF 30 or more on the whole face.
  3. Midday (summer): blot the T-zone with tissue rather than washing again. Reapply sunscreen if you are outdoors.
  4. Once a week, if you like: a clay pack on the T-zone only, not on dry cheeks. Use a plain moisturiser that night.

5. Mistakes that make combination skin worse

  • Using the same thick layer everywhere. It feels heavy on the nose and still not enough on the cheeks.
  • Washing the face many times a day. It dries the cheeks and can make the T-zone look oilier later.
  • Strong scrubs or lemon on the nose. They irritate the skin without making pores smaller.
  • Skipping sunscreen because the face feels oily. Choose a gel or matte sunscreen instead of skipping it, because sun makes uneven tone look worse.
  • Adding many actives at once. If you also use a salicylic acid face wash or serum, read glutathione cream with salicylic acid before layering.

7. When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if you have painful or frequent breakouts on the chin or jawline, redness that does not settle, flaking with itching around the nose and eyebrows, or dark patches that spread. These need a proper diagnosis, and a cosmetic cream is not a treatment for them.

FAQ

Is glutathione cream good for combination skin?

Yes, if you use thin layers: less on the oily T-zone and a little more on dry cheeks, with sunscreen every morning. Patch test first.

Should I apply glutathione cream on my nose if it is oily?

Use only a trace on the nose, or skip it on humid days. Most of the pea-sized amount should go on the cheeks.

Can I use glutathione cream in the morning on combination skin?

Yes, a thin layer on the cheeks under sunscreen works for most people. In summer, many prefer using it only at night.

Will glutathione cream make my T-zone more oily?

Not if you use a thin layer. A heavy layer on any oily area can feel greasy, so use less there rather than skipping moisture on the whole face.

How long before I see a difference?

Skin can feel softer within days. A brighter, more even-looking tone usually takes 8 to 12 weeks of steady use with daily sunscreen.
