Glutathione cream for dark upper lip area: causes and a gentle routine

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

A glutathione cream can help a dark upper lip area look a little lighter over 8 to 12 weeks: use a rice-grain amount above the lip, not on the lips, skip it after threading, waxing or bleach, and wear sunscreen every morning. A broad patch may be melasma; see a dermatologist.

A glutathione cream can help a dark upper lip area look a little lighter and more even over 8 to 12 weeks, if you use a small amount gently, stop rubbing and irritating the area, and wear sunscreen there every morning. Keep the cream on the skin above the lip, not on the lips themselves, and see a dermatologist if the darkness looks like a patch that may be melasma.

A darker shadow above the lip is very common in India. It usually comes from small, repeated irritation (threading, waxing, bleach, hair removal creams), sun and sometimes hormones. Because the skin here is thin and moves a lot, it needs a gentler routine than the cheeks.

2. A gentle routine for the upper lip

  1. Patch test the cream on the side of the jaw for 2 to 3 days first; the upper lip is more sensitive.
  2. Night: wash with a gentle face wash, pat dry, and wait a few minutes so the skin is fully dry.
  3. Apply a rice-grain amount of cream with one fingertip, pressing it softly onto the skin above the lip. Stay a few millimetres away from the lip line.
  4. Put lip balm on the lips afterwards so they stay soft and the cream does not spread onto them.
  5. Morning: a thin layer of cream if you like, then sunscreen on the upper lip as well as the rest of the face.
  6. Judge after 8 to 12 weeks, with photos in the same light.

Start every other night if your skin is sensitive. See glutathione cream for sensitive skin.

3. Threading, waxing and bleach: plan around them

Each session slightly irritates the skin, and repeated irritation is one of the most common reasons the upper lip looks darker.

  • Skip the cream for about 24 hours after threading or waxing, and for 2 to 3 days after bleach. Use a plain moisturiser and cool water instead.
  • Do not bleach the upper lip often; bleach on thin skin can leave it red and irritated.
  • Leave longer gaps between sessions if you can, and go to someone who works hygienically.
  • If you get bumps or burning after hair removal, let the skin settle fully before using any cream there.

Read face cream after facial, threading or waxing for exact timings.

4. Sunscreen on the upper lip

The upper lip is often missed when applying sunscreen, and sweat wipes it off quickly. Dab a little extra above the lip every morning, reapply after lunch or after wiping your face, and cover your face on long rides. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

5. What to avoid

  • Lemon, baking soda, toothpaste or potato scrubs on the upper lip. Irritation here often makes it darker. See lemon with glutathione cream.
  • Rubbing or scrubbing the area, or wiping it hard with a towel.
  • Thick layers of cream; more product means more chance of stinging, not faster results.
  • Unknown creams with no full ingredient list, especially those promising fast results.

7. When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if the darkness is a broad patch that also appears on the cheeks or forehead (possible melasma), appeared during pregnancy or after starting a medicine, keeps getting darker despite sunscreen, or comes with itching, burning, peeling or rash. Melasma and irritation need a proper diagnosis, and a cosmetic cream alone is not the answer.

FAQ

Can I apply glutathione cream on my upper lip?

Yes, on the skin above the lip, in a rice-grain amount, after a patch test. Keep it off the lips themselves and skip it right after threading, waxing or bleach.

Why is my upper lip darker than the rest of my face?

Usually from repeated threading, waxing or bleach, sun, and lip licking. Hormonal patches such as melasma can also show up there.

How long does it take to see a difference?

Give it 8 to 12 weeks of gentle, steady use with sunscreen every morning, and less irritation from hair removal.

Can I use the cream on my lips?

No. Lips are very thin and need a plain lip balm, not an active face cream.

Does glutathione cream make the upper lip fair?

No. It does not change your natural skin colour. It can help darkness from sun and irritation look a little lighter and more even over weeks.
