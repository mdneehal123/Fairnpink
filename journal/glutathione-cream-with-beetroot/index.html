Can you use beetroot with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, as a separate step: dab fresh beetroot juice or a simple pack on clean skin, rinse after 5 to 10 minutes, pat dry, then apply the glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Never mix beetroot into the jar; its pink tint is temporary.

Yes, you can use beetroot with glutathione cream, but only as a separate step: apply beetroot juice or a beetroot pack on clean skin, rinse it off after 5 to 10 minutes, pat dry, then apply the glutathione cream, with sunscreen every morning. Never mix beetroot into the cream jar.

Beetroot (chukandar) is a popular home remedy in India for a "rosy glow". The pink-red colour you see after a pack mostly comes from the beet's own pigment staining the top layer of skin, and it washes off within a day. It is a fun, cheap pack, not a brightening treatment, and it does not change your natural skin colour.

2. How to use beetroot with the cream

  1. Patch test first: dab a little beetroot juice on your jawline for 10 minutes, rinse, and wait a day.
  2. Wash your face with a mild face wash and pat it dry.
  3. Apply fresh beetroot juice with cotton, or a simple pack of grated or blended beetroot mixed with a spoon of curd or aloe gel, avoiding the eyes and lips.
  4. Leave it on 5 to 10 minutes only. Longer means more staining, not better results.
  5. Rinse with plain water and pat dry gently.
  6. Wait a few minutes, then apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream.
  7. Next morning: sunscreen as usual.

Do this once or twice a week at most. For the cream steps on other days, see how to use the cream. If you like kitchen packs, our guides on curd and multani mitti follow the same rules.

3. Staining: what to expect

  • Beetroot can leave a pink tint on the skin, nails, towels and pillow covers. Use an old towel.
  • The tint on skin usually fades after one or two face washes.
  • If you want to avoid a tint before work or college, do the pack at night.
  • Do not scrub hard to remove the colour; it will fade on its own, and scrubbing can irritate skin.

4. Who should skip it

  • Sensitive or reactive skin: any food pack can sting or redden. See glutathione cream for sensitive skin.
  • Freshly waxed, threaded or bleached skin: wait a day or two; read face cream after facial, threading and waxing.
  • Active pimples, cuts or rashes: keep packs away from these areas.
  • Very oily or acne-prone skin in humid weather: a juice dab is lighter than a thick pack; skip curd or oils in the mix.

5. Common mistakes

  • Mixing beetroot into the cream jar. Fresh juice can spoil the cream and grow germs. Always keep them separate.
  • Adding lemon or baking soda to "boost" the pack. These can irritate skin; see lemon with glutathione cream.
  • Using stored juice. Make it fresh each time and throw away leftovers.
  • Skipping sunscreen because the face looks rosy. Sun is the main reason tan and spots return.

6. Eating beetroot vs putting it on your face

Beetroot is a healthy vegetable to eat as part of a balanced diet, and a good diet, sleep and water support how your skin looks overall. But eating beetroot will not lighten spots or change skin colour either. For dull-looking skin, a steady routine matters more than any single food; see night cream for dull skin.

8. When to see a dermatologist

Stop and see a dermatologist if a pack causes burning, itching, swelling or a rash, or if you have patches of darkness that keep spreading. Patch test every new home remedy first.

FAQ

Can I mix beetroot juice with glutathione cream?

No. Use beetroot as a rinse-off step first, then apply the cream on clean, dry skin.

How often can I use a beetroot face pack?

Once or twice a week is enough. Daily packs are not needed and can irritate sensitive skin.

How long does the pink tint from beetroot last?

Usually only until your next one or two face washes. It is a temporary stain, not a change in your skin.

Can I use beetroot on my lips?

A little beetroot juice can tint lips for a while, but keep the glutathione cream off the lips.

Does beetroot with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. With sunscreen, the cream can help tan and marks look lighter over weeks.
