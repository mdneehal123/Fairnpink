When to apply face cream after a facial, threading or waxing

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Wait about 24 hours after threading or waxing, until the next night after a facial, and 2 to 3 days after bleach before using an active or fragranced face cream. Until then use cool water, a plain moisturiser and sunscreen.

After threading or waxing, wait about 24 hours before applying a face cream with active ingredients or fragrance; after a regular facial, wait until the next night, and after a bleach or chemical peel, wait two to three days or as your parlour or dermatologist advises. Until then, keep it simple: cool water, a plain fragrance-free moisturiser or aloe gel, and sunscreen if you step out.

Why the wait matters

Threading and waxing pull out hair from the root and remove a thin top layer of skin, leaving tiny openings that can sting and redden. A facial often includes scrubbing, steam, massage and extraction, which leave skin warm and more reactive for a few hours. Active ingredients and fragrance are perfectly fine on calm skin, but on freshly treated skin they are more likely to cause burning, redness or small bumps, especially in hot, humid Indian weather when skin is already sweating.

How long to wait: a quick guide

TreatmentWait before an active creamUse in the meantime
Eyebrow or upper-lip threadingAbout 24 hours on that areaCool water, aloe gel or a plain moisturiser
Face waxingAbout 24 to 48 hoursPlain moisturiser; no scrubs, steam or hot water
Clean-up or fruit facialUntil the next nightA light moisturiser and sunscreen by day
Facial with extraction or massageAbout 24 hoursHands off any spots that were extracted
Face bleachAbout 48 to 72 hoursPlain moisturiser; stop if skin burns or itches
Chemical peel or clinic treatmentOnly when your dermatologist says soExactly what the clinic prescribed

These are cautious general timings, not rules. If your skin still feels hot, tight or stings, wait longer.

Your routine for the first day

  1. Rinse with cool or room-temperature water; avoid hot water and steam.
  2. Pat dry with a clean, soft towel; do not rub.
  3. Apply a thin layer of a plain, fragrance-free moisturiser or aloe vera gel.
  4. Wear sunscreen if you go outdoors. Freshly treated skin tans and darkens more easily.
  5. Skip makeup, scrubs, face packs, toners with alcohol and active creams for the day.
  6. Keep your hands, phone and pillow cover clean to avoid bumps after waxing.

Going back to your regular cream

Once skin feels calm (no redness, stinging or heat), return to your usual routine: a mild cleanser, a pea-sized amount of cream, and sunscreen every morning. Signs you should stop and see a doctor

  • Burning, swelling or blisters that last more than a day
  • Pus-filled bumps or spreading redness after waxing
  • Patches that look darker or rough after bleach or a peel

Stop all products except a plain moisturiser and see a dermatologist. Do not apply home remedies such as lemon, toothpaste or baking soda on irritated skin.

Tips for your next parlour visit

  • Stop retinol and exfoliating acids two to three days before waxing or a facial.
  • Tell the beautician about any active creams you use and any allergies.
  • Ask for a patch test before a new bleach or facial product.
  • Book treatments in the evening, so you are not heading into the afternoon sun.
  • Before a wedding or festival, schedule waxing and facials at least two to three days ahead, so any redness settles before the event and your usual cream routine is back on track.
  • In monsoon and humid months, keep the treated area dry and clean, since sweat and dampness make bumps after waxing more likely.

FAQ

Can I apply glutathione cream right after a facial?

It is better to wait until the next night. Skin is warm and reactive after a facial, and a calm start gives you a more comfortable routine. See also who can use Fair N Pink cream.

Is it okay to use sunscreen right after threading?

Yes, if you are going out. Choose a gentle sunscreen and apply it lightly over the area; it protects skin that is more likely to darken in the sun. Read more on using sunscreen with a glutathione cream.

Can I use the cream on my upper lip after threading?

After about 24 hours, once the area no longer stings, yes. Use a very small amount.

Does a facial make a cream work faster?

No. A facial can make skin look fresh for a day or two, but steady daily use of a cream with sunscreen over four to eight weeks matters more. Results differ from person to person.
