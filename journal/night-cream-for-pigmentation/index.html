Night cream for pigmentation and dark spots: what actually helps

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

For the look of pigmentation and dark spots, use a night cream with alpha arbutin and niacinamide every night for at least 8 weeks, with sunscreen every morning. See a dermatologist for melasma or patches that spread.

For the look of pigmentation and dark spots, choose a night cream with alpha arbutin and niacinamide, use it every night for at least eight weeks, and wear sunscreen every morning. Deep, spreading or sudden patches, including melasma, need a dermatologist, not just a cream.

Ingredients to look for

  • Alpha arbutin: used to help reduce the look of dark spots and patches, gradually.
  • Niacinamide: helps uneven tone look more even and supports the skin barrier.
  • Glutathione: an antioxidant used in creams for a brighter-looking complexion.

Why night?

At night the cream stays on clean skin for hours, with no sun, sweat or makeup. That makes night the easiest time to be consistent.

Why sunscreen still decides everything

Sunlight darkens existing spots and makes new ones. A night cream without daily sunscreen rarely shows results.

How long it takes

Expect softer skin in a week, a fresher look in a few weeks, and slow fading of spots over two months or more. Some old marks may not change.

When to see a dermatologist

  • Patches that are spreading, very dark or appeared suddenly
  • Pigmentation during pregnancy or from hormonal medicines
  • No change at all after three months of careful use
