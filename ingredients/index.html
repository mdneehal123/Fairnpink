What is inside

Fair N Pink cream ingredients

Advance Radiance Cream is built around three ingredients: glutathione, niacinamide and alpha arbutin. Here is what each one is and why it is in the jar.

Fair N Pink Advance Radiance Cream is built around three key ingredients: L-glutathione, niacinamide (vitamin B3) and alpha arbutin, in a moisturising cream base with fragrance. It contains no steroids, mercury, hydroquinone or parabens, and the full ingredient list is printed on every box.

L-Glutathione

Glutathione is an antioxidant made of three amino acids, and it is found naturally in the body. In skincare it is used in creams made for brighter-looking skin. It is the ingredient the cream is named for: the French line on the jar, crème éclat glutathione, means glutathione radiance cream.

Niacinamide (vitamin B3)

Niacinamide is a form of vitamin B3 and one of the most widely used ingredients in modern skincare. It helps skin look smoother and more even, and it suits most skin types. On some skin it causes a brief warm or tingling feeling in the first few days of use.

Alpha arbutin

Alpha arbutin is used in creams that help reduce the look of dark spots and uneven tone. It works slowly, which is why we suggest judging the cream over weeks, not days, and always with sunscreen in the morning.

How the three work together

Each ingredient has a different job, which is why they are often paired. Niacinamide helps the skin look smoother and supports its moisture barrier, alpha arbutin is used for the look of dark spots and patchy areas, and glutathione is an antioxidant used in creams for brighter-looking skin. None of them works fast, and none of them can keep up with daily sun on its own, so sunscreen every morning is part of the routine. More on the pairing: glutathione and niacinamide together and niacinamide and alpha arbutin.

What it does not contain

  • No steroids: steroid creams should only be used on the face when a doctor prescribes them.
  • No mercury and no hydroquinone.
  • No parabens.

It does contain fragrance, which is the main reason we ask everyone to patch test first.

Who should be careful

The cream contains fragrance, so do not use it if you are allergic to perfumed products. Do a patch test on your inner arm for 24 hours before your first use. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding or under treatment for a skin condition, ask your doctor before using any new cream.

Does Fair N Pink cream contain steroids?

No. It contains no steroids, mercury, hydroquinone or parabens. Be careful with any face cream that does not print its full ingredient list and maker's address.

How much glutathione is in Fair N Pink cream?

Is glutathione in a cream the same as glutathione tablets or injections?

No. A cream is a cosmetic that works on the surface of the skin. Tablets and injections are a different matter; ask a doctor before taking any of them.
