Can you use castor oil (arandi ka tel) with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Yes, sparingly: apply the glutathione cream first, wait a few minutes, then press one drop of castor oil only on dry areas at night, with sunscreen every morning. Castor oil is very thick, so oily or acne-prone skin should usually skip it, and never mix it into the jar.

Yes, you can use castor oil with glutathione cream, but sparingly: apply the cream first on clean, dry skin, wait a few minutes, then press one drop of castor oil only on dry areas, and only at night. Castor oil is very thick and sticky, so most faces need little or none of it, and it should never be mixed into the jar.

Arandi ka tel is found in many Indian homes for hair, eyebrows and cracked heels. It is a heavy oil that can help very dry patches feel softer in winter. It does not remove dark spots or tan, and on oily or acne-prone skin it often feels greasy and can lead to bumps.

2. The right order at night

  1. Patch test a drop of castor oil on your jawline for 2 to 3 nights first. Some people react to it.
  2. Wash your face with a mild face wash and pat it dry.
  3. Apply a thin layer of the glutathione cream on the face, away from the eyes and lips.
  4. Wait 3 to 5 minutes so the cream settles.
  5. Warm one drop of castor oil between your fingertips. If it feels too thick, mix it with a drop of a lighter oil such as almond or coconut oil.
  6. Press it only on dry areas, such as flaky cheeks or the sides of the nose. Do not rub it over the whole face.
  7. Sunscreen every morning, SPF 30 or higher. Wash the oil off in the morning before your day cream and sunscreen.

Putting the oil first can stop the cream from spreading evenly, which is why the cream goes on first. For the daily cream steps, read how to use the cream.

4. Eyebrows, lashes and lips

Many people use castor oil on eyebrows and lashes. A tiny amount on clean brows with a cotton bud is fine for most people. On lashes, keep it out of the eyes, use very little, and stop if your eyes get red or itchy. There is little good evidence that castor oil makes brows or lashes grow; it mainly makes them look glossier. Keep the glutathione cream away from the lash line and the lips, and do not mix the two near the eyes.

5. Habits to skip

  • Castor oil mixed into the cream jar: never. It changes the cream and spreads a heavy oil all over your face.
  • A thick layer of castor oil all over the face: it often leads to greasiness and bumps, especially in humid weather.
  • Castor oil under makeup or sunscreen in the day: it can make sunscreen slide and makeup patchy.
  • Castor oil with lemon or baking soda "packs": these can sting and irritate. See lemon with glutathione cream.

6. Who should skip castor oil on the face

  • Oily or acne-prone skin. It is usually too heavy; see glutathione cream for acne prone skin.
  • Sensitive skin that reacts easily; patch test, and see glutathione cream for sensitive skin.
  • Anyone who has had a rash from castor oil or from lip products that contain it.
  • Broken skin, or skin right after threading, waxing or bleach.

7. Castor oil for dark spots: an honest view

Castor oil is often shared online as a remedy for dark spots and pigmentation. There is no reliable evidence that it fades them. Where it can help is comfort: a drop can make a very dry patch feel softer in winter, and calm, well-moisturised skin is easier to care for. Dark spots and tan look lighter slowly, over weeks, with sunscreen every morning and steady care; read how to reduce dark spots on face and glutathione cream in winter.

9. When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if your skin itches, burns or breaks into a rash after castor oil, if your eyes get red or swollen after using it on lashes, if new bumps keep appearing, or if dark patches on the cheeks keep spreading (this can be melasma).

FAQ

Can I apply castor oil after glutathione cream?

Yes, at night. Wait a few minutes after the cream, then press one drop only on dry areas.

Can I mix castor oil into my glutathione cream?

No. Keep them separate so the cream stays as it was made and you control how much oil goes on.

Is castor oil good for oily skin?

Usually not. It is very heavy and can feel greasy and lead to bumps. A thin layer of cream is enough.

Can I use castor oil on my face every night?

Only on very dry patches, and only if it does not cause bumps. Many people need it only in winter.

Does castor oil with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Used with sunscreen, the cream can help tan and marks look lighter over weeks.
