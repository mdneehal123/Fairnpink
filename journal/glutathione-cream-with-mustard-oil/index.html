Can you use mustard oil with glutathione cream?

By Fair N Pink · Updated 5 October 2026

Short answer

Only in a small way: apply the glutathione cream first, let it settle, then press a single drop of mustard oil on a very dry patch at night, after a patch test. Skip it on oily, acne-prone or sensitive skin; sarson ka tel is better kept for body massage.

You can use mustard oil with glutathione cream only in a very small way: a single drop pressed onto a very dry spot after the cream has settled, never as a full-face layer. For most faces it is better to skip it, because sarson ka tel is heavy, has a strong smell and is known to irritate sensitive skin in some people.

In many North Indian homes, a winter massage with warm mustard oil before a bath is a habit from childhood, and some people rub the same oil on the face once the cold, dry air arrives. On the body it is a familiar comfort step. On the face, especially with an active cream, it needs more care. Here is how to think about it.

2. If you want to use both: the right order

  1. Patch test the oil first on the side of the jaw for 24 to 48 hours. Any stinging, redness or itching means it is not for your face.
  2. Wash with a gentle face wash and pat dry.
  3. Apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream on the face and neck and let it settle for a few minutes.
  4. Warm one drop of oil between your fingertips and press it only on a dry patch, such as the outer cheeks or around the mouth. Keep it away from the eyes.
  5. Do not use it in the morning. Oil under sunscreen makes the face greasy and the sunscreen slide.
  6. Every morning: sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, as the last step. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

Never put the oil first. A layer of oil underneath stops the cream from spreading and reaching the skin evenly. For gentler oils people use the same way, see glutathione cream with almond oil and glutathione cream with coconut oil.

3. Why mustard oil needs more care on the face

  • It can irritate. Mustard oil is naturally pungent, which is part of why it feels warming. Some people get stinging or redness, especially on thin facial skin.
  • It is heavy. On oily or acne-prone skin, a rich oil left on all night often leads to bumps.
  • The smell lingers. On the pillow, hair and dupatta.
  • It does not help the cream work. The cream works as it is made; oil only adds softness on dry spots.

5. A better place for sarson ka tel: the body

If you love the winter oil ritual, keep it where it works well: a warm-oil massage on the arms, legs and feet before a bath, then a lukewarm (not very hot) bath. Pat dry and use a plain body moisturiser on very dry shins and elbows. Use your face cream separately on the face. For the face itself, a plain moisturiser after the glutathione cream is usually kinder than any kitchen oil; see glutathione cream for dry skin.

6. Mistakes to avoid

  • Mixing the oil into the jar. It makes the cream greasy and can spoil a small 10 g jar.
  • Heating the oil until very hot. Lukewarm is enough; hot oil can burn skin.
  • Using it with haldi or besan as a "tan pack" and leaving it on. Rinse any pack off. See glutathione cream with turmeric.
  • Sitting in the sun with oil on the face. Oil gives no sun protection.
  • Expecting tan to fade faster. Tan and dark spots fade slowly. See how long glutathione cream takes to work.

FAQ

Should I apply mustard oil before or after glutathione cream?

After, and only one drop on a dry spot. Oil underneath stops the cream from spreading evenly.

Can I mix mustard oil into glutathione cream?

No. It makes the cream greasy and can spoil the jar. Keep them as separate steps.

Is mustard oil good for the face in winter?

For most people it is better on the body. On the face it can irritate some skin and clog pores, so patch test first or choose a plain moisturiser.

Can I leave mustard oil on my face overnight?

Only a single drop on a very dry patch, and only if it passed a patch test. Skip it if you are oily or acne-prone.

Does mustard oil with glutathione cream make skin fair?

No. Neither changes your natural skin colour. Oil only softens dry skin; used with sunscreen, the cream can help skin look brighter and tan look lighter over weeks.
